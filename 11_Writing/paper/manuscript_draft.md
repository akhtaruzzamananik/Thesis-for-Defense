## Abstract (`WRIT0009`)

**Background & Problem:** Two-dimensional orthopantomograms (2D OPGs) are the primary full-arch radiographic screening modality in dentistry, yet published deep-learning object-detection benchmarks suffer from widespread methodological artifacts. Across `122` screened publications (`42` full-text Core studies), `84.9%` (`62/73`) of dental YOLO studies omit strict multi-threshold localization (`mAP@0.50:0.95`), `87.7%` (`64/73`) lack external validation, `73.8%` (`31/42`) omit per-image inference latency, and pre-split augmentation leakage inflates reported `mAP@0.50` by up to `+30.4%`.  
**Methods:** We construct a leakage-controlled, six-cohort 2D OPG benchmark comprising `7,457` canonical deduplicated radiographs (`41,103` bounding boxes across six countries: Romania, Türkiye, Bangladesh, Vietnam, the United Arab Emirates, and Iran), maintained as six license-isolated, granularity-consistent tracks (`TrackA–TrackF`, `CLS0001–CLS0041`) under connected-component duplicate-cluster `70/15/15` splits (`281` redundant copies removed, `0` cross-partition leaks) and invertible coordinate-preserving preprocessing (`TransformLedger` round-trip error `<= 0.0041 px`). Thirteen controlled Kaggle `Tesla T4` experiments (`EXP0001–EXP0013`, `YOLO11s/v8s/11m`, locked seed `20261124`) were executed and frozen strictly on validation (`FREEZE0001`, `0` prior test exposure) before single-pass evaluation on `1,260` held-out radiographs (`1,082` internal `test` + `178` external-hospital `external_test`) with `1,000`-resample `GroupID` cluster bootstrap `95%` confidence intervals.  
**Results:** (1) Frozen `YOLO11s` achieved held-out `mAP@0.50` (`mAP@0.50:0.95`) of **`0.8246`** (`0.5163`) on `TrackE` (UAE `6`-class), **`0.4868`** (`0.2207`) on `TrackD` (Hanoi `PAI 3–5` `@1280px`), **`0.4397`** (`0.2673`) on `TrackB` (DENTEX `4`-class), **`0.4165`** (`0.1741`; external hospital **`0.4338`** / **`0.2038`**) on `TrackA` (Romania `13`-class `@1280px`), **`0.2508`** (`5`-class pathology **`0.2631`**) on `TrackC` (Dhaka handheld film `6`-class), and **`0.0537`** on `TrackF` (pediatric small-N mixed dentition) (`CLAIM0013`). (2) Per-class evaluation confirmed a sharp radiographic contrast hierarchy (`0.76–0.998` `AP@0.50` on metallic implants/crowns/impactions vs `0.043–0.330` on focal caries, apical lesions, and diffuse bone loss; `CLAIM0014`). Across `12` zero-shot cross-country bridges spanning five countries (`HC01–HC05`), high-contrast `HC03 Impacted Tooth` generalized strongly (`AP@0.50 = 0.8181` Romania->Türkiye, `0.7109` Romania->Bangladesh, `0.6967` UAE->Türkiye), whereas low-contrast `HC02 Periapical` (`0.1779–0.2355`) and `HC01 Caries` (`0.1627–0.1635`) collapsed (`CLAIM0015`). (3) Controlled `RQ2` ablations refuted blanket `CLAHE` enhancement (`3/3` validation degradations: `-0.018` to `-0.037`) while proving consistent held-out gains from `1280px` spatial resolution (`+0.0449` `mAP@0.50` and `+20.2%` relative `mAP@0.50:0.95` with non-overlapping `95% CIs` on `TrackD`; `+0.0350` internal / `+0.0622` external `mAP@0.50` on `TrackA`; `CLAIM0016`). (4) Under serial `batch = 1` `FP32` timing, pure GPU `FPS_infer` (`20.20–70.39`) overstated end-to-end `FPS_e2e` (`6.35–38.20`) by `1.8×–5.6×`, while static `ONNXRuntime` deployment on an `Intel Core i5-1135G7` laptop CPU achieved `0.9829–0.9965` PyTorch box parity IoU in **`201.6 ms/OPG`** (`4.96 FPS_e2e`) at `640px` and **`847.9–878.1 ms/OPG`** (`1.14–1.18 FPS_e2e`) at `1280px` (`CLAIM0017`).  
**Conclusion:** Exhaustive `6,718`-box `T19` error analysis and a hash-verified, clean-room reproducible (`R1–R3 PASS`) non-clinical prototype (`OPGPrototypeEngine`) establish rigorous baseline boundaries for 2D OPG object detection.

**Keywords:** Orthopantomogram (OPG), Panoramic Dental Radiography, YOLO11, Leakage-Controlled Benchmark, Cross-Country Domain Shift, CLAHE Ablation, ONNXRuntime.

## 1. Introduction (`WRIT0009`)

Orthopantomography (2D OPG) captures the complete maxillomandibular complex and dentition in a single low-dose projection [Lutescu2026_P0028; Mureanu2024_P0023]. Automated spatial localization of dental restorations and pathologies on 2D OPGs using single-stage You Only Look Once (YOLO) detectors has expanded rapidly [BaseriSaadi2026_P0001; Eftimie2025_P0015; Hartman2026_P0027], refuting naive claims that multi-class OPG object detectors do not exist (`CLAIM0001`). However, our systematic audit of `122` screened records (`42` full-text Core studies) reveals five pervasive methodological failures: (i) severe radiographic contrast disparity between metallic hardware (`0.83–0.98` `mAP@0.50`) and subtle radiolucencies (`0.07–0.40`; `CLAIM0002`) [Mureanu2024_P0023; Peng2026_P0009; Dehghani2025_P0014]; (ii) task conflation between whole-image/patch classifiers and spatial detectors (`CLAIM0003`) [Bhoopalan2026_P0002; Rahimi2026_P0010; Gwak2024_P0038] alongside `84.9%` (`62/73`) omission of `mAP@0.50:0.95` and `87.7%` (`64/73`) lack of external validation (`CLAIM0004`) [Hartman2026_P0027; Peker2025_P0019]; (iii) pre-split data augmentation and duplicate leakage inflating identical datasets by `+30.4%` `mAP@0.50` (`CLAIM0010`) [Scarano2026_P0011; Oztekin2026_P0008; Khurshid2026_P0006; Singh2025_P0021; RashidiRanjbar2023_P0025]; (iv) unablated use of Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization (`CLAHE`) despite contradictory controlled evidence (`CLAIM0008–CLAIM0009`) [CH2026_P0004; Nayak2026_P0029; Rahman2024_P0024]; and (v) omission of per-image latency in `73.8%` (`31/42`) of Core studies or conflation of bare GPU forward pass time with end-to-end throughput (`CLAIM0011–CLAIM0012`).

To resolve these gaps without overclaiming clinical diagnostic utility or cross-modality equivalence to 3D CBCT (`CLAIM0005–CLAIM0007`) [Kazimierczak2024_P0039; vanNistelrooij2026_P0032; Kk2025_P0018; BonfantiGris2024_P0022], this paper addresses four pre-registered research questions (`RQ0–RQ3`, `G03 PASS`):
- **`RQ0`:** What held-out detection accuracy (`mAP@0.50`, `mAP@0.50:0.95`, `GroupID` `95% CI`) do single-stage YOLO detectors achieve across six deduplicated, license-isolated public 2D OPG cohorts?
- **`RQ1`:** How does localization vary across radiographic contrast tiers, YOLO architectures (`YOLO11s/v8s/11m`), unseen same-country external hospitals, and `12` zero-shot five-country domain-shift bridges (`HC01–HC05`)?
- **`RQ2`:** Under identical grouped splits and seeds, how do parameterized `CLAHE` (`PP0002–PP0003`) and spatial resolution doubling (`PP0004` `1280px` vs `PP0001` `640px`) affect detection accuracy?
- **`RQ3`:** What bounded single-image (`BS = 1`, `FP32`) GPU (`Tesla T4`) and consumer CPU (`ONNXRuntime`) end-to-end latencies (`T_pre + T_infer + T_post`), systematic `T19` error modes, and clean-room reproducibility (`R1–R3`) are achieved?

## 2. Related Work and Methodological Gap Audit (`WRIT0010`)

Two independent searches harvested `180` candidate papers; after removing `58` duplicates, `122` unique studies (`P0001–P0122` in `02_Literature/references.bib`) were screened, yielding **`42` Core full-text studies** (`23` Group A direct 2D OPG box detectors and `19` Group B methodological/contextual studies), `55` Reserve studies (`27` evaluating YOLO), and `25` exclusions (`TAB0001`).

1. **Multi-Class Contrast Hierarchy & Representation (`GAP0001`, `GAP0003`, `CLAIM0001–0002`, `CLAIM0005`):** Thirteen of `23` Group A studies already evaluate multi-class (`4–13` classes) 2D OPG detectors [BaseriSaadi2026_P0001; Mureanu2024_P0023; Eftimie2025_P0015; Peng2026_P0009; BonfantiGris2024_P0022; Rahman2024_P0024; Dehghani2025_P0014; evik2025_P0016; Biengil2026_P0003; Khurshid2026_P0005]. However, across UMFIH (`13` classes) [Mureanu2024_P0023; Eftimie2025_P0015], RCTE (`11` classes) [Peng2026_P0009], DENTEX (`4` classes) [Dehghani2025_P0014], and AU-OPG (`6` classes) [AlabdAljabar2026_P0026], metallic/structural targets achieve `0.83–0.98` `AP@0.50` while focal caries (`0.25–0.40`), periapical radiolucencies (`0.07–0.32`), and diffuse bone resorption (`0.15–0.17`) collapse. While axis-aligned bounding boxes (`AABBs`) outperform semantic segmentation on discrete multi-instance screening over identical OPGs [Kk2025_P0018; BonfantiGris2024_P0022], AABBs struggle with diffuse periodontal contours and root-length keypoint tasks [zden2026_P0013].
2. **Task Conflation, Metric Omission & Leakage (`GAP0002`, `GAP0005`, `CLAIM0003–0004`, `CLAIM0010`):** Several papers framed as OPG detection actually evaluate whole-image classifiers [Bhoopalan2026_P0002], sliding patches (`P = 0.318` in [Rahimi2026_P0010]), Grad-CAM heatmaps without test IoU [Gwak2024_P0038], or closed-set confusion matrices omitting background false positives [Singh2025_P0021], while tight tooth crops truncate periapical lesions (`Sens = 0.538` vs `0.854` in [vanNistelrooij2026_P0032]) and post-crop random splits leak patient teeth across train and test [PornprasertsukDamrongsri2025_P0036]. In [Hartman2026_P0027], `84.9%` of YOLO dental studies omit `mAP@0.50:0.95`, masking severe localization decay (e.g., `mAP@0.50 = 0.995` vs `mAP@0.50:0.95 = 0.0995` on Tooth 71 in [Peker2025_P0019]). Most critically, pre-split offline augmentation in [Scarano2026_P0011] (`17,004` pre-augmented copies of Do 2024) produced `0.9194` `mAP@0.50` versus `0.494–0.615` on the exact same cohort after deduplication in [Oztekin2026_P0008].
3. **Modality Ceiling, Domain Shift, Preprocessing & Latency (`GAP0004–0006`, `CLAIM0006–0009`, `CLAIM0011–0012`):** Paired OPG-vs-CBCT evaluation demonstrates a physical 2D sensitivity ceiling (`33.33%` on OPG vs `77.78%` on 3D CBCT for periapical lesions [Kazimierczak2024_P0039]), prohibiting cross-modality leaderboard comparisons. Within 2D OPG, external scanner shifts degrade `mAP@0.50` by `-9.0%` to `-22.2%` [Eftimie2025_P0015; Mureanu2024_P0023; evik2025_P0016; Nayak2026_P0029; NISHIMURA2026_P0030]. Moreover, while `CLAHE` improved a single cropped canine detector (`0.774 -> 0.948` [CH2026_P0004]), controlled multi-class and segmentation studies report `-1.7%` to `-3.91%` degradation from `CLAHE`/enhancement [Scarano2026_P0011; Nayak2026_P0029; Gwak2024_P0038], whereas `1280px` resolution and native tiling consistently preserve small lesions [Oztekin2026_P0008; Eftimie2025_P0015]. Finally, `73.8%` (`31/42`) of Core studies omit per-image inference latency (`ms/OPG` or `FPS`), leaving bounded end-to-end GPU and CPU timing open.

## 3. Materials and Methods (`WRIT0011`)

### 3.1 Six-Cohort Dataset Portfolio, Ontology & Connected-Component Splitting (`G04–G07`)
Figure `FIG0001` illustrates our stage-gated governance pipeline (`G01–G14`). We curated six de-identified public 2D OPG cohorts (`DS0001–DS0006`) across six countries (`Romania`, `Türkiye`, `Bangladesh`, `Vietnam`, `United Arab Emirates`, and `Iran`), quarantining `17,566` pre-augmented or unlabeled files at intake (`0` cross-cohort overlaps). To respect incompatible label taxonomies, box granularities (focal lesion AABBs vs whole-tooth AABBs), selective healthy-tooth labeling in `DS0003` (`~2.8` healthy teeth boxed per OPG), and the non-commercial `CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0` license of `DS0002` (DENTEX), the six cohorts are maintained as **six non-merged tracks (`TrackA–TrackF`, Table `TAB0002`)** governed by a frozen `41`-entry ontology (`04_Annotations/classes.csv`, `CLS0001–CLS0041`, `sha256: c3f91dfc...`; `G05–G06 PASS`, round-trip coordinate conversion error `< 0.002 px`, `0` invalid boxes). Two unsupported source classes (`DS0001 Root_Resorption` with `N = 5` train / `0` eval boxes; `DS0006 Smile_Zone` framing ROI) were excluded prior to splitting, and five harmonized cross-cohort concepts (`HC01` Caries, `HC02` Periapical Lesion, `HC03` Impacted Tooth, `HC04` Residual Root/BDC, `HC05` Dental Implant) were registered strictly for zero-shot evaluation.

Forensic audit of official public splits exposed a **`27.5%` validation duplicate leak** in `DS0001` and a **`4.4%` validation leak** in `DS0005`. We discarded all contaminated internal splits (retaining only `DS0001`'s independent `178`-OPG external hospital cohort as `external_test`), formed connected-component duplicate/re-shoot clusters (`GroupID`) via `SHA-256`, perceptual hashing, and interior pixel correlation (`281` redundant copies removed), and generated deterministic Sechidis multi-label group-stratified **`70/15/15`** splits (`SPLIT0001–SPLIT0006`: **`7,457` canonical OPGs** [`5,106 train / 1,091 val / 1,082 test / 178 external_test`], **`41,103` boxes**, **`0` cross-partition cluster leaks**; `G07 PASS`). Sealed `test` and `external_test` partitions remained programmatically locked (`_is_g11_passed()`) until `G11 PASS`.

### 3.2 Invertible Preprocessing (`PP0001–PP0004`), `13`-Experiment Matrix & Pre-Test Freeze (`G08–G11`)
All transformations in `src/opg/preprocessing.py` (`sha256: 011e67c0...`) emit an invertible affine `TransformLedger` (`max round-trip error = 0.003630 px @ 640`, `0.004068 px @ 1280`, `0` dropped boxes across `41,103` annotations; `G08 PASS`): **`PP0001`** (`Raw 640×640` letterbox, RGB standardization, EXIF strip, and blind black-border autocrop on `DS0006`), **`PP0002`** (`PP0001 + luminance CLAHE 2.0, 8×8`), **`PP0003`** (`PP0001 + luminance CLAHE 3.0, 16×16`), and **`PP0004`** (`High-Resolution Raw 1280×1280`).

On Kaggle `NVIDIA Tesla T4` GPUs (`torch 2.11.0+cu130`, `ultralytics==8.3.253`, verified via `G09` interrupt/resume smoke test `EXP0000_SMOKE`), we executed `13` controlled experiments (`EXP0001–EXP0013`, Table `TAB0003`) comparing `YOLO11s` (`9.4M` params), `YOLOv8s` (`11.2M` params), and `YOLO11m` (`20.1M` params) under identical locked hyperparameters: `AdamW` (`lr0 = 0.001`, `lrf = 0.01`, `weight_decay = 0.0005`), `epochs = 100`, `patience = 20` on `val mAP@0.50`, `seed = 20261124`, `deterministic = true`, `batch = 16 @ 640` (`8 @ 1280`), class-aware `NMS IoU = 0.65`, `max_det = 300`, and online augmentations with **`fliplr = 0.0` / `flipud = 0.0` disabled** to preserve dental quadrant laterality (`13/13` `G10 PASS`).

Strictly prior to unlocking `test`/`external_test` (`FREEZE0001`, `2026-11-21T01:40:00+06:00`, `G11 PASS`), validation selection (`val mAP@0.50`, tie-breaker `val mAP@0.50:0.95`) froze `6` track winners (`MODEL0005`, `MODEL0004`, `MODEL0006`, `MODEL0013`, `MODEL0008`, `MODEL0007`) and `4` `RQ1`/`RQ3` comparators (`MODEL0001`, `MODEL0002`, `MODEL0003`, `MODEL0010`), excluded `3/3` degrading `CLAHE` models (`MODEL0009`, `MODEL0011`, `MODEL0012`), and locked each model's operating threshold `conf_op` at its validation `BoxF1_curve` macro-`F1` peak. Held-out metrics were computed once in original source pixel coordinates via frozen `src/opg/evaluator.py` (`sha256: 8fa2c6d8...`, `metric_conf_floor = 0.001`, continuous all-point AP, `1,000`-resample `GroupID` cluster bootstrap `95% CI` with `seed = 20261131`).

### 4.2 Controlled `RQ2` Ablation: `CLAHE` Failure vs `1280px` Resolution Gain (`CLAIM0016`)
Figure `FIG0003` and Table `TAB0005` report our controlled `RQ2` ablations under locked splits and seed `20261124`. Luminance `CLAHE` degraded validation `mAP@0.50` in **`3 / 3` controlled trials**: **`-0.037`** (`EXP0008` `PP0002` `0.344` vs `EXP0001` `0.381` on `TrackA`), **`-0.018`** (`EXP0009` `PP0003` `0.363` vs `0.381` on `TrackA`), and **`-0.027`** (`EXP0012` `PP0002` `0.232` vs `EXP0004` `0.259` on handheld-photographed `TrackC`), refuting blanket `CLAHE` prescriptions. Conversely, doubling spatial resolution to `1280×1280` (`PP0004`) improved held-out detection on **both** tested cohorts: **`+0.0449` `mAP@0.50`** (`0.4868` vs `0.4419`) and **`+0.0371` (`+20.2%` relative) `mAP@0.50:0.95`** on `TrackD` Hanoi with **strictly non-overlapping `95% CIs`** (`[0.2025, 0.2495]` vs `[0.1659, 0.2085]`), and **`+0.0350` internal / `+0.0622` external `mAP@0.50`** (`+22.5%` relative external `mAP@0.50:0.95`) on `TrackA`.

### 4.3 Zero-Shot Five-Country Cross-Cohort Shift (`HC01–HC05`, `RQ1`, `CLAIM0015`)
Across `12` zero-shot cross-country bridges (`20` concept-bridge rows in Table `TAB0006` and Figure `FIG0004`, using frozen source weights and source `conf_op` across `Romania, Türkiye, Bangladesh, Vietnam, UAE`), high-contrast **`HC03 Impacted Tooth`** generalized strongly without target-domain training: **`AP@0.50 = 0.8181`** (`XSHIFT04_A` Romania->Türkiye), **`0.7109`** (`XSHIFT03_A` Romania->Bangladesh, exceeding `TrackC`'s own small-sample `0.6601`), **`0.6967`** (`XSHIFT04_E` UAE->Türkiye), **`0.6116`** (`XSHIFT01` Romania->UAE), **`0.4935`** (`XSHIFT02_EXT` UAE->Romania Ext), **`0.4469`** (`XSHIFT03_E` UAE->Bangladesh), and **`0.3814`** (`XSHIFT02_INT` UAE->Romania Int). Medium-contrast **`HC04 Residual Root`** achieved **`0.5055`** (UAE->Bangladesh), **`0.3816`** (Romania->UAE), **`0.3152`** / **`0.2420`** (UAE->Romania Ext/Int), and **`0.0844`** (Romania->Bangladesh), while **`HC05 Dental Implant`** reached **`0.4626`** (UAE->Romania Ext) and **`0.3189`** (Romania->UAE) but dropped to **`0.0723`** on Romanian internal OPGs due to whole-tooth vs fixture-only box-extent differences. By contrast, low-contrast radiolucencies suffered severe cross-country collapse: **`HC02 Periapical Lesion`** dropped to **`0.2355`** (`XSHIFT05_A2D` Romania->Vietnam), **`0.2163`** (`XSHIFT05_D2A_EXT` Vietnam->Romania Ext), and **`0.1779`** (`XSHIFT05_D2A_INT` Vietnam->Romania Int), while **`HC01 Whole-Tooth Caries`** collapsed to **`0.1635`** (`XSHIFT06_E2C` UAE->Bangladesh) and **`0.1627`** (`XSHIFT06_E2B` UAE->Türkiye).

### 4.4 Bounded GPU vs CPU Latency & `RQ3` Pareto Tradeoffs (`CLAIM0017`)
Under serial single-image streaming (`BS = 1`, `FP32`, `10` warm-up + `100` timed test OPGs, `0` errors; Table `TAB0005`, Figure `FIG0005`), pure `Tesla T4` GPU model+NMS `FPS_infer` (`20.20–70.39 FPS`) exceeded true end-to-end `FPS_e2e` (`T_pre + T_infer + T_post`: `6.35–38.20 FPS`) by **`1.8×–5.6×`** due to full-resolution decode, letterbox, and autocrop overhead. On `TrackA`, `YOLO11s@1280` (`MODEL0005`, `36.6 MB` ONNX, `9.24` GPU / `1.14` CPU `FPS_e2e`) outperformed `YOLO11m@640` (`MODEL0003`, `76.7 MB` ONNX, `13.14` GPU / `1.95` CPU `FPS_e2e`) on internal `mAP@0.50` (`0.4165` vs `0.3990`) and external `mAP@0.50:0.95` (`0.2038` vs `0.1940`) at half the memory footprint. All `7` exported static `ONNX` (`opset = 17`) models deployed on an `Intel Core i5-1135G7` laptop CPU (`ONNXRuntime 1.32.0`) matched PyTorch GPU boxes (`0.9829–0.9965` mean IoU) within sub-second latency: **`201.6 ms/OPG`** (`4.96 FPS_e2e`) for `MODEL0008` (`TrackE@640`), **`181.9 ms/OPG`** (`5.50 FPS_e2e`) for `MODEL0010` (`TrackD@640`), **`263.4 ms/OPG`** (`3.80 FPS_e2e`) for `MODEL0001` (`TrackA@640`), and **`847.9–878.1 ms/OPG`** (`1.18` and `1.14 FPS_e2e`) for `1280px` winners `MODEL0013` and `MODEL0005`.

## 5. Error Analysis, Limitations, Reproducibility & Conclusion (`WRIT0013`)

### 5.1 Exhaustive `T19` Error Taxonomy, Visual Audit & Clean-Run Reproducibility (`G13–G14`)
Read-only decomposition of all `6,718` held-out ground-truth boxes across the `7` primary winner evaluations (`ERRREV0001`, Figure `FIG0006`, `G13 PASS`) exposed four dominant failure mechanisms:
1. **Small-Area Recall Bottleneck (`2.6×` Drop):** On `TrackA` (`EVAL0001`, `1,077` total FPs / `1,420` FNs), recall dropped monotonically from **`80.3%`** (`Large >= 1.0%` OPG area) and **`66.4%`** (`Medium 0.2–1.0%`) to **`30.9%`** (`Small < 0.2%`, `428/1,385`), with an identical profile on `external_test` (`80.1% -> 65.2% -> 30.9%`). On `TrackF`, `100%` (`137/137`) of pediatric caries boxes occupied `< 0.2%` area (`12.4%` recall), whereas `1280px` scaling on `TrackD` preserved **`61.1%` recall on `756` small apical lesions** (`only 68/999 = 6.8%` complete misses).
2. **Ordinal Boundary Swaps:** Adjacent `PAI 3 ↔ 4 ↔ 5` swaps accounted for **`86.7%` (`189/218`)** of `TrackD` classification false positives (`FP_Cls = 218`), and `Deep_Caries ↔ Caries` accounted for **`65.9%` (`56/85`)** of `TrackB` `FP_Cls`.
3. **Source-Label Incompleteness vs Detector Hallucination:** Direct visual inspection of `28` deterministically sampled cases (`4` per primary evaluation) proved that in `EVAL0001`'s worst `FP_Bkg` radiograph (`DS0001_IMG_000722`, `GT = 6, FP_Bkg = 27`), the source reference missed `>15` visibly obvious radiopaque root canal fillings and prosthetic bridges correctly localized by `MODEL0005`. Similarly, `69.4%` (`127/183`) of `TrackC` FPs resulted from unannotated healthy teeth across the arch.
4. **2D OPG Superimposition Artifacts:** Midline cervical-spine ghost shadows washed out anterior mandibular caries (`DS0005_IMG_000367`) and mimicked metallic wire edges (`DS0001_IMG_000509`), while mental-ridge radiolucencies and class-aware NMS co-firing triggered nested `PAI_3`/`PAI_5` alerts (`DS0004_IMG_015992`).

Our packaged `OPGPrototypeEngine` (`src/opg/inference_prototype.py`) reuses frozen `preprocessing.py` verbatim, enforces startup `SHA-256` verification, isolates `CC-BY-NC-SA` `MODEL0004`, and passed our `REPRO0001` audit (`G14 PASS`): **`R1`** (`50/50` SHA-256 checksums exact), **`R2`** (`ΔmAP = 0.0` deterministic metric replay), and **`R3`** (`0.0` ONNX CPU self-diff, `0.9938–0.9946` PyTorch-ONNX IoU parity across `40` raw test OPGs, and clean `git archive` checkout verification), with full `R4` GPU training recipes archived (`12_Documentation/reproduce.md`).

### 5.2 Study Limitations & Non-Clinical Boundary
This work is bounded by ten explicit limitations (`09_Evaluation/limitations.md`): (1) 2D OPG superimposition cannot replace 3D CBCT or intraoral bitewings; (2) public cohorts omit cryptographic patient IDs (deduplication guarantees `0` near-duplicate leaks across `281` removed copies, not longitudinal patient linkage); (3) sparse source labels (`DS0001_IMG_000722`, `DS0003` selective healthy teeth) and ordinal subjectivity cap upper metrics; (4) focal-lesion and whole-tooth tracks are non-comparable numerically; (5) small-N (`TrackF` `N = 121` train) and ultra-rare classes (`GT <= 10`) require larger cohorts; (6) radiolucent pathologies (`HC01–HC02`) require local domain adaptation before cross-clinic transfer; (7) single-seed (`20261124`) training under free GPU quota relies on `1,000`-resample `GroupID` bootstrap CIs; (8) scope is restricted to single-stage YOLO `AABB` detectors; (9) single-laptop CPU latency varies with OS load (`IQR`/`p95` reported); and (10) **no model or prototype is clinically validated or regulatory-cleared—zero predicted boxes never exclude dental disease**.

### 5.3 Conclusion
Under strict leakage, coordinate, and pre-test freeze governance across `7,457` 2D OPGs from six countries, single-stage `YOLO11s` delivers strong localization on high-contrast dental structures (`0.73–0.998` `AP@0.50`, zero-shot cross-country `HC03` up to `0.8181`) and sub-second laptop CPU `ONNXRuntime` inference (`201.6–878.1 ms/OPG`), while spatial resolution (`1280px`, `+20.2%` rel. `mAP@0.50:0.95`) reliably outperforms `CLAHE` (`3/3` failures) and parameter doubling. Future work should integrate overlapping high-resolution tiling for `< 0.2%`-area caries, ordinal focal losses with inter-grade NMS for `PAI 3–5`, domain adaptation for radiolucencies, and prospective CBCT-adjudicated clinical reader studies.
