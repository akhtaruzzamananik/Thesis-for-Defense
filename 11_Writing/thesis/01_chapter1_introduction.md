# Chapter 1: Introduction (`WRIT0002`)

## 1.1 Background and Clinical-Technical Context

Orthopantomography (OPG)—commonly referred to as two-dimensional (2D) panoramic dental radiography—provides a single wide-field radiographic projection capturing the entire maxilla, mandible, dentition, temporomandibular joints, and surrounding alveolar bone at comparatively low radiation dose and acquisition cost [Lutescu2026_P0028; Mureanu2024_P0023]. Because a single panoramic radiograph can simultaneously exhibit anatomical variations, restorative hardware (implants, crowns, intracoronal fillings, endodontic root canal fillings, surgical plates, orthodontic appliances), and radiographic abnormalities (impacted teeth, residual root fragments, carious radiolucencies, periapical periodontitis, furcation defects, and alveolar bone resorption), automated localization of multiple dental findings has attracted substantial interest in medical computer vision [BaseriSaadi2026_P0001; Eftimie2025_P0015; Hartman2026_P0027].

Among deep-learning paradigms, single-stage object detectors from the You Only Look Once (YOLO) family have emerged as the dominant architecture for 2D OPG screening due to their unified spatial bounding-box regression, multi-scale feature pyramids, and favorable compute efficiency relative to heavier two-stage detectors and pixel-wise segmentation cascades [Hartman2026_P0027; BonfantiGris2024_P0022; Kk2025_P0018]. Unlike whole-image classifiers—which cannot identify *where* or *how many* pathologies appear across a 32-tooth arch [Bhoopalan2026_P0002; Gwak2024_P0038]—bounding-box detectors output explicit class-labeled coordinates that directly map onto the full radiograph.

## 1.2 Problem Statement and Verified Literature Gaps

Although an expanding body of literature applies YOLO models to panoramic radiographs—refuting earlier claims that multi-class OPG detection does not exist [CLAIM0001; Mureanu2024_P0023; Eftimie2025_P0015; Peng2026_P0009]—our systematic audit of `122` screened studies (`42` verified full-text Core papers; Chapter 2) exposes five severe methodological weaknesses that undermine reliability and comparability across published benchmarks:

1. **Stark Radiographic Contrast Disparity (`GAP0001` narrowed / `CLAIM0002`):** Multi-class detectors frequently report `0.83–0.98` `mAP@0.50` on large, high-contrast metallic or structural objects (implants, crowns, impacted teeth) while collapsing to `0.07–0.40` `AP@0.50` on small low-contrast radiolucent lesions (caries, periapical periodontitis), diffuse alveolar bone loss, and rare classes [Mureanu2024_P0023; Peng2026_P0009; Dehghani2025_P0014].
2. **Incomplete Localization Metrics and Data Leakage (`GAP0002`, `GAP0005` / `CLAIM0004`, `CLAIM0010`):** A PRISMA 2020 review of `73` dental YOLO studies demonstrated that `84.9%` (`62/73`) omit strict multi-threshold localization (`mAP@0.50:0.95`) and `87.7%` (`64/73`) perform no external validation [Hartman2026_P0027]. Omitting `mAP@0.50:0.95` conceals severe box-alignment failures (e.g., `mAP@0.50 = 0.995` vs. `mAP@0.50:0.95 = 0.0995` in [Peker2025_P0019]). Worse, pre-split data augmentation and un-deduplicated public splits severely inflate reported metrics—exemplified by a `+30.4%` `mAP@0.50` inflation on the identical Do (2024) periapical dataset between pre-augmented training (`0.9194`) [Scarano2026_P0011] and deduplicated OPG-level splitting (`0.494–0.615`) [Oztekin2026_P0008].
3. **Unresolved Preprocessing Paradox (`GAP0005` / `CLAIM0008–CLAIM0009`):** Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization (`CLAHE`) is routinely applied without controlled ablation [Rahman2024_P0024; zden2026_P0013], despite contradictory evidence where `CLAHE` improved one cropped canine detector [CH2026_P0004] yet *degraded* localization in controlled multi-class OPG studies [Scarano2026_P0011; Nayak2026_P0029; Gwak2024_P0038], whereas preserving native spatial resolution (`1280px` or tiling) more reliably aids small-lesion recall [Oztekin2026_P0008; Eftimie2025_P0015].
4. **Cross-Hospital and Cross-Country Domain Fragility (`GAP0004` / `CLAIM0006–CLAIM0007`):** Beyond the hard physical visibility ceiling of 2D OPG compared to 3D CBCT (`33.3%` vs. `77.8%` sensitivity in paired validation [Kazimierczak2024_P0039]) and intraoral bitewings [vanNistelrooij2026_P0032], within-OPG external scanner shifts consistently reduce `mAP@0.50` by `-9%` to `-22.2%` [Eftimie2025_P0015; Mureanu2024_P0023; evik2025_P0016], yet cross-country transfer across distinct clinical cohorts remains largely unquantified.
5. **Unverified Speed Claims (`GAP0006` / `CLAIM0011–CLAIM0012`):** `73.8%` (`31/42`) of Core OPG studies omit per-image inference latency entirely—even when asserting `"real-time"` capability—and studies reporting GPU forward speed omit end-to-end preprocessing and post-processing boundaries (`T_pre + T_infer + T_post`) or CPU deployment latency [Peng2026_P0009; Nayak2026_P0029].

## 1.3 Research Objectives (`O1–O3`) and Research Questions (`RQ0–RQ3`)

Grounded in our approved project scope (`scope.md v1.0.0`, `G01 PASS`) and systematic gap audit (`research_questions.md v1.0.0`, `G03 PASS`), this thesis addresses three operational objectives:

- **`O1` (Leak-Free Multi-Cohort YOLO Pipeline):** Design, deduplicate, group-split, and train reproducible YOLO bounding-box detectors for approved radiographically visible dental objects and abnormalities across independent public 2D OPG cohorts.
- **`O2` (Rigorous Held-Out Detection Evaluation & Controlled Ablations):** Evaluate validation-frozen models once on sealed held-out `test`, external-hospital, and cross-country OPG partitions using deterministic `Precision`, `Recall`, `F1`, `mAP@0.50`, `mAP@0.50:0.95`, `GroupID` cluster bootstrap `95%` confidence intervals, and controlled `CLAHE` vs. resolution ablations.
- **`O3` (Bounded End-to-End Speed, Failure Profiling & Research Prototype):** Benchmark single-image (`batch = 1`) GPU and CPU end-to-end latency (`T_pre + T_infer + T_post`, `FPS_e2e`), perform systematic error and label-uncertainty analysis, and deliver a parity-verified non-clinical research inference prototype.

These objectives are operationalized through four approved research questions:

- **`RQ0` (Primary):** How effectively and efficiently can an approved YOLO bounding-box detector localize predefined dental objects and radiographically visible abnormalities across available public 2D OPG datasets under a deduplicated, duplicate-cluster grouped leakage-controlled evaluation protocol?
- **`RQ1` (Localization, Contrast Tiers & Domain Shift):** Under a deduplicated grouped split, what overall, visibility-tier (high-contrast restorations/impactions vs. small radiolucent pathologies), per-class, external-hospital, and cross-country zero-shot bounding-box localization performance (`P`, `R`, `F1`, `mAP@0.50`, `mAP@0.50:0.95`) do frozen YOLO detectors achieve?
- **`RQ2` (Controlled Preprocessing & Resolution Factor):** Under identical grouped splits, class schemas, seeds, and deterministic evaluation rules, how do unenhanced raw OPG (`640px`), parameterized `CLAHE` (`2.0 / 8×8`, `3.0 / 16×16`), and high-resolution scaling (`1280px`) affect localization accuracy and small radiolucent pathology recall?
- **`RQ3` (Bounded Speed & Systematic Failure Modes):** Under an explicit single-image (`batch = 1`, post-warmup) GPU and CPU measurement boundary (`T_e2e = T_pre + T_infer + T_post`), what end-to-end speed (`ms/OPG`, `FPS_e2e`) and accuracy-vs-latency tradeoffs do compared detectors achieve, and what systematic radiographic failure modes and reference-label uncertainties account for errors?

## 1.4 Scope Boundaries and Thesis Organization

This study is strictly bounded to **2D OPG axis-aligned bounding-box (`AABB`) object detection** on de-identified secondary public cohorts. Three-dimensional CBCT modeling, pixel-wise segmentation replacement, whole-image classification substitutes, cross-modality numeric superiority claims, autonomous clinical diagnosis, and clinician workload-reduction claims are explicitly **out of scope**. Figure `FIG0001` illustrates the end-to-end governance and execution workflow (`G01–G14`).

The remainder of this thesis is organized as follows: **Chapter 2** synthesizes the systematic literature audit (`122` screened / `42` Core studies) and gap verdicts (`GAP0001–GAP0006`); **Chapter 3** details the six-cohort dataset portfolio (`DS0001–DS0006`), ontology (`CLS0001–CLS0041`), deduplicated grouping (`SPLIT0001–SPLIT0006`), reversible preprocessing (`PP0001–PP0004`), `13`-experiment YOLO matrix, and pre-test freeze (`FREEZE0001`); **Chapter 4** presents official held-out results (`EVAL0001–EVAL0014`), `95%` confidence intervals, `RQ2` ablations, `12` zero-shot five-country shift bridges (`HC01–HC05`), and `RQ3` GPU-vs-CPU timing; **Chapter 5** presents systematic `T19` error analysis, visual label-uncertainty review, study limitations, the packaged `OPGPrototypeEngine`, and clean-environment reproducibility (`R1–R3`); and **Chapter 6** concludes with bounded contributions and future work.
