## Rationale

`PP0004` is **Controlled Factor 3 for `RQ2` (`GAP0005` / `P0008, P0015`)**: doubling linear spatial resolution (`1280 × 1280` vs `640 × 640`, $4\times$ pixel area) without contrast filtering to test whether spatial scale preservation improves recall on tiny radiolucent lesions (`DS0004` `PAI 3–5`, `DS0001` focal caries & apical periodontitis, `DS0006` pediatric caries) and to measure its accuracy-vs-latency (`FPS`) tradeoff under `RQ3`.

## ordered transforms

Identical to `PP0001`, except aspect-preserving letterbox resize targets **`1280 × 1280`** (`pad_value = 114`).

## parameters

- `target_size = [1280, 1280]`, `preserve_aspect_ratio = true`, `pad_value = 114`, `contrast_enhancement.method = "none"`.

- Deterministic per-image operation (`0` validation/test statistics used).
