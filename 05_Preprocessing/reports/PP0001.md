## Rationale

`PP0001` establishes the **minimal justified, deterministic unenhanced baseline** (`OS §4.3`, `RQ1` & `RQ2` control) across all `6` approved `2D OPG` cohorts (`REL0001–REL0006`, `7,457` unique canonical OPGs, `41,103` canonical boxes). It solves the observed `STEP-013` structural defects (`RGBA`/`L` channel inconsistencies, `DS0003` EXIF headers, and `DS0006`'s `66.17%` black letterbox padding) while preserving native panoramic aspect ratios (`1.73–2.20`) and raw radiographic gray levels without applying unverified blurring or contrast filters.

---

## ordered transforms

1. **Metadata Stripping:** Strip all EXIF, XMP, and text chunks on export (`strip_exif_xmp = true`).
2. **Channel Standardization (`standardize_channels_rgb`):** Convert grayscale `L` (`1`-ch) or `RGBA` (`4`-ch, e.g., `DS0001` `v60.png` and `DS0005` `17` files) deterministically to `3`-channel grayscale-consistent `RGB uint8`.
3. **Box-Safe Black-Border Autocrop (`DS0006` only — `autocrop_black_border_with_boxes`):** Strip outer solid-black framing ($I \le 8$) with an `8 px` safety margin expanded to enclose $100\%$ of ground-truth boxes (`0` clipped or dropped boxes).
4. **Aspect-Preserving Letterbox Resize (`640 × 640`):** Scale isotropically by $s = \min(640/W_{\text{crop}}, 640/H_{\text{crop}})$ using area decimation (`cv2.INTER_AREA` when $s < 1$) and pad symmetrically to `640 × 640` with constant neutral gray (`pad_value = 114`).
5. **Intensity Scaling:** Divide pixel values by `255.0` to `[0.0, 1.0]` `float32`.
6. **Training-Only Online Augmentation (`Phase C`):** Minor rotation ($\pm 5^\circ$), scale ($[0.90, 1.10]$), translation ($\pm 5\%$), brightness/contrast ($\pm 10\%$) restricted strictly to `Partition == train` during training (`apply_to_val_or_test = false`).
