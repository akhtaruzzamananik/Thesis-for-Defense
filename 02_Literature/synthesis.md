## 3. Contextual & Methodological Evidence — Group B (`19` Studies: `P0002, P0010, P0026–P0042`)

### 3.1 Systematic Reviews & Narrative Syntheses (`P0027, P0028, P0034, P0037`)
- **`P0027` (`Hartman et al., 2026`):** `PRISMA 2020` (`PROSPERO CRD420251135014`) of `73` YOLO studies across `14` dental domains (`~31` OPG, `~15` intraoral photo, `~13` PA, `~8` BW, `~6` CBCT/CT/MRI). Proves **`84.9%` (`62/73`) omit `mAP@0.5:0.95`**, **`87.7%` (`64/73`) lack external validation**, and `QUADAS-2` Reference Standard bias (`κ`/consensus omission) is endemic. *Forensic flags:* uncritically praises leaked `P0021` (`F1=0.994`), mislabels `P0022` as intraoral & `P0016` external validation as `"No"`, with 6 ref swaps (`SUBJAREA "DENT"` missed IEEE/MethodsX/MTAP).
- **`P0034` (`Bonfanti-Gris et al., 2025`):** `PRISMA-DTA` + `HSROC` across `20` pre-Sept-2023 OPG studies (`0` `YOLOv7–v12`/`RT-DETR`, `5%` public data, `30%` High Risk / `50%` Unclear `QUADAS-2`, Egger's `p=0.025`). Documents `4/6` impacted-tooth OPG studies needed **`CBCT` GT**. *Forensic flags:* broken PRISMA arithmetic, copy-pasted sensitivity columns in `Fig. 4D/E`, stacks `Kuwada [30]` `8×`, and pools a classifier (`Aljabri [32]`) as mesiodens detection.
- **`P0028` (`Lutescu et al., 2026`):** Narrative synthesis (`61` refs, `21` in `Table 1`, no `PRISMA`/`QUADAS-2`) of clinical OPG translation and `FDA PCCP` / `EU MDR + AI Act` regulation. ### 4.2 Preprocessing Paradox: `CLAHE` & Super-Resolution vs. Native Tiling / Resolution
- **Positive controlled evidence (`P0004`):** `CLAHE(clip=2)` + sharpening boosted cropped `YOLOv5` on `500` private OPGs from `0.701/0.774` to `0.924/0.948 mAP@0.5`, whereas `Gaussian` blur dropped `ResNet50` accuracy `-16.6%` despite higher `PSNR`. `P0024` (`YOLOv7`) and `P0013` (`YOLOv12x-pose`) used `CLAHE` without ablating it against raw input; `P0042` showed visual `AME/LogAME` gains only.
- **Negative / Null controlled evidence (`P0011, P0029, P0038, P0041, P0018, P0001, P0008`):** `P0011` (`Table 4`) lost `-1.71% mAP50` and **`-3.91% mAP50-95`** under `Sharpening+CLAHE+Gaussian`; `P0029` (`Table 30`) proved **raw `Mask R-CNN` (`IoU=0.8173`) beat fixed `CLAHE` (`0.8104`), `Adaptive CLAHE` (`0.8134`), and full enhancement (`0.8151`)** at `1/7.9th` the runtime; `P0038` dropped from `97.50–99.17%` to `95.69%` with `CLAHE`; `P0018`'s face-prior `GFP-GAN` degraded `RT-DETR-L/X` (`-0.5% to -1.9%`); `P0001, P0008, P0041` achieved top performance explicitly **without** `CLAHE`.
- **What consistently improved small-lesion recall:** Native-resolution tiling (`P0008 +0.039 to +0.121 mAP50` with mandatory background tiles; `P0028` citing `Rohrer` `+294%` RCTs at `20` tiles) and higher input scale (`1280–1600 px` in `P0011, P0015, P0017`), whereas aggressive `75%` central cropping deleted `5.7%` of boxes (`P0015`) and `512–640 px` full-OPG squashing crushed `<32×32 px` caries and periapical lesions (`P0008, P0009, P0014`). **Design consequence:** In Phase 2 (`O2`), `Raw` vs parameterized `CLAHE (clip, grid)` and resolution/tiling will be tested head-to-head as **falsifiable ablation factors**, not assumed gains.

### 4.3 Speed & Efficiency Protocol Audit
Across all **`42` Core studies**, **`31 / 42` (`73.8%`) omit per-image inference latency (`ms/OPG` or `FPS`)** — comprising `14 / 23` (`60.9%`) Group A papers and `17 / 19` (`89.5%`) Group B papers — even though **`P0005, P0017, P0018, P0019, P0033, P0035`** assert `"real-time"` or speed advantages! Among the `11` studies reporting runtime:
- `P0006, P0009 (FPS_infer only), P0012, P0015, P0020` show modern compact YOLO models achieve `30.1–478.8 FPS` on GPU forward passes, **but** none concurrently prove bounded end-to-end (`T_pre + T_infer + T_post`, CPU + GPU) speed alongside leak-free high recall on small radiolucent lesions (`P0009 PA = 0.0726`; `P0015` `-21%` external drop; `P0006` leaked; `P0020` math errors).
- Meanwhile, accuracy-preserving dense tiling (`P0008`: `0.42 s/OPG = 2.38 OPG/s` on RTX 4090), two-stage ROI cascades (`P0011`: `0.1589 s/OPG = 6.29 FPS` on RTX 5000 Ada), heavy enhancement cascades (`P0029`: `3.37 s = 0.30 FPS` on T4), and cloud reporting (`P0001`: `15 ± 1.4 s/OPG`) drop well below `30 FPS`. **Zero Core studies** close the loop with a leak-free multi-class `2D OPG` benchmark reporting matched **`mAP@0.50` + `mAP@0.50:0.95` + per-class pathology breakdown + explicit CPU/GPU `T_pre + T_infer + T_post` E2E timing (`O3`)**.
