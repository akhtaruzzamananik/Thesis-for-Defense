## Verified citation

Ozcelik, E., Simsek, H., Aktas, A., Ilhan, H. O., & Yasa, Y. (2026). Teeth identification and numbering in mixed dentition: evaluating deep learning models for pediatric panoramic radiographs. *BMC Oral Health*, 26, 727. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12903-026-08097-w (PDF p. 1).

## question/task/modality

- **Modality:** 2D pediatric panoramic radiographs (`Planmeca ProMax 2D`, `60–70 kVp, 8–10 mA, 13–16 s`, `.bmp` format, p. 3).
- **Task:** Automated tooth detection, segmentation, and FDI tooth numbering across **`44` tooth classes** (`20` primary teeth + `24` permanent teeth, excluding third molars `18, 28, 38, 48` which do not develop at ages `6–12`; note text p. 3 line 219 writes "28 classes for permanent teeth... teeth numbered 18, 28, 38, and 48 were excluded") during the mixed dentition period (`6–12` years), comparing **`YOLOv8`, `YOLOv11`, `Mask R-CNN`, and `DeepLabV3`** under two scenarios:
  - *Scenario 1:* Overall dataset performance without age separation (pp. 1, 5, 7–8).
  - *Scenario 2:* Age-stratified analysis for each individual age group (`Ages 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, and 12`, pp. 1, 5, 8–10).

## data/counts

- **Source cohort (`pp. 1, 3, 5, 7`):** **`1,378` anonymized pediatric panoramic radiographs** (ages `6–12` years, collected Jan–Oct 2023 at the Department of Pediatric Dentistry, Ordu University, Turkey; Ethics approval `No. 280`, dated `27.10.2023`).
- **Exclusion criteria (`p. 3`):** Blurred/distorted contours, supernumerary teeth, cystic lesions or pathology distorting tooth boundaries, and active fixed orthodontic appliances.
- **Availability (`p. 12`):** Private dataset ("available from the corresponding author on reasonable request"). Total annotated tooth instance count and per-age image counts (plotted in `Fig. 3`, p. 5) are `not reported` numerically in tables.

## annotation

- **Tool & format (`pp. 3–4`):** Polygon-based tooth boundary annotation using `Colabeler` (`MacGenius, Blaze Software, CA, USA`), exported in `JSON` format with FDI tooth numbers.
- **Verification workflow (`pp. 3–4`):** Initially labeled by a pediatric dentistry research assistant (`E.O.`), then systematically reviewed and finalized via a **consensus meeting** between two senior specialists: a pediatric dentist (`H.S.`) and an oral & maxillofacial radiologist (`Y.Y.`), focusing on low-calcification tooth germs, superimpositions, and crowding.
- **Inter-annotator agreement (`kappa`):** `not reported`.

## split

- **Partitioning (`pp. 5, 7`):** Random **`70% / 20% / 10%` split performed at the image level** (with `135` unseen panoramic images in the `10%` test set, p. 7). Whether multiple radiographs belonged to the same patient is `not reported`.

## model

- **Evaluated architectures (`pp. 1, 5–7, Table 1`):**
  1. `YOLOv8` (anchor-free single-stage detector/segmenter; specific scale `n/s/m/l/x` is `not reported`)
  2. `YOLOv11` (refined FPN/PAN multi-scale neck + attention mechanisms; specific scale `not reported`)
  3. `Mask R-CNN` (two-stage RPN + `RoIAlign` instance segmentation)
  4. `DeepLabV3` (semantic segmentation with Atrous Spatial Pyramid Pooling `ASPP`).

## preprocessing

- **Operations (`pp. 3, 5, Table 1`):** Anonymized `.bmp` radiographs trained with **framework-specific default data augmentation and default input settings** (`Table 1`, p. 5). Explicit `CLAHE`, contrast enhancement, or custom filtering was **not applied** (`not reported`).

## training

- **Unified hyperparameters (`p. 5, Table 1`):** All four models trained with **`AdamW` optimizer, learning rate `0.001`, and batch size `8`**, keeping all other hyperparameters and augmentations at framework defaults. Exact epoch count and input image resolution are `not reported`.

## metrics/IoU

- **Reported metrics (`pp. 1, 7–9, Tables 2–3`):** Precision (`TP / (TP + FP)`), Recall (`TP / (TP + FN)`), F1-score, `mAP@0.5` (`mAP50`), and `mAP@0.5:0.95` (`mAP50-95`).

## timing/hardware

- **Compute hardware (`GPU/CPU`), parameter counts, and inference speed (`FPS` / ms per image):** **`not reported`**.

## limitations

- **Author-stated (`p. 12`):** (1) Single-center, single-device (`Planmeca ProMax 2D`) dataset lacking multi-center external validation; (2) reports only aggregate and age-stratified metrics without per-tooth-type breakdown or `95%` confidence intervals; (3) excludes pathology (`cysts`, `supernumerary teeth`) and fixed orthodontic appliances, limiting generalizability to abnormal clinical cases; (4) offline evaluation without clinician-in-the-loop workflow study.

## critique

- Direct comparative benchmark on `1,378` pediatric OPGs demonstrating why **`YOLOv11` and `YOLOv8` beat two-stage `Mask R-CNN` and semantic `DeepLabV3`** on overall `F1` (`0.8592` and `0.8450` vs `0.8259` and `0.7319`) and `mAP@0.5` (`0.8715` and `0.8689` vs `0.7870` and `0.6167`): `Mask R-CNN` achieves high precision (`0.9066`) but suffers a **`-11.7%` recall drop (`0.7584` vs `0.8755`)** in crowded/overlapping dentition, while `DeepLabV3` cannot separate touching tooth instances (`mAP50 = 0.6167`).
- Reporting gaps (`OS §3.2`): omits YOLO scale (`n/s/m/l/x`), training epochs, input resolution, hardware specs, and measured inference `FPS`/latency (`G6`). Also, `Table 3` age-specific scores (`0.94–0.98 mAP50` in every single age bin) are all higher than the pooled Scenario 1 `mAP50` (`0.8715`), suggesting that within-age models face lower cross-age class confusion than a single age-agnostic 44-class model.

## reuse

- Core `Group A` benchmark for **`RQ1` (`YOLOv11` vs `YOLOv8` vs `Mask R-CNN` vs `DeepLabV3`)**: justifies selecting single-stage `YOLO` over `Mask R-CNN` (higher recall on overlapping dental structures) and over semantic segmentation (`DeepLabV3`) when individual tooth/condition localization is required.

## conflicts

- None; complements `P0001` (`Panognostics-Plus`) and `P0006` (`Khurshid 2026`) by confirming `YOLOv11 > YOLOv8 > Mask R-CNN` on 2D OPG instance localization.
