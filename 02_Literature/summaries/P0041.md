## 1. Problem & Clinical / Technical Motivation
- **Clinical Challenge (`pp. 1–2, 5`):** Identifying developmental absence (hypodontia/agenesis) or excess (supernumerary teeth) of permanent tooth germs on pediatric panoramic radiographs (`OPGs`) guides timely endodontic, restorative, and surgical treatment planning, but pediatric OPGs suffer from mixed-dentition anatomical superimposition and positioning artifacts (`p. 6`).
- **Objective (`p. 2`):** Train and benchmark a real-time single-stage object detector (**`YOLOv4`**) to localize permanent tooth germs on a large pediatric OPG cohort (`ages 5–13`), which the authors present as the first CNN-based study specifically targeting permanent tooth germ detection (`p. 2, 6`).

---

## 2. Dataset, Annotation & Training Setup (`pp. 2–4`, `Figs. 1–2`)
- **Ethics & Cohort (`p. 2`):** Approved by Istanbul Okan University Ethics Committee (`13.01.2021 / 131`): **`4,518` anonymized pediatric OPGs** (`ages 5–13 yrs`; individual age/sex distribution suppressed). All radiographs acquired on a single unit (**Planmeca ProMax 2D Panoramic**, `68 kVp, 14 mA, 12 s`).
- **Annotation (`p. 2`, `Fig. 1`):** All visible permanent tooth germs in maxilla and mandible were manually enclosed with bounding boxes in **`LabelImg`** (`22`), totaling **`50,096` ground-truth germs** (`~11.09` germs/OPG overall; **single-class** without FDI numbering; annotator count, credentials, and inter-rater reliability `not reported`).
- **Data Split (`pp. 2–3`):** Described as `"10% test"` on `p. 2`, whereas `p. 3` specifies **`3,395` training OPGs** and **`523` test OPGs** (`5,898` GT tooth germs; `600` OPGs unaccounted for — see Section 4.2).
- **Model & Hyperparameters (`pp. 3–4`):** **`YOLOv4`** (`CSPDarknet53` backbone `[26]`, input resized to `608 × 608` [contrast `416 × 416` in `Fig. 2` diagram], NO `CLAHE` or enhancement applied). Claimed `PyTorch` implementation (`100` epochs, `Adam`, `LR = 0.001`, `batch size = 32`, **`subdivision = 4`**, `NVIDIA RTX 2080 Ti 11 GB` + `192 GB RAM`; `IoU` matching threshold `not reported`).

## 4. Limitations & Reviewer Methodological Critique (`OS §3.2`)

### 4.1 Author-Discussed Constraints (`p. 6`)
*(No dedicated `Limitations` subsection appears; the Discussion notes three constraints on `p. 6`:)*
1. **Absence of tooth numbering (`p. 6`):** Single-class germ detection cannot tell clinicians *which* specific permanent tooth germ is developmentally missing; integrating numbering algorithms is identified as future work.
2. **Pediatric OPG artifacts (`p. 6`):** Anatomical superimposition and severe maxillo-mandibular positioning difficulties degrade pediatric panoramic detail.
3. **Lack of direct prior comparators (`p. 6`):** Authors note no earlier deep-learning permanent-tooth-germ studies existed for head-to-head comparison.

### 4.2 Reviewer Methodological Critique (`OS §3.2` Audit)
1. **Missing `600` Radiographs in Split Arithmetic (`pp. 2–3`):**
   - Total dataset is **`4,518` OPGs**, `p. 2` says `10%` (`~452`) was used for testing, yet `p. 3` states **`3,395` train + `523` test** (`= 3,918`), leaving **`600` OPGs (`13.3%`, and `50,096 - 5,898` germ counts across train/val) completely unaccounted for** (`no validation split mentioned`, or `3,395` is a typo for `3,995`). Patient-level split independence (across children imaged serially between ages `5–13`) is also `not reported`.
2. **Corpus Tag Correction (`CLAHE` / `YOLOv3` / `Faster R-CNN` Misattribution):**
   - Prior screening tags listed `CLAHE` and `YOLOv3; Faster R-CNN` for `P0041`. Full-text verification confirms **`P0041` tested ONLY `YOLOv4` without `CLAHE`**; `Faster R-CNN` (`Tuzoff et al. [16]`) and `YOLOv3 + CLAHE` (`Ha et al. [19]`) appear purely as cited background studies (`pp. 2, 6`).
3. **Evaluation, Statistical & Reporting Flaws (`pp. 2–4, 7`):**
   - **Missing localization rigor:** Does not specify the **`IoU` matching threshold** (`e.g., 0.50`) or confidence cutoff used to declare `TP/FP`, omits **`mAP50-95`**, includes zero competing baseline models or clinician comparisons, and does not report annotator credentials or inter-rater agreement.
   - **Flawed statistical statements (`p. 4`):** Defines `"TN"` (`a correct detection other than a permanent tooth germ`) despite `TN` being undefined in single-class YOLO detection; runs a meaningless `t-test` on confidence values with TP/FP coded `"1"/"0"`; and conflates a `0.5` Precision/F1 baseline line in `Fig. 3` with a statistical p-value (**`"It is significant when the F1-score yields a value above P = 0.5. Since P > 0.5 in this study..."`**, `p. 4`).
   - **Implementation & diagram contradictions (`pp. 3–4, 7`):** Text claims `PyTorch` + `608 × 608` input, yet specifies Darknet's **`subdivision = 4`** (`p. 4`) and reproduces an **aerial object detection `YOLOv4` diagram (`Fig. 2`, citing `Ali et al., 2021 [37]`) showing `416 × 416` input (`52×52, 26×26, 13×13` heads)**; cites a UAV video paper (`[25]`) for YOLO speed/accuracy. Code and dataset are private.
