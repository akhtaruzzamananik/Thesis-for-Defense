## Verified citation

Wang, L., & Li, Z. (2026). Transformer-based intelligent detection model for early dental caries in panoramic radiographs. *Scientific Reports*, 16, 3507. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-33391-y (PDF p. 1).

## question/task/modality

- **Modality:** 2D dental panoramic radiographs (`Planmeca ProMax` & `Sirona Orthophos XG`, `60–85 kVp, 4–16 mA, 12–18 s`, `2304 × 1152` to `3000 × 1500` px, 8-bit grayscale, pp. 3–4).
- **Task:** 4-grade bounding-box detection and severity grading of dental caries (`D1: Initial enamel caries`, `D2: Established enamel caries`, `D3: Dentin caries`, `D4: Deep dentin caries`; `Table 1`, p. 2) using a domain-adapted **Hierarchical Vision Transformer + FPN detector** (`ViT` + Multi-Scale Fusion `MSF` + Spatial Attention `SA` + Enhanced 2D Positional Encoding `EPE` + Feature Enhancement Module `FEM`), benchmarked against **`Faster R-CNN`, `RetinaNet`, `YOLOv5`, `EfficientDet`, `DETR`, and `Swin Transformer`** (pp. 1, 3–8, Table 4).

## data/counts

- **Source cohort (`pp. 1, 3–5, 7, 12–13, Table 3`):** **`3,856` panoramic radiographs** from `3,856` adult patients (ages `18–75` years, `1` OPG per patient) collected Jan 2021 – Dec 2023 across three tertiary dental hospitals in China (General Hospital of PLA Northern Theater Command, Shenyang; Ethics `IRB-2021-001`, `IRB-2021-002`, `IRB-2021-003`).
- **Lesion counts (`Table 3`, p. 7 & `pp. 4–5`):** **`12,847` annotated carious lesions** across the `3,856` OPGs:
  - *By severity (`Table 3`, p. 7):*
    - `D1` (initial enamel caries): **`2,611` (`20.3%`)** (`1,824` train / `392` val / `395` test)
    - `D2` (established enamel caries): **`1,913` (`14.9%`)** (`1,278` train / `274` val / `361` test)
    - `D3` (dentin caries): **`3,067` (`23.9%`)** (`2,145` train / `459` val / `463` test)
    - `D4` (deep dentin caries): **`5,256` (`40.9%`)** (`3,588` train / `768` val / `900` test)
    - *Total:* `12,847` (`8,835` train / `1,893` val / `2,119` test; early `D1–D2` = `35.2%`).
  - *By surface (`pp. 4–5`):* `Proximal = 8,762 (68.2%)`, `Occlusal = 3,121 (24.3%)`, `Buccal/Lingual = 964 (7.5%)`; posterior teeth (molars/premolars) account for `68.4%` (`p. 5`).
- **Availability (`p. 11`):** Private clinical dataset ("available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request, subject to institutional data sharing agreements").

## annotation

- **Protocol (`pp. 4–5`):** Three experienced dentists (`10+` years clinical experience) independently labeled all carious lesions with bounding boxes, severity grades (`D1–D4`), and surface locations (`occlusal, proximal-mesial, proximal-distal, buccal, lingual`).
- **Quality control & agreement (`p. 5`):** Two-stage verification where any inter-rater disagreement exceeding `20% IoU` (`IoU < 0.80`) or severity grade discrepancy was adjudicated by a senior endodontist. Inter-rater reliability achieved **Cohen's kappa `κ = 0.87`** (and model-vs-expert concordance achieved `κ = 0.84`, p. 8).

## split

- **Patient-level split (`pp. 5, 7, Table 3`):** Stratified random **`70% / 15% / 15%` patient-level split** ensuring zero data leakage (`2,699` training OPGs [`8,835` lesions], `578` validation OPGs [`1,893` lesions], and `579` test OPGs [`2,119` lesions]).

## model

- **Proposed Architecture (`pp. 3, 5–8, Tables 2 & 5`):**
  - Hierarchical Vision Transformer (`512 × 512` input, `16 × 16` patch size -> `32 × 32` tokens, embedding dim `768`, `12` encoder layers, `12` attention heads, features extracted at layers `3, 6, 9, 12`) + FPN detection head + `3`-branch parallel decoder (classification, `GIoU` box regression, severity grading).
  - Enhancements (`Table 5`, p. 8): `Baseline ViT` (`86.2M` params) + Multi-Scale Fusion `MSF` (`89.5M`) + Spatial Attention `SA` (`92.8M`) + Enhanced 2D Positional Encoding `EPE` (`93.1M`) + Feature Enhancement Module `FEM` (**`95.7M` parameters, `187.4 GFLOPs`**, `8.2 GB` peak training VRAM, pp. 6, 8).
- **Compared Baselines (`Table 4`, p. 7):** `Faster R-CNN`, `RetinaNet`, `YOLOv5` (specific scale `s/m/l/x` `not reported`), `EfficientDet`, `DETR`, and `Swin Transformer`.

## preprocessing

- **Pipeline (`p. 3`):** (1) **Histogram equalization**, (2) **bilateral filtering**, (3) intensity normalization, and (4) resizing to `512 × 512` pixels.
- **Training Augmentation (`pp. 3, 5`):** Random rotation (`±15°`), horizontal flip (`p = 0.5`), brightness (`±20%`), contrast (`±15%`), Gaussian noise (`σ = 0.02`), and random crop (`scale [0.8, 1.2]`).
- **Post-processing (`p. 3`):** Soft-NMS with Gaussian decay and adaptive confidence thresholds (`0.3` for `D1–D2`, `0.5` for `D3–D4`).

## training

- **Schedule & Optimizer (`pp. 3, 5, Table 2`):** Two-stage training: `50` epochs ImageNet pretraining followed by `200` epochs end-to-end fine-tuning (early stopping on validation `mAP`), `AdamW` optimizer, initial learning rate `1 × 10^-4` with cosine annealing, dropout `0.1`, batch size `16` with automatic mixed precision (`AMP`), multi-task loss `L_total = 1.0 L_cls + 2.0 L_box + 1.5 L_focal`.

## metrics/IoU

- **Reported metrics (`pp. 5, 7–8, Tables 4–5`):** `mAP@0.5`, per-grade `AP@0.5` (`D1, D2, D3, D4`), sensitivity (`Recall`), specificity, `FPS` (`ms/image`), parameters (`M`), and `GFLOPs`. Stricter `mAP@0.5:0.95` is `not reported`.

## timing/hardware

- **Hardware (`p. 5`):** Computing cluster with **`4 × NVIDIA V100 GPUs (32 GB memory each)`**, PyTorch `1.12.0`, CUDA `11.6`, Ubuntu `20.04 LTS`.
- **Inference Speed (`Table 4`, p. 7 & `pp. 1, 5, 9`):**
  - **`YOLOv5`:** **`45.7 FPS` (`~21.9 ms/image`, fastest model — `3.2×` faster than proposed Transformer)**
  - `EfficientDet`: `28.4 FPS` (`~35.2 ms`)
  - `RetinaNet`: `22.6 FPS` (`~44.2 ms`)
  - `Faster R-CNN`: `18.3 FPS` (`~54.6 ms`)
  - `Swin Transformer`: `16.8 FPS` (`~59.5 ms`)
  - **`Proposed Transformer`:** **`14.2 FPS` (`70 ms/radiograph`)**
  - `DETR`: `12.5 FPS` (`~80.0 ms`).

## results with locators

- **Multi-Model Detection Benchmark (`Table 4`, p. 7 — `mAP@0.5 / AP(D1) / AP(D2) / AP(D3) / AP(D4) / FPS`):**
  - **`Proposed Transformer` (`95.7M`, `187.4 GFLOPs`):** **`87.3%` / `79.8%` / `82.6%` / `91.5%` / `94.7%` / `14.2 FPS`**
  - `Swin Transformer`: `84.6% / 74.5% / 79.2% / 89.4% / 93.8% / 16.8 FPS`
  - `DETR`: `83.4% / 72.1% / 77.8% / 88.1% / 92.6% / 12.5 FPS`
  - `Faster R-CNN`: `81.5% / 68.4% / 74.1% / 86.2% / 91.5% / 18.3 FPS`
  - `EfficientDet`: `80.9% / 67.8% / 73.9% / 85.3% / 90.2% / 28.4 FPS`
  - `RetinaNet`: `80.1% / 66.9% / 72.8% / 84.7% / 89.8% / 22.6 FPS`
  - **`YOLOv5`:** **`78.2%` / `63.2%` / `70.5%` / `82.1%` / `88.4%` / `45.7 FPS`**.
- **Component Ablation (`Table 5`, p. 8 — `mAP@0.5 / AP(D1) / AP(D2) / Params`):**
  - `Baseline ViT` (`86.2M`): `82.1% / 70.3% / 75.6%`
  - `+ MSF` (`89.5M`): `84.3% / 74.8% / 78.9%` (`+2.2%` mAP)
  - `+ MSF + SA` (`92.8M`): `85.8% / 77.1% / 80.7%` (`+1.5%` mAP)
  - `+ MSF + SA + EPE` (`93.1M`): `86.5% / 78.4% / 81.8%` (`+0.7%` mAP)
  - `+ MSF + SA + EPE + FEM` (`95.7M`): **`87.3% / 79.8% / 82.6%`** (`+0.8%` mAP; total `+5.2%` mAP and `+9.5%` `D1 AP`).
- **Diagnostic Sensitivity & Error Breakdown (`pp. 1, 7–9`):**
  - Overall test sensitivity `88.4%` and specificity `91.7%` vs expert dentist sensitivity `86.2%` and specificity `89.5%` (`p. 7`); sensitivity `81.3%` for `D1` and `84.7%` for `D2` (`pp. 1, 8`).
  - By tooth group (`p. 8`): Premolars `87.3%` (though proximal premolar caries drops to `79.3%` vs `87.6%` in molars due to overlap, `p. 9`), Molars `84.6%`, Anterior teeth `76.9%`.
  - Error modes (`pp. 8–9`): `87` false positives (`31.0%` developmental grooves, `24.1%` cervical burnout artifacts, `18.4%` restorative interfaces); `68` false negatives (`55.9%` minimal `D1` lesions `<30%` mineral loss, `27.9%` overlapping proximal contacts, `16.2%` root surface lesions). Accuracy drops to `82.7%` in patients with orthodontic appliances (`p. 9`).

## limitations

- **Author-stated (`pp. 9–10`):** (1) Reduced accuracy (`82.7% mAP`) on regions obscured by metallic restorations or orthodontic appliances; (2) premolar interproximal overlap limits 2D OPG sensitivity (`79.3%`), requiring bitewing confirmation; (3) geographically restricted cohort (China); (4) 2D OPG modality only.

## critique

- **Important architectural and methodological findings (`OS §3.2`):**
  1. **Accuracy vs Compute/Latency Trade-off (`RQ1`, `G6`):** While the custom `ViT+FPN` model (`95.7M` params, `187.4 GFLOPs`) outperforms `YOLOv5` (`87.3%` vs `78.2% mAP@0.5`), it requires **`4 × V100 (32 GB) GPUs`** and runs at only **`14.2 FPS` (`70 ms/image`, below the `30 FPS` real-time threshold)**, whereas `YOLOv5` runs `3.2×` faster (`45.7 FPS`) — and the authors only tested legacy `YOLOv5` (`2020`), omitting modern `YOLOv8/v9/v10/v11/v12` which include attention (`C2PSA`/`R-ELAN`) at `<25M` parameters!
  2. **Internal count discrepancy (`p. 7 Table 3` vs `p. 8 line 476`):** `Table 3` (`p. 7`) lists `395` `D1` + `361` `D2` = **`756` early `D1–D2` lesions** in the test set (and `2,119` total test lesions), whereas the text on `p. 8` states *"evaluated on 534 D1-D2 lesions in the test set"* and *"examined 87 false positives and 68 false negatives"* (which would imply `96.8%` recall if out of `2,119` lesions, conflicting with `88.4%` sensitivity).
  3. **Radiographic visibility of `D1` enamel caries (`UD04`):** Even the paper's own Literature Review (`p. 2`) notes that *"Early dental caries require 30–40% mineral loss before becoming radiographically detectable"*, yet claims `79.8% AP` / `81.3%` sensitivity on `D1` initial outer-enamel caries on `512 × 512` down-sampled OPGs without bitewing/clinical ground-truth verification.

## reuse

- Strong comparator for **`RQ1`** (`YOLOv5` `45.7 FPS` vs heavy Vision Transformer `95.7M` params / `187.4 GFLOPs` / `14.2 FPS`), **`G3`** (omits modern `YOLOv8–v12` baselines when claiming Transformers beat YOLO), and **error-mode taxonomy (`S15`)** (developmental grooves `31.0%`, cervical burnout `24.1%`, restorative margins `18.4%`, premolar overlap `27.9%`).

## conflicts

- Contrasts with `P0003` (`Biçengil 2026`, `YOLOv8x mAP@0.5 = 0.541`) and `P0009` (`Peng 2026`, `Caries mAP@0.5 = 0.2497`); `P0012`'s much higher `D1–D4` scores (`78.2%–87.3%`) rely on `512 × 512` OPG-only consensus labels (`κ = 0.87`) and adaptive low confidence thresholds (`0.3` for `D1–D2`).

## unresolved fields

- Specific `YOLOv5` scale (`n/s/m/l/x`), `mAP@0.5:0.95`, preprocessing ablation (effect of histogram equalization + bilateral filter vs raw OPG), and resolution of the `756 vs 534` test `D1–D2` lesion count discrepancy are `not reported`.
