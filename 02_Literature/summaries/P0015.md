## Verified citation

Eftimie, S., Ileni, T., Iacob, L., Hedeșiu, M., & Dioșan, L. (2025). Tooth-level detection and mapping of dental pathologies on panoramic radiographs using YOLOv11 and RT-DETR. *MethodsX*, 15, 103696. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mex.2025.103696 (PDF p. 1).

## question/task/modality

- **Modality:** 2D adult panoramic radiographs (`OPGs`, pp. 1–3).
- **Task:** Two-stage automated tooth-level diagnostic workflow (`pp. 1–3, 5–7`):
  1. *Stage 1 (Tooth Segmentation):* `32`-class FDI individual tooth polygon segmentation (`classes 0–31`) on the Tufts Dental Database using **`YOLOv11n/s/m/l/x-seg`**, benchmarked against `FPN`, `U-Net`, `U-Net++`, `PSPNet`, `DeepLabV3`, and `DeepLabV3+` (`Tables 3–4`, p. 12).
  2. *Stage 2 (Pathology Detection):* **`12`-class dental pathology and treatment bounding-box detection** (`prosthetic restorations, dental implants, fillings/obturations, endodontic treatments, caries, periapical lesions, periodontal bone loss, furcation lesions, impacted teeth, root fragments, orthodontic treatments, surgical devices`; `apical surgery [APS]` and `root resorption [ROR]` excluded from the initial 14 labels due to extreme scarcity, `pp. 3, 13`) comparing **`YOLOv8x` (retrained `[14]`), `YOLOv11n/s/m/l/x`, `RT-DETR-l`, and `RT-DETR-x`** on internal and multi-center external validation cohorts (`Tables 1–2`, pp. 7–8).
  3. *Stage 3 (Tooth–Pathology Association):* Spatial overlay linking predicted pathology boxes to segmented teeth at **`IoU ≥ 0.25`** (`pp. 6–7, Tables 5–6`, p. 12).

## data/counts

- **Three Evaluated Datasets (`pp. 2–3, 13–14`):**
  1. **`UMFIH Dental Pathology Dataset` (Internal Cohort, `p. 3`):** **`1,628` adult panoramic radiographs** (`Vatech PCH-2500`, `804 × 415` to `2980 × 1536` px, mean `2455 × 1383 ± 459 × 286` px) collected Apr 2021 – Dec 2023 at Iuliu Hațieganu University of Medicine and Pharmacy, Cluj-Napoca, Romania (Ethics `117/4.06.2024` & `244/13.12.2024`).
  2. **Multi-Center External Validation Dataset (`p. 3`):** **`180` panoramic radiographs** collected from multiple medical centers across Romania using the same annotation protocol.
     - **Open Zenodo Release (`pp. 2, 13–14`):** Both the `1,628`-OPG `UMFIH` dataset and the `180`-OPG external validation dataset are publicly available on **Zenodo (`doi:10.5281/zenodo.15487429`, `https://zenodo.org/records/15487430`)**.
  3. **`Tufts Dental Database` (`p. 3`):** **`1,000` panoramic radiographs** (`1615 × 840` px) with `32` FDI tooth polygon classes (`0–31`).

## annotation

- **Tools & protocol (`pp. 2–3`):** `UMFIH` and external pathology datasets manually annotated with YOLO bounding boxes in `Makesense.ai`; `Tufts` annotated with `32` FDI tooth polygons.
- **Inter-annotator agreement (`kappa`):** `not reported` in this `MethodsX` article (references companion study `Mureșanu et al., Diagnostics 2024, 14:2336` `[14]`).

## split

- **`UMFIH` Pathology Split (`p. 3`):** **`1,375` training** (`84.5%`), **`153` validation** (`9.4%`), and **`100` internal test** (`6.1%`) images, plus **`180` external multi-center test** images (`Table 2`, p. 8).
- **`Tufts` Tooth Segmentation Split (`p. 3`):** **`850` training** (`85%`) and **`150` test** (`15%`) images (`Table 3`, p. 12). Patient-level grouping is `not reported` (1 OPG per adult patient is standard in both cohorts).

## model

- **Pathology Detectors (`pp. 3, 5, 13, Table 7`):** Ultralytics COCO/ImageNet-pretrained **`YOLOv8x`** (`68.16M` params, `258.19 GFLOPs`), **`YOLO11n`** (`2.59M`, `6.45 GFLOPs`), **`YOLO11s`** (`9.43M`, `21.57 GFLOPs`), **`YOLO11m`** (`20.06M`, `68.24 GFLOPs`), **`YOLO11l`** (`25.32M`, `87.33 GFLOPs`), **`YOLO11x`** (`56.88M`, `195.53 GFLOPs`), **`RT-DETR-l`** (`32.83M`, `108.05 GFLOPs`), and **`RT-DETR-x`** (`67.33M`, `232.39 GFLOPs`).
- **Tooth Segmenters (`p. 12, Tables 3–4`):** `YOLO11n/s/m/l/x-seg` vs `FPN`, `U-Net`, `U-Net++`, `PSPNet`, `DeepLabV3`, and `DeepLabV3+`.

## preprocessing

- **4 Controlled Formatting & Resolution Experiments (`pp. 5–6, 8, Fig. 7`):**
  - `Experiment 1`: Full image resized to `1024 × 1024`
  - `Experiment 2`: Full image resized to `1280 × 1280`
  - `Experiment 3`: Central `75%` crop (`25%` peripheral margins removed) resized to `1024 × 1024`
  - `Experiment 4`: Central `75%` crop (`25%` peripheral margins removed) resized to `1280 × 1280`.
  - **Crucial Cropping Audit (`p. 8`):** Removing `25%` margins **eliminated `5.7%` of ground-truth pathology boxes and partially cropped `20.8%` of boxes** on the test set! No `CLAHE` or manual contrast filtering was applied (`p. 5`).
- **Training Augmentation (`p. 5`):** `MixUp (20%)`, `Copy-Paste (10%)`, `Random Erasing (30%)`, `Mosaic (10%)`, rotation (`±15°`), scaling (`30%`), perspective, translation (`30%`), shearing (`15%`), HSV value brightness/contrast (`v = 0.4`, `h = 0, s = 0` on 3-channel grayscale), horizontal flip (`50%`), vertical flip (`50%`), and dropout (`0.3`).

## training

- **Hardware & Batch Size (`p. 5`):** Workstation with **Intel i7-12700H CPU** and **NVIDIA RTX 3060 GPU**; batch size `32` for `YOLOv11n` and `8` for `YOLOv11x` and `RT-DETR-l/x`. Exact epoch count, learning rate, and optimizer are `not reported` (`retrained with the hyperparameters and optimizations applied in this study`, `Table 1`, p. 7).

## metrics/IoU

- **Reported metrics (`pp. 6–8, 12–13`):** Box (`B`) and Mask (`M`) `mAP@50`, `mAP@50–95`, Precision, Recall; binary pixel accuracy, `IoU`, and `Dice` (`Table 4`); tooth-pathology association `P_combined`, `R_combined`, `F1_combined` at `IoU ≥ 0.25` (`Tables 5–6`); and `Params (M)`, `GFLOPs`, `CPU Inf. (ms)`, and `GPU Inf. (ms)` (`Table 7`).

## limitations

- **Author-stated (`p. 13`):** (1) Severe domain-shift drop (`~22%` `mAP@50` drop from `0.7697` internal to `0.5476` external validation, `p. 9`); (2) class imbalance forced exclusion of rare `apical surgery (APS)` and `root resorption (ROR)` classes; (3) frequent confusion between prosthetic crowns and large coronal fillings/obturations; (4) higher false-positive rate in screening mode.

## critique

- **One of the most directly relevant benchmark papers in our entire corpus (`OS §3.2`):**
  1. **Direct head-to-head `YOLO11` (`n/s/m/l/x`) vs `RT-DETR` (`l/x`) + `U-Net`/`DeepLabV3+` with full CPU/GPU latency (`Table 7`, p. 13):** Shows that at `1280 × 1280` (`Exp 2`), `RT-DETR-l` edges out `YOLO11m` by `+2.81%` internal `mAP@50` (`0.7697` vs `0.7416`) and `+1.10%` external `mAP@50` (`0.5476` vs `0.5366`), **but `YOLO11m` beats `RT-DETR-l` on external `mAP@50-95` (`0.2636` vs `0.2558`), external Precision (`0.6872` vs `0.5634`), uses `39%` fewer parameters (`20.06M` vs `32.83M`), `37%` fewer GFLOPs (`68.24` vs `108.05`), and runs `2.02×` faster on an RTX 3060 GPU (`33.23 ms` [`30.1 FPS`] vs `67.11 ms` [`14.9 FPS`])**!
  2. **Proves why medium-scale models (`YOLO11m` / `RT-DETR-l`) beat extra-large (`YOLO11x` / `RT-DETR-x`) on `~1,375` OPGs (`pp. 7–9`):** `YOLO11m` (`0.7416` internal / `0.5366` external) outperforms `YOLO11x` (`0.7226` / `0.5025`), and `RT-DETR-l` (`0.7697` / `0.5476`) outperforms `RT-DETR-x` (`0.7542` / `0.5280`), confirming overfitting of `x`-scale models on medium-sized dental datasets.
  3. **Quantifies the danger of fixed `75%` central cropping (`p. 8`):** Blind `25%` margin cropping cut `20.8%` of ground-truth boxes and deleted `5.7%` of peripheral lesions!

## conflicts

- Whereas `P0006` (`Khurshid 2026`) and `P0011` (`Scarano 2026`) found `YOLOv11/v12 > RT-DETR/RF-DETR` on `mAP@50`, `P0015` finds `RT-DETR-l` slightly ahead of `YOLO11m` on `mAP@50` (`0.7697` vs `0.7416` internal; `0.5476` vs `0.5366` external) while `YOLO11m` wins on external `mAP@50-95` (`0.2636` vs `0.2558`) and is `2×` faster (`30.1` vs `14.9 FPS`). Presenting both sides strengthens our balanced `RQ1` synthesis!
