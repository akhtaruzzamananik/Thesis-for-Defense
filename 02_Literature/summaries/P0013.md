## Verified citation

Özden, S., Kula, B., & Tankuş, M. (2026). Automated deep learning detection of orthodontically induced external apical root resorption in maxillary incisors on panoramic radiographs. *Progress in Orthodontics*, 27, 7. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40510-026-00610-9 (PDF p. 1).

## question/task/modality

- **Modality:** Longitudinal 2D panoramic radiographs (`Planmeca Proline XC`, `66 kV, 5.0 mA, 18 s`, p. 3) acquired before and after `≥12` months of fixed orthodontic treatment.
- **Task:** Automated quantification and **4-grade Sharpe severity classification (`Grade 0` to `Grade 3`)** of Orthodontically Induced External Apical Root Resorption (`OIEARR`) in the `4` maxillary central and lateral incisors by comparing two **`YOLOv12x`** paradigms (pp. 1, 3, 5–7):
  1. *Object Detection (`OD`, `YOLOv12x`):* Localizes `8` rectangular bounding boxes per OPG (`4` crown boxes + `4` root boxes, `Fig. 3`, p. 6) and estimates root length from vertical box height.
  2. *Pose Estimation (`PE`, `YOLOv12x-pose`):* Localizes `12` anatomical keypoints per OPG (`3` landmarks per maxillary incisor: `incisal edge`, `cervical margin`, and `root apex`, `Fig. 2`, p. 5) and computes crown-normalized percentage root shortening between pre- and post-treatment OPGs (`pp. 3, 6–7`).

## data/counts

- **Source cohort (`pp. 1, 3–5, Fig. 1`):** From `1,800` screened orthodontic patients at İnönü University (2010–2025; Ethics Approval `2025/7540`, dated `30/04/2025`), `1,044` met inclusion criteria (ages `10–18` years, `≥12` months fixed orthodontic therapy, high-quality pre/post OPGs) and `732` were excluded (impacted maxillary incisors/canines, craniofacial anomalies, missing anterior teeth, incomplete root development, pre-treatment EARR, prior ortho therapy), yielding **`312` patients and `624` panoramic radiographs** (`312` pre-treatment + `312` post-treatment OPGs; `1,248` maxillary incisors).
- **Power analysis (`p. 3`):** `G*Power 3.1.9.7` one-tailed exact binomial test (`H0 = 0.25`, `g = 0.05`, `α = 0.05`, `power = 0.80`) required at least `494` samples (`618` total with train/test).
- **Availability (`p. 14`):** Private clinical dataset ("available from the corresponding author if needed"). Per-grade tooth counts across `Grades 0–3` are `not reported`.

## annotation

- **Clinical Ground Truth (`p. 3, 8–9`):** Two experienced orthodontists (`S.Ö.` and `M.T.`, `>5` years experience) graded each maxillary incisor in a joint consensus session using Sharpe's 4-point scale:
  - `Grade 0`: No apical root resorption (`0%`)
  - `Grade 1`: Slight blunting of the root apex
  - `Grade 2`: Moderate resorption up to `1/3` of root length
  - `Grade 3`: Severe resorption exceeding `1/3` of root length.
- **Reproducibility (`pp. 8–9`):** One-month re-evaluation of a random `20%` subset yielded an **Intraclass Correlation Coefficient `ICC = 0.989`**.
- **Spatial Landmark & Box Labeling (`p. 3`):** Manual annotation in `CVAT` (`12` keypoints/OPG for `PE`; `8` crown/root bounding boxes/OPG for `OD`).

## split

- **Dataset partitioning (`p. 5`):** `80% / 10% / 10%` split of the `624` radiographs into **`496` training**, **`64` validation**, and **`64` testing** OPGs.
- **Test set structure (`p. 5`):** The `64` test OPGs consist of paired **`32` pre-treatment and `32` post-treatment radiographs from `32` patients** (`32 × 4 = 128` test maxillary incisors). Whether the `496` train and `64` validation radiographs were strictly separated by patient is not explicitly stated (`p. 5`).

## model

- **Architectures (`pp. 3, 5–6, Fig. 4`):**
  - `YOLOv12x` (Object Detection `OD`) and `YOLOv12x-pose` (Pose Estimation `PE`) in PyTorch / Ultralytics (described on p. 5 as sharing a `CSPDarkNetV8` backbone and `BiFPN++` neck; `OD` uses a decoupled classification/box head + NMS, while `PE` uses a dual heatmap + offset keypoint head).
  - Note: No other architectures (`YOLOv8/v11` or `RT-DETR`) were trained by the authors; `YOLOv8`, `YOLOv11`, and `RT-DETR` are only discussed from prior literature (`Saber et al. [30]` and `Karamüftüoğlu [45]`) on `p. 11`.
  - Parameter counts (`M`) and `GFLOPs`: `not reported`.

## preprocessing

- **Normalization & geometric correction (`pp. 6–7`):** Pixel intensities scaled from `[0, 255]` to `[0, 1]`, resized to `640 × 640` (`Table 1`, p. 7). Predicted `(X, Y)` coordinates are normalized by radiograph width/height, and post-treatment root length is rescaled by the pre/post crown-length ratio (`Correction Factor = Initial crown length / Post crown length`, `p. 7`) before computing percentage root loss.
- **Training augmentation (`Table 1`, pp. 7–8):**
  - *Photometric & contrast:* **`CLAHE` (`clip_limit = (1, 4)`, `grid_size = (8, 8)`)**, `ToGray` (`weighted_average`), HSV (`s = 0.7, v = 0.4, h = 0.015`).
  - *Filtering & geometric:* `Blur` (`limit = (3, 7)`), `MedianBlur` (`limit = (3, 7)`), rotation (`1.0°`), translation (`0.1`), scale (`0.5`).

## training

- **Hyperparameters (`Table 1`, p. 7):** Up to `1,000` epochs with early stopping (`patience = 30`), `AdamW` optimizer, initial learning rate `10^-3` decaying via cosine schedule to `10^-5` (`3` warmup epochs), momentum `0.9`, weight decay `5 × 10^-4`, batch size `4`, image size `640`, loss gains: `box = 7.5`, `cls = 0.5`, `dfl = 1.5`, `pose = 12.0`, `kobj = 2.0`.

## metrics/IoU

- **Reported metrics (`pp. 7–10, Table 2`):**
  - Methodological root-length measurement error (`mean ± SD %`) on `Grade 0` reference teeth (`p. 9`).
  - 4-class Sharpe grade classification Accuracy, Precision, Recall, Specificity, F1-score (`Table 2`, p. 9), confusion matrices (`Fig. 6`), and one-vs-rest `ROC-AUC` (`Fig. 7`, p. 10).
- **Bounding-box / keypoint localization metrics (`mAP@0.5`, `mAP@0.5:0.95`, `IoU`, `OKS`):** **`not reported`**.

## timing/hardware

- **Hardware (`p. 6`):** Workstation with **Intel Core i9-12900K CPU**, `128 GB DDR5 RAM`, and **NVIDIA Quadro RTX A6000 GPU**.
- **Inference speed (`p. 13`):** Both `YOLOv12x` (`OD`) and `YOLOv12x-pose` (`PE`) process a single panoramic radiograph in **`<500 ms` (`>2 FPS`)** on a standard work computer, completing all pre/post calculations and report generation in **`<1 s`** (exact ms/FPS on the RTX A6000 GPU is `not reported`).

## results with locators

## limitations

- **Author-stated (`p. 13`):** (1) Restricted to maxillary central and lateral incisors (`4` teeth per OPG), leaving premolars and molars untested; (2) 2D panoramic radiography cannot capture 3D volumetric/labiopalatal root resorption and is sensitive to head positioning; (3) evaluates only `YOLOv12` without benchmarking other architectures on the dataset; (4) single-center, single-device (`Planmeca Proline XC`) cohort.

## critique

- **Methodological & Geometric Insights (`OS §3.2`):**
  1. Demonstrates why **axis-aligned bounding boxes (`OD`) perform poorly when a clinical grade depends on a sub-millimeter linear length ratio along tilted teeth** (`OD` weighted `F1 = 0.60`, `Grade 1–2 F1 = 0.47–0.58`, length error `3.13% ± 5.85%`), whereas **anatomical keypoint localization (`YOLOv12x-pose`)** reduces length error to `0.74% ± 1.81%` and raises weighted `F1` to `0.88` (`+28%` F1).
  2. **Reporting omissions & internal inconsistencies:** (a) Omits standard detection box `mAP@0.5` / `mAP@0.5:0.95` and pose `OKS mAP`; (b) swaps parenthetical model names in `Table 2` headers (`p. 9`); (c) states root length is `cervical-to-apical` distance on `p. 3` (`line 247`) but `incisal-to-apical` distance on `p. 6` (`line 378`); and (d) cites three slightly different `PE` `ROC-AUC` ranges (`0.90–0.99` on p. 1, `0.93–0.98` on p. 10, and `0.88–0.95` on p. 13).

## reuse

- Useful `Group A` reference for `YOLOv12x` on 2D OPG, illustrating: (1) `CLAHE (limit 1–4, grid 8×8)` integration in the Ultralytics training pipeline (`Table 1`, p. 7), (2) `ICC = 0.989` observer reliability reporting, and (3) why axis-aligned bounding-box dimensions alone cannot reliably measure 1–2 mm apical root blunting (`UD04`).
