## 1. Problem & Clinical / Technical Motivation
- **Clinical & methodological gap (`pp. 1–2`):** Panoramic radiographs (`OPGs`) are distorted by anatomical superimposition, magnification, and observer fatigue, limiting diagnostic consistency (`p. 1`). Prior to this review, Umer et al. (`2022 [1]`) highlighted a lack of standardized evaluation methodology across deep learning tooth-detection studies, leaving uncertainty over clinical readiness.
- **Objective (`p. 2`):** Conduct a `PRISMA-DTA`-compliant (`PROSPERO CRD42023453081`) systematic review and diagnostic test accuracy meta-analysis evaluating deep learning (`DL-CNN`) performance on OPG tooth **object detection (`OD`)**, **object segmentation (`OS`)**, and **object labeling (`OL`)** (primary outcome), plus impacted **mesiodens / supernumerary & canine detection** (secondary outcomes).

## 2. Search Strategy, Screening & Study Characteristics (`pp. 2–10`, `Fig. 1`, `Table 1`)
- **Search databases & window (`pp. 1–2, 4`):** **`PubMed (Medline)` (`1,536`)**, **`Embase` (`426`)**, and **`Cochrane` (`65`)** searched in `December 2022` and updated up to `September 2023`, restricted to **`JCR`-indexed journals** (`2013` onward).
- **`PRISMA` study selection flow (`p. 3–4`, `Fig. 1`):**
  - **Records identified:** `2,027` in `Fig. 1` (`1,536 + 426 + 65`; note `Abstract`, p. 1 mistakenly states `2,207`).
  - **Duplicates removed:** `310` -> **`1,717` records screened** (inter-reviewer title/abstract agreement `98%`, `κ = 0.926`).
  - **Title/abstract exclusions:** `1,436` (`17` published `< 2013`, `1,419` out of scope).
  - **Full-text retrieval & exclusions (`Fig. 1`, p. 4):** `567` sought for retrieval (mismatch vs. `1,717 − 1,436 = 281`); `287` non-JCR records removed; `181` excluded because OPGs were not the primary dataset plus unreported count for insufficient methodology; `91` final full-text reports assessed (full-text agreement `97.8%`, `κ = 0.872`) -> **`20` included studies** (`14` general tooth `OD/OS/OL` + `6` mesiodens / impacted tooth studies; note `p. 3` line 169 misstates `"26 studies"`).
- **Characteristics of the `20` included studies (`pp. 3–10, 14–16`, `Table 1`):**
  - **Sample sizes (`p. 4`, `Table 1`):** Ranged from **`50` OPGs** (`Lee et al., 2020 [19]`, `Mask R-CNN`, `IoU = 0.877, F1 = 0.875`) to **`4,545` pediatric OPGs** (`Kaya et al., 2022 [20]`, `YOLOv4`, `OD mAP = 0.922`, `OL mAP = 0.889, F1 = 0.911`), with most cohorts containing `500–1,500` OPGs.
  - **Architectures represented (`p. 4`, `Table 1`):** `Faster R-CNN` (`7` studies), `ResNet` (`6`), `VGG-16` (`5`), **`YOLO` (`5` studies: `YOLOv3`, `YOLOv4`, `YOLOv5`, generic YOLO — NO `YOLOv7–v12` or `RT-DETR`)**, `Mask R-CNN` (`3`), `U-Net` (`2`), `Inception v2/v3` (`3`), plus `EfficientDet-D3 + EfficientNet-B3` (`[22]`), `SwinNet` (`[18]`), `DeepLabV3/V3+` (`[9, 26]`), `DenseNet` (`[32]`), `DetectNet` (`[30]`), `AlexNet`, and `SqueezeNet`.
  - **Methodological deficits across primary literature (`pp. 4, 15`):**
    - **Train/Val/Test discipline:** Only **`11 / 20` (`55%`)** separated `TrS / VS / TeS`; **`8 / 20` (`40%`)** omitted an independent validation split; **`9 / 20` (`45%`)** applied data augmentation; **`3 / 20` (`15%`)** used cross-validation.
    - **Public data & external validation:** Only **`1 / 20` (`5%`, `Chandrashekar et al., 2022 [4]`)** used a public dataset, and only **`4 / 20` (`20%`)** used assorted multi-source test sets (`[9, 18, 22, 25]`).
    - **Ground truth (`p. 15`):** **`8 / 20` (`40%`)** relied on a single annotator, `7 / 20` (`35%`) used multiple operators (`2–5`), and `5 / 20` (`25%`) did not report annotator count; `4 / 6` impacted-tooth studies used `CBCT` GT (`[21, 26, 31, 32]`).

## 4. Limitations & Reviewer Methodological Critique (`OS §3.2`)
### 4.1 Author-Stated Limitations (`pp. 14–16`)
1. **No standardized reporting of `mAP / IoU / P / R` or operating thresholds (`pp. 15–16`).**
2. **Prevalence of small/single-institution datasets and reliance on single-operator GT (`pp. 15–16`).**
3. **Clinical application remains premature due to scarce external validation (`p. 16`).**

### 4.2 Reviewer Methodological Critique (`OS §3.2` Audit)
1. **Severe Copy-Paste & Arithmetic Defects in `Fig. 4` Forest Plots & `PRISMA` Flow (`Fig. 1, p. 4`; `Fig. 4, p. 13`):**
   - **Broken `PRISMA` math (`Fig. 1`, p. 4):** `1,717` screened minus `1,436` excluded equals **`281`**, yet the next box states **`567` records sought for retrieval**; full-text exclusions omit the count for `Reason 2`, and `Abstract` (`p. 1`) says `2,207` identified while `Fig. 1` sums to `2,027` (`p. 3` also says `"26 studies"` vs. `20` included).
   - **Copy-pasted `Sensitivity` columns inside `Fig. 4D` and `Fig. 4E` (`p. 13`):** In `Fig. 4E` (`OR`), the entire left-hand numeric table (`0.952`, `0.870`, `0.973`, ..., `"Pooled Sen 0.875"`, `"I-square = 96,1%"`) is a **literal duplicate of `Fig. 4A` (`Sensitivity`)**, completely contradicting the right-hand `DOR` forest plot (`192.30 [49.12–752.88]`). In `Fig. 4D` (`NLR`), the CI column lists Sensitivity CIs (`0.903–0.980`, etc.) instead of `NLR` CIs. In `Fig. 4B` (`Specificity`), `Aljabri` lists an impossible CI (`0.919 [0.982–0.941]`) and `Kuwada` lists an inverted CI (`0.843 [0.843–0.747]`).
2. **Contradictions Between Hierarchical `metandi` (`p. 1, 12`) and `Fig. 4` / `p. 14`, Plus Wrong `r` Interpretation (`p. 1` vs. `p. 12`):**
   - `Abstract` and `p. 12` report pooled `Sensitivity = 0.92`, whereas `Fig. 4A` and `p. 14` report `0.88` (`88%`); `Abstract` claims `r = 0.886` (`"positive correlation"`), whereas `p. 12` prints **`r = -0.886`** and still calls it a *"positive correlation"*.
3. **Topic Mismatch & Unit-of-Analysis Inflation in the `6`-Study Meta-Analysis (`p. 9, 12–13`):**
   - **`Aljabri et al. [32]` studied canine impaction classification**, NOT mesiodens (`Table 1`, p. 9), yet was pooled as a mesiodens study (`pp. 1, 12`).
   - Studies testing multiple architectures on the **exact same images** (`e.g., Kuwada [30]: AlexNet + VGG-16 + DetectNet`, giving `TP+FN = 400` from `50` test images and averaging `S = (0.84 + 0.44 + 0.92)/3 = 0.733`) and training/test pools (`Dai [29]: 145+705 = 850` vs. `TeS = 100`) were stacked into `14` models (`p. 12`), inflating precision and heterogeneity (`I² = 96.1%`).
4. **JCR-Only Bias, `Sept 2023` Cutoff & Favorable Self-Grading (`Table 2`, p. 12):** Restricting retrieval to `JCR` journals (`287` non-JCR excluded) and stopping in `September 2023` excluded all `YOLOv7–v12` and `RT-DETR` studies (`no YOLOv8+ paper included!`). Furthermore, the authors rated their own prior paper (`Bonfanti-Gris et al. 2022 [15]`, `TeS = 300`, single-center web tool with unspecified annotator experience and `OL S/E = 0.500`) as all-green **`Low Risk`** (`Table 2`).

## 5. Relevance to Our Thesis (`STEP-007` Synthesis Links)
- **Quantifies pre-2024 baseline evidence & justifies our 2024–2026 scope (`G1 / G2 / G5`):** Establishes `QUADAS-2` and `GRADE` evidence up to `September 2023` (`40%` single-annotator GT, `55%` missing 3-way splits, `5%` public data use, `0` `YOLOv7–v12` studies), cleanly demarcating why our thesis focuses on `2024–2026` modern architectures (`YOLOv8–v12 / YOLO26 / RT-DETR`) with strict `mAP@0.5:0.95` and external multi-clinic validation (`Mendeley V4` + `Roboflow` + Dhaka cohort).
- **Methodological checklist warning (`G4`):** Demonstrates the critical importance of automating table/plot generation directly from CSVs (preventing `PRISMA` arithmetic mismatches and copy-pasted forest-plot tables) and never stacking multiple correlated models from the same test split without patient/image clustering.
