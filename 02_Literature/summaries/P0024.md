## 1. Problem & Clinical / Technical Motivation
- **Clinical & regional motivation (pp. 1–3, 12):** Publicly accessible, expert-annotated panoramic dental radiograph (`OPG`) datasets from South Asian clinical populations—particularly **Dhaka, Bangladesh**—are scarce, limiting reproducible benchmarking of deep learning object detection and classification models for common dental pathologies.
- **Dataset & benchmark objective (pp. 1–2, 9, 12):** Curate and publicly release the **`Dental OPG X-RAY Dataset`** (`Mendeley Data V4`, `doi:10.17632/c4hhrkxytw.4`) comprising **`232` original panoramic radiographs** (`1,204` bounding-box instances across **6 classes**: `Healthy Teeth`, `Caries`, `Impacted Teeth`, `BDC/BDR` [Broken-Down Crown/Root], `Infection`, and `Fractured Teeth`), enhanced with **`CLAHE`** and benchmarked using **`YOLOv7`**.

## 2. Dataset, Annotators & Splits
- **Clinical provenance (`Specifications Table`, p. 2; `Section 4.1`, p. 4; `Ethics Statement`, p. 13):**
  - **Source clinics:** Collected from **3 private dental/diagnostic centers in Banani and Dhanmondi, Dhaka, Bangladesh**:
    1. `Prescription Point Ltd`
    2. `LabAid Specialized Hospital`
    3. `Ibn Sina Diagnostic and Imaging Center`
  - **Ethical approval (`p. 13`):** Approved by the local Ethics Committee of **Bangladesh Dental College & Hospital** (`Protocol No: MK-198109`).
  - **Public repository (`p. 2, Ref. [1]`):** Mendeley Data V4 (`doi:10.17632/c4hhrkxytw.4`, `https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/c4hhrkxytw/4`, `197 MB` `.zip` containing both an `Object Detection Dataset` folder and a `Classification Dataset` folder, `Fig. 2`, p. 6).
- **Image acquisition method (`Section 4.2`, pp. 2, 7, 9, Table 2):**
  - Radiographs were photographed handheld using a **`Samsung Galaxy A52` (`SM-A525F`)** smartphone camera (`64 MP`, `f/1.8` aperture, `26 mm` focal length, `ISO 50–3200`, auto exposure `1/6000 – 10 s`, center-weighted average metering, no flash).
  - Stored in `.jpg` format with a `16:9` aspect ratio and **median image dimensions of `1176 × 603` pixels** (`pp. 2, 7`).
- **Cohort & class distribution (`Table 1`, p. 4; `Section 4.1`, p. 7):**
  - **Cohort:** **`232` original panoramic radiographs** from male and female patients aged **`10 to 90` years** (children `< 10` years excluded to avoid deciduous/mixed dentition variability, `pp. 2, 4, 7`).
  - **Total bounding-box instances (`p. 7`):** **`1,204` instances** (`mean = 5.19` labeled instances/OPG).
  - **Class breakdown (`Table 1`, p. 4):**

| :---: | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| `1` | **`Healthy Teeth`** | `223` | Male & Female | `10 – 90` years | Intact enamel/crown/root with normal alignment and no decay/infection |

- **Two-tier expert annotation protocol (`Section 4.4`, p. 9, Fig. 6; `Acknowledgments`, p. 13):**
  - Annotated with bounding boxes (classes `0` to `5`) using **`CVAT`** (`Computer Vision Annotation Tool`).
  - **Primary annotator:** Experienced dentist with **`16` years of clinical experience**.
  - **Senior reviewer:** Reviewed and verified (additions, deletions, confirmations) by a senior dentist with **`32` years of clinical experience** (`p. 9`; Prof. Dr. Md. Rafiqur Rahman acknowledged on `p. 13`). Inter-observer agreement (`κ` / `ICC`) is `not reported`.
- **Dataset split & Roboflow augmentation (`Sections 4.1, 4.5`, pp. 2, 6–7, 9, 12, Fig. 2):**
  - The `232` original OPGs were partitioned **`80% / 10% / 10%` (`186` train / `23` valid / `23` test)**, and `Roboflow` generated **3 augmented outputs per training example** (`186 × 3 = 558` training images), yielding **`604` total images** in the split folders (`558` train + `23` valid + `23` test = `604`, `pp. 2, 7, 9`).

## 3. Methodology & Experimental Design
- **Preprocessing & contrast enhancement (`Sections 4.3, 4.5`, pp. 9–10, Fig. 5):**
  - **`CLAHE` (Contrast-Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization):** Applied to all radiographs to enhance local dental/bone contrast (`p. 9, Fig. 5`; exact `clipLimit` and `tileGridSize` values `not reported`).
  - **Roboflow preprocessing & augmentations (`pp. 9, 12`):** Auto-orientation, resize to **`640 × 640` pixels**, horizontal flip, rotation (`-15°` to `+15°`), horizontal and vertical shear (`±10°`), image and bounding-box exposure adjustment (`-10%` to `+10%`), and **Mosaic augmentation** (`Ref. [3]`, `p. 12`).
- **Baseline object detector (`Section 4.6`, p. 12, Table 4, Fig. 7):**
  - Evaluated a **pretrained `YOLOv7`** model over **`100` epochs** (`Fig. 7`, p. 12). Batch size, optimizer, learning rate, compute hardware, and inference latency (`ms/FPS`) are `not reported`.

## 5. Limitations
### 5.1 Author-Stated Limitations (`Limitations`, p. 13)
1. **Small original sample size (`232` OPGs, `p. 13`):** Limits broad generalization without further multi-institutional expansion.
2. **Geographic concentration (`p. 13`):** Collected from only 3 private clinics in Dhaka, Bangladesh.
3. **Exclusion of pediatric patients (`< 10` years, `p. 13`):** Cannot be used directly for primary/early-mixed dentition studies.
4. **Single smartphone camera capture (`p. 13`):** Handheld capture with a single `Samsung Galaxy A52` camera may introduce device-specific quality bias.

### 5.2 Reviewer Methodological Critique (`OS §3.2`)
1. **Handheld smartphone re-photography of X-ray films rather than native digital exports (`pp. 1–2, 7, 9, Table 2`):** Capturing OPGs by photographing X-ray films handheld (`Samsung A52`, median `1176 × 603` pixels) introduces perspective keystoning, non-uniform backlighting, ambient room reflections, and JPEG compression artifacts compared to direct digital `DICOM`/`PNG` exports (`~2500–3000 × 1500` pixels).
2. **Severe minority-class scarcity in the `23`-image test split (`Table 1`, p. 4; `p. 7`):** Because `Fractured Teeth` appears in only `13` of `232` OPGs (`5.6%`) and `Infection` in only `23` of `232` OPGs (`9.9%`), a `10%` test split (`23` OPGs) contains only **`~1–2` test radiographs** with `Fractured Teeth` or `Infection`. Omitting per-class `AP50`, `Precision`, and `Recall` in `Table 4` (`p. 12`) conceals how `YOLOv7` performed on these rare pathology classes vs. the dominant `Healthy Teeth` class (`223/232` OPGs).
3. **No ablation of `CLAHE` vs. raw images and incomplete reporting (`pp. 9, 12`):** Although `CLAHE` is a featured preprocessing step (`Section 4.3`, `Fig. 5`), `clipLimit`/`tileGridSize` parameters are `not reported` and no `Raw vs. CLAHE` detection ablation is conducted; `mAP50-95`, per-class instance counts (out of `1,204`), GPU hardware, and inference `FPS`/`ms` are also `not reported`. *(Note on split arithmetic: although `p. 2` ambiguously states "split the augmented data into train, test, and valid", `Section 4.5` on `p. 9` clarifies that Roboflow generated 3 outputs per training example—i.e., `186 × 3 = 558` train + `23` unaugmented valid + `23` unaugmented test = `604` images from the `232` (`186+23+23`) original OPGs).*

## 6. Relevance to Our Thesis (`STEP-007` Synthesis Links)
- **Directly relevant Bangladeshi clinical OPG dataset (`G1` / `G3`):** Represents an open-access (`CC BY-NC 4.0`, Mendeley `doi:10.17632/c4hhrkxytw.4`) panoramic dental dataset collected from **Dhaka, Bangladesh** (`Banani` & `Dhanmondi`) with two-tier specialist dentist annotations (`16` yrs + `32` yrs experience) across `Caries`, `Impacted Teeth`, `BDC/BDR` (retained roots/broken crowns), `Infection` (periapical lesions), `Fractured Teeth`, and `Healthy Teeth`.
- **Motivation for controlled `CLAHE` parameter ablation (`S7`) and native digital vs. photographed OPG quality analysis (`G3`):** While `P0024` applies `CLAHE` with `YOLOv7` (`mAP50 = 93.54%`), its lack of `CLAHE` parameter specification and absence of a `Raw vs. CLAHE` ablation directly motivate our thesis's systematic `CLAHE` grid/clip-limit ablation (`S7`) and per-class evaluation (`mAP@0.50` and `mAP@0.50:0.95`).
