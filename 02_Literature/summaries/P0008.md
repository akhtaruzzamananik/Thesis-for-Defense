## Verified citation

Oztekin, F., Katar, O., Avci, N., & Konus, F. (2026). Automated Detection and Ordinal Severity Grading of Periapical Lesions on Panoramic Radiographs Using a Lesion-Preserving Tiling and Ordinal-Aware YOLO Framework. *Diagnostics*, 16(17), 2778. https://doi.org/10.3390/diagnostics16172778 (PDF p. 1).

## question/task/modality

- **Modality:** 2D dental panoramic radiographs (`Sirona Orthophos XG`, `235 dpi` JPEGs, `~2444 × 1292` px, pp. 4, 7).
- **Task:** Simultaneous object detection and **3-grade ordinal Periapical Index (`PAI`) severity grading** of apical periodontitis lesions (`PAI 3 < PAI 4 < PAI 5`; `PAI 1–2` treated as healthy at source, `Table 1`, p. 6), benchmarking **six consecutive medium-scale YOLO generations (`YOLOv8m`, `YOLOv9m`, `YOLOv10m`, `YOLO11m`, `YOLO12m`, `YOLO26m`)** on full down-sampled radiographs vs native-resolution `640 × 640` lesion-preserving tiles, and extending `YOLO12m` with an ordinal-aware BCE loss (`β = 0.05`), a stride-4 `P2` small-object head (`160 × 160`), severity class-weighting, test-time augmentation (`TTA`), and a label-free dense sliding-window deployment audit (pp. 1, 4–16).

## data/counts

- **Source cohort & anti-leakage filtering (`pp. 4, 6, 36, Table 1`):**
  - Publicly available Mendeley Data collection (`https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/kx52tk2ddj/3`, Ref `[35]`), originally acquired at Hanoi Medical University (Jan 2016 – Mar 2021) from a parent archive of `16,519` OPGs.
  - **Explicit deduplication of pre-augmented files (`p. 4`):** Because the public Mendeley repository includes scaled, mirrored, rotated, and noise-augmented copies of each radiograph, the authors **discarded all augmented duplicates** and retained only the **`3,924` original panoramic radiographs** containing **`6,741` annotated periapical lesions** (`4,641` native-resolution `640 × 640` lesion-preserving tiles).
- **Class / grade distribution (`Table 1`, p. 6):**
  - `PAI 3` (changes in bone structure with some mineral loss; median box side `53` px, p. 11): `4,277` lesions (`63.45%`)
  - `PAI 4` (periodontitis with well-defined radiolucent area; median box side `75` px): `1,755` lesions (`26.03%`)
  - `PAI 5` (severe periodontitis with exacerbating features; median box side `117` px): `709` lesions (`10.52%`).
- **Small-object scale analysis (`p. 6`):** Median lesion box side is `60` pixels (`IQR 50–77`, `0.114%` of radiograph area); when a full `2444 × 1292` radiograph is down-sampled to `640 × 640`, the median lesion shrinks to `~16` pixels and **`95.7%` of all lesions fall below the `32 × 32` COCO small-object threshold**.

## annotation

- **Source consensus rule (`pp. 4–5`):** Three dentists (`>5` years experience) annotated independently after calibrating on `50` radiographs; a lesion was retained only when `≥2/3` readers' boxes overlapped at **`IoU > 0.80`** (radiographs where all 3 disagreed were discarded at source). Individual reader labels were not distributed in the public dataset.
- **Blinded human reader study (`pp. 17, 27–28, Table 9`):** On a stratified sample of `249` hold-out lesions (`83` per grade), 3 endodontist co-authors (`F.O.`, `N.A.`, `F.K.`) independently graded localized crops, achieving **Quadratic Weighted Kappa (`QWK`) vs reference = `0.704 [0.628, 0.773]`, `0.572 [0.493, 0.649]`, and `0.525 [0.441, 0.605]`** (`Exact agreement = 66.3%, 56.2%, 49.8%`; pairwise inter-reader `QWK = 0.648, 0.509, 0.312`, mean `0.490`), whereas the proposed framework achieved **`QWK = 0.766 [0.700, 0.822]`** and **`73.0%` exact agreement**.

## split

- **Radiograph-level split (`p. 15`):** Strictly partitioned at the **radiograph level** so no two tiles from the same radiograph straddle a split:
  - *Train:* `2,690` radiographs -> `3,195` tiles (`4,623` lesions; `494` `PAI 5`)
  - *Validation:* `673` radiographs -> `780` tiles (`1,133` lesions)
  - *Locked Hold-out Test:* `561` radiographs -> `666` tiles (`985` lesions: `628 PAI 3`, `274 PAI 4`, `83 PAI 5`).
- **Patient-level caveat (`p. 34`):** Public dataset lacks patient identifiers, so patient-level independence across radiographs cannot be verified.

## model

- **6 Benchmarked YOLO Generations (`Table 2`, p. 9):**
  1. `YOLOv8m` (2023): `25.9M` params, `79.3 GFLOPs` (`C2f` backbone, anchor-free decoupled head, NMS)
  2. `YOLOv9m` (2024): `20.2M` params, `77.9 GFLOPs` (`GELAN` + `PGI`, NMS)
  3. `YOLOv10m` (2024): `16.6M` params, `64.5 GFLOPs` (consistent dual assignments, NMS-free)
  4. `YOLO11m` (2024): `20.1M` params, `68.5 GFLOPs` (`C3k2` + `C2PSA` attention, NMS)
  5. `YOLO12m` (2025): `20.2M` params, `68.1 GFLOPs` (attention-centric `R-ELAN`/area-attention backbone, NMS)
  6. `YOLO26m` (2026): `21.9M` params, `75.4 GFLOPs` (end-to-end NMS-free detection head).
- **Proposed `YOLO12m` Extensions (`pp. 5, 8–12`):**
  - *Stride-4 `P2` detection head (`160 × 160` grid):* Built with `C3k2` blocks alongside `P3/P4/P5`, increasing `YOLO12m` to **`20.6M` parameters** (`p. 5`).
  - *Ordinal-aware classification loss (`β = 0.05`, Eqs. 2–4, p. 9):* Softens one-hot targets (`[0.95, 0.05, 0]`, `[0.05, 0.90, 0.05]`, `[0, 0.05, 0.95]`) so off-by-two errors (`PAI 3 <-> PAI 5`) are penalized more heavily than adjacent errors.
  - *Severity class-weighting (`w`) & 3-view Test-Time Augmentation (`TTA`).*

## preprocessing

- **Lesion-preserving tiling (`pp. 6–8, Algorithm 1`):** Greedy horizontal grouping + native-resolution `640 × 640` window cropping without resizing (unless a lesion group exceeds `640` px), guaranteeing `100%` of lesions remain intact and un-duplicated. No `CLAHE` or intensity filtering applied.
- **Label-free inference tiling (`p. 16`):** Dense `640 × 640` sliding window with stride `320` px (`~26.7` windows per radiograph) + radiograph-level class-wise NMS.
- **Training augmentation (`Table 3`, p. 15):** Online only (`HSV s=0.2, v=0.3`, rotation `10°`, scale `0.3`, shift `0.1`, horizontal flip `0.5`, mosaic `1.0`).

## training

- **Configuration (`Table 3`, p. 15):** Ultralytics `8.4.90`, PyTorch `2.5.1`, CUDA `12.1`, **`MuSGD` optimizer**, `lr0 = lrf = 0.01`, momentum `0.9`, weight decay `0.0005`, warmup `3` epochs, batch size `8`, AMP mixed precision, max `500` epochs with validation-loss early stopping (`patience = 10`, terminating at `28–62` epochs), best validation `mAP@0.50` checkpoint retained, random seed `42`.

## metrics/IoU

- **Localization (`pp. 12–15`):** `mAP@0.50` and `mAP@0.50:0.95` (`mAP`).
- **Ordinal & clinical grading (`pp. 12–15`):** Severity-weighted Average Precision (`Sev-wAP`), Quadratic Weighted Kappa (`QWK`), catastrophic off-by-two error rate (`Catas. %`, `|g_hat - g| = 2`), Severity MAE, per-grade AP/Recall, FROC, and Expected Calibration Error (`ECE`), with `95%` radiograph-level cluster bootstrap confidence intervals.

## timing/hardware

- **Hardware (`Table 3`, p. 15):** Single **NVIDIA RTX 4090 GPU (`24 GB`)**.
- **Inference speed (`p. 16`):** Full label-free dense sliding-window scan (`~26.7` windows/OPG) across all `561` test radiographs took `234 s` = **`0.42 s per radiograph` (`~2.38 OPG/s`)** on a shared RTX 4090 (reported as an upper bound). Per-tile single-pass inference latency (`ms/tile`) is `not reported`.

## limitations

- **Author-stated (`pp. 4–5, 15, 25–27, 34–35`):** (1) Spectrum bias: dataset contains only OPGs with `≥1` consensus-verified lesion (`IoU > 0.80`) and zero healthy/lesion-free patients; (2) single-center, single-device (`Sirona Orthophos XG`) origin; (3) no patient identifiers in public release to verify patient-level disjointness; (4) `β`, severity weights, and backbone selection were compared on the hold-out partition (`pp. 15, 34`); (5) annotation-centered tiling inflates metrics unless evaluated with a label-free dense grid and background tiles (`Table 8`); (6) reader study used co-author endodontists rather than external clinicians.

## critique

- **One of the most methodologically exemplary papers in our entire 42-paper corpus (`OS §3.2`):**
  1. Explicitly catches and removes pre-augmented duplicates from a public Mendeley OPG dataset (`https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/kx52tk2ddj/3`) before splitting (`p. 4`) — directly validating our **`RF06` / `STEP-010` deduplication protocol**.
  2. Quantifies why standard `640 × 640` full-OPG resizing hurts small periapical lesions (`95.7%` shrink below `32 × 32` px, `p. 6`) and proves that `YOLO12m > YOLO11m ≈ YOLO26m > YOLOv9m > YOLOv10m > YOLOv8m` on tiled OPGs while `YOLOv11m`/`YOLOv10m` lead on un-tiled full OPGs (`Table 4`, p. 17).
  3. Proves a crucial deployment lesson (`Table 8`, p. 25): models trained only on lesion-cropped tiles fail on whole radiographs (`37.5%` false positives in normal anatomy) unless negative/background tiles are included.

## reuse

- **Cornerstone reference for `RQ1`, `G1` (small-lesion localization), `G4` (split & bootstrap CI rigor), `G5` (pre-augmented public dataset deduplication), and `STEP-010`:**
  - Provides a second major public Mendeley OPG dataset (`https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/kx52tk2ddj/3`, `3,924` original OPGs, `6,741` periapical lesions) and cites a third (`Ref [43]`, `3,673` OPGs, `5,662` lesions, p. 35) for `STEP-010`.
  - Directly informs our multi-generation YOLO discussion (`YOLOv8` through `YOLO26`) and small-object (`P2` head / resolution) analysis.

## conflicts

- Shows that on full down-sampled `640 × 640` radiographs (`Table 4` column 1), **`YOLO11m` (`0.528`) and `YOLOv10m` (`0.517`) actually outperform `YOLO12m` (`0.494`) and `YOLO26m` (`0.496`)**, whereas `YOLO12m` (`0.615`) wins only after native-resolution `640 × 640` tiling! This is a vital nuance for our full-OPG experiments.
