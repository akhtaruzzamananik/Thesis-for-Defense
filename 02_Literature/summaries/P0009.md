## Verified citation

Peng, J., Liu, J., Shen, Y., Yin, M., Liu, C., Zhou, J., Liu, J., Zhang, R., & Hong, Q. (2026). RCTE: A multi-class object detection framework for dental panoramic radiographs. *PLOS One*, 21(10), e0359630. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0359630 (PDF p. 1).

## question/task/modality

- **Modality:** 2D dental panoramic radiographs (`OPGs`, pp. 1, 10–11).
- **Task:** 11-class object detection of dental restorations, pathologies, and anatomical structures (`Filling`, `Crown`, `Root Canal Treatment`, `Periapical lesion`, `Missing teeth`, `Root Piece`, `Caries`, `Impacted tooth`, `Implant`, `Mandibular Canal`, and `Maxillary sinus`), proposing **`RCTE-Net`** (a `YOLOv8n`-based detector with Cross-Scale Channel Transformer `CSCT`, `RepNCSPELAN4`-based Re-parameterized Feature Pyramid Fusion `RPF`, and Multi-Scale Efficient Multi-Scale Attention `MS-EMA`) and benchmarking against **`YOLOv5n`, `YOLOv6n`, `YOLOv8n`, `YOLOv11n`, and `YOLO26n`** (pp. 1, 5–14).

## data/counts

- **Dataset source (`pp. 1, 10–11, Table 1`):** Public third-party Roboflow dataset (`Tooth Filling Detection`: `https://universe.roboflow.com/pidray-8gl3p/tooth-filling-detection/dataset/1` on p. 1; `/dataset/2` on p. 10) comprising **`8,124` de-identified OPG images** across `11` categories.
- **Provenance & count gaps (`pp. 1, 10–11`):** Authors did not collect the data and report no institutional origin, X-ray equipment, patient count, per-class instance counts, or audit for pre-augmented Roboflow duplicates (`not reported`).

## annotation

- **Format (`p. 11`):** Bounding boxes converted into `YOLO` format across `11` classes.
- **Annotator credentials, consensus protocol, and inter-rater agreement (`kappa`):** **`not reported`** (unverified third-party Roboflow labels, p. 10).

## split

- **Allocation (`p. 11, Table 1`):** `8:1:1` split into **`6,498` training**, **`813` validation**, and **`813` test** images. Patient-level grouping and deduplication checks across splits are `not reported`.

## model

- **Proposed `RCTE-Net` (`pp. 5–10, 14, Table 4`):** Built on `YOLOv8n` (`P3/P4/P5` from backbone layers `4/6/9`, channels `64/128/256`) + `CSCT` cross-scale channel transformer + `RPF` (`RepNCSPELAN4` bidirectional neck fusion) + `MS-EMA` (`3`-scale feature recalibration before the original YOLOv8 detection head); **`5.46M` parameters**.
- **Compared baselines (`Table 4`, p. 14):** `YOLOv5n` (`2.18M`), `YOLOv6n` (`4.16M`), `YOLOv8n` (`2.69M`), `YOLOv11n` (`2.58M`), and `YOLO26n` (`2.50M`).
- **Theoretical complexity (`GFLOPs`) and model file size (`MB`):** Mentioned as evaluation metrics on p. 12–13, but numeric values are **`not reported`** in `Tables 4` or `6`.

## preprocessing

- **Operations (`pp. 11–13, Table 3`):** Resizing to `640 × 640` and standard normalization before inference. No `CLAHE`, contrast enhancement, or tiling reported.

## training

- **Hyperparameters (`p. 11–12, Table 3`):** `100` epochs, batch size `16`, input size `640 × 640`, `SGD` optimizer, initial learning rate `0.01`, final learning rate factor `0.01`, momentum `0.937`, weight decay `0.0005`, `workers = 0`, `AMP = False`, evaluation `IoU threshold = 0.7`, `max_det = 300`.

## metrics/IoU

- **Reported metrics (`pp. 11–14, Tables 4–7`):** Precision (`P`), Recall (`R`), F1-score, `mAP50` (`IoU = 0.50`), `mAP75` (`IoU = 0.75`), `mAP50–95` (`IoU = 0.50:0.05:0.95`), parameters (`M`), and pure inference `FPS` (`FPS_infer = 1 / T_infer`, Eq. 14, p. 13; `FPS_all` including pre/post-processing defined in Eq. 13 but numeric `T_pre`, `T_post`, and `FPS_all` are `not reported`).

## timing/hardware

- **Hardware & software (`p. 11, Table 2`):** Windows 11 workstation with **AMD Ryzen 9 8945HX CPU** and **NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5060 Laptop GPU (`8 GB`)**, Python `3.10.0`, PyTorch `2.7.1 + CUDA 12.8`.
- **Pure Inference `FPS` (`Table 4`, p. 14 & `Table 6`, p. 20):**
  - `YOLOv8n`: **`478.78 FPS`** (`~2.09 ms`)
  - `YOLO26n`: **`422.98 FPS`** (`~2.36 ms`)
  - `YOLOv11n`: **`405.16 FPS`** (`~2.47 ms`)

## limitations

- **Author-stated (`p. 22`):** (1) Lacks external multi-center validation; (2) 2D panoramic projection limits 3D structural assessment (e.g., mandibular canal); (3) poor detection of small, low-contrast categories (`Caries` and `Periapical lesion`, `pp. 17–18, 22`); (4) lacks clinical deployment evaluation.

## critique

- **Crucial empirical evidence for three of our core thesis arguments (`OS §3.2`):**
  1. **Why uncurated 11-class Roboflow mirrors fail on radiolucent pathologies (`UD02`, `UD04`, `G1`):** While `RCTE-Net` achieves `mAP50 = 0.9513` on `Impacted tooth`, `0.9203` on `Implant`, `0.7964` on `Crown`, and `0.6684` on `Filling`, it collapses on **`Periapical lesion` (`Recall = 6.85%`, `mAP50 = 0.0726`, `mAP75 = 0.0063`)** and **`Caries` (`Recall = 19.98%`, `mAP50 = 0.2497`)** when `11` heterogeneous classes (mixing huge `Maxillary sinus` / `Mandibular Canal` boxes with tiny radiolucent lesions) are trained at `640 × 640` on unverified Roboflow annotations!
  2. **Nano-scale YOLO ordering (`RQ1`):** Shows that at the `nano` scale (`~2.2–2.7M` params) on `640 × 640` full OPGs, `YOLOv5n` (`mAP50 = 0.5570`) and `YOLOv8n` (`0.5450`) slightly exceed vanilla `YOLOv11n` (`0.5330`) and `YOLO26n` (`0.5206`, where NMS-free `YOLO26n` has high precision `0.7072` but low recall `0.4707`).
  3. **Incomplete metric reporting (`G6`):** Explicitly defines `GFLOPs`, `model file size`, `T_pre`, `T_post`, and `FPS_all` (Eq. 13, pp. 12–13) in the Methods section, but omits all of them from the Results tables (`Tables 4 & 6`), reporting only `FPS_infer`.

## reuse

- Direct `Group A` benchmark for `RQ1` (`YOLOv5n/v6n/v8n/v11n/26n` vs attention-neck `RCTE-Net`), `UD02` (pitfalls of unverified Roboflow datasets (`pidray-8gl3p/tooth-filling-detection`)), and `UD04` (why mixing poorly annotated sinus/canal/missing-tooth boxes with tiny radiolucent lesions degrades detector convergence).

## conflicts

- Contrast with `P0008` (`Oztekin 2026`): `P0008` achieves `mAP50 = 0.664` on periapical lesions by using specialist-consensus (`IoU > 0.80`) annotations and `640 × 640` native-resolution tiling, whereas `P0009` gets `mAP50 = 0.0726` on periapical lesions when down-sampling full OPGs to `640 × 640` on noisy Roboflow labels.

## unresolved fields

- Per-class instance counts, patient count, Roboflow annotation provenance/kappa, `GFLOPs`, model weight size (`MB`), and end-to-end `FPS_all` (`T_pre + T_infer + T_post`) are `not reported`.
