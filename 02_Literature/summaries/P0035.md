## 1. Problem & Clinical / Technical Motivation
- **Clinical & technical problem (`pp. 1–3`):** Impacted teeth are frequently overlapped or malpositioned inside the alveolar bone, making manual detection on panoramic radiographs (`OPGs`) subjective and time-consuming. Prior automated studies largely focused on bounding-box region detectors (`YOLO`, `Faster R-CNN`, `EfficientDet`) rather than fine pixel-level delineation of crown/root contours.
- **Objective (`pp. 1, 3`):** Systematically evaluate **`4` pixel-based semantic segmentation decoders (`U-Net`, `PSPNet`, `FPN`, `LinkNet`) × `10` ImageNet-pretrained backbones (`40` configurations total)** for binary segmentation of impacted teeth (`impacted tooth` vs. `background`) on OPGs.

---

## 2. Dataset, Annotation & Experimental Setup (`pp. 3–4, 6–7`, `Tables 1–3`)
- **Combined Multi-Source Cohort (`407` OPGs in `Table 1`, p. 3 & `p. 4`):**
  1. **Local clinic dataset (`Imak et al., 2023 [26]`):** **`304`** OPGs (`540 × 380 px, .png`) — all `304` used.
  2. **Open-access repository (`Román et al., 2021 [27]`):** `598` OPGs (`2041 × 1024 px, .jpg`) — **`53`** selected.
  3. **Dental imaging laboratory (`Abdi et al., 2015 [28]`):** `116` OPGs (`3100 × 1300 px, .png`) — **`50`** selected (`304 + 53 + 50 = 407` total; **identical `407`-OPG [`325/21/61`] impacted-tooth cohort used in `Küçük et al., 2025 [P0018]`** via shared co-authors A. Çelebi & M. Türkoğlu). *(Note: `Section III.B`, p. 7 misstates `413` OPGs.)*
- **Annotation (`p. 4`):** Binary pixel masks (`white = impacted`, `black = background`) annotated in **`MATLAB Image Labeler`** by **2 oral and maxillofacial experts (`average 10 years` clinical experience)** using a double-review consensus protocol (`no formal Cohen's κ or inter-rater Dice reported`).
- **Fixed Single Split (`80 / 5 / 15`, `p. 4` vs. `p. 7`):** **`325` train / `21` validation / `61` test** (`p. 4`; `p. 7` states `326 / 20 / 61`). No data augmentation, k-fold CV, or training input resolution resizing is reported.
- **Training Configuration (`Table 3`, p. 7):** `NVIDIA RTX 6000` GPU, Python, `ImageNet` pretraining, **`Adam` optimizer**, **`LR = 0.001`**, **`300` epochs** (`Fig. 8` plots `350` epochs), **`Sigmoid`** output activation (`loss function, batch size, and inference ms/OPG not reported`).

## 4. Limitations & Reviewer Methodological Critique (`OS §3.2`)
### 4.1 Author-Stated Limitations (`Sections IV.C, V`, pp. 12–13)
1. **Rare-position & boundary failures (`Fig. 9`, pp. 12–13):** Models failed on atypically located (midline) impacted teeth and occasionally misclassified non-impacted erupting teeth (`Example 3`, `Fig. 9`, where `U-Net` & `LinkNet` missed both midline teeth completely).
2. **Lack of `Mask R-CNN`, `SegFormer`, and Transformer baselines, plus single-split `407`-OPG sample (`p. 13`).**

### 4.2 Reviewer Methodological Critique (`OS §3.2` Audit)
1. **Multiple Internal Numerical & Metadata Contradictions Across Tables/Text:**
   - **Miscalculated Means in `Table 8` (`p. 11`):** `4 / 10` backbone averages in `Table 8` contain arithmetic errors relative to `Tables 4–7`: `EfficientNetB7` (`84.41%` true vs. `84.16%` printed), `SEResNeXt101` (`80.64%` vs. `80.14%`), `MobileNet` (`79.24%` vs. `79.48%`), and `VGG16` (`79.09%` vs. `79.34%`).
   - **Dataset & split contradictions (`pp. 1, 4, 7`):** Abstract and `p. 4` state **`407` OPGs** (`325 / 21 / 61`), whereas `Section III.B` (`p. 7`) states **`413` OPGs** with splits `326 / 20 / 61` (`= 407 != 413`).
   - **`Table 2` (`p. 6`) mismatches `Tables 4–8` (`pp. 7–11`):** `Table 2` describes **`9` backbones** using `-50` layer variants (`ResNet50, ResNeXt50, SEResNeXt50`, omitting `SEResNet`) with incorrect metadata (`InceptionV3 Params = 237M` [actually `~23.8M`]; `SEResNeXt50 Dept = 10`), whereas `Tables 4–8` evaluate **`10` backbones** using **`101`-layer variants** (`ResNet101`, `ResNeXt101`, `SEResNet101`, `SEResNeXt101`).
   - **`Fig. 8` (`p. 12`) & text discrepancies:** `Fig. 8` curves plot `"Train"` and `"Test"` (not `"Validation"`) over `350` epochs (vs. `300` in `Table 3`), showing noisy validation/test `IoU` hovering near `0.78–0.83`; `p. 13` claims a `"2% higher IoU"` over `Imak et al. [26]` (`84.48%`) when `85.29% − 84.48% = +0.81%`; `p. 10` claims `VGG16` `"outperformed"` others in `Table 7` despite ranking 8th/10th (`79.08%`).
2. **Unsubstantiated Superiority & Real-Time Claims:**
   - Abstract (`p. 1`) claims *"new insights into the advantages of pixel-based approaches over region-based methods"*, yet **zero object detectors (`YOLO`, `RT-DETR`, `Faster R-CNN`, `Mask R-CNN`) were trained in this study** — whereas `Küçük et al., 2025 [P0018]` achieved `mAP@0.5 = 98.3%, F1 = 96.0%` using `YOLOv8L + RT-DETR + WBF` on the **exact same `407`-OPG dataset (`325/21/61`)**.
   - `Conclusion` (`p. 13`) claims `"real-time dental images quickly"`, without reporting parameters, FLOPs, or `ms/OPG` runtime for heavy `EfficientNetB7 (66M)`.
3. **Class-Agnostic Binary Formulation & Evaluation Gaps (`G1 / G3 / G5`):** Binary semantic segmentation cannot separate adjacent impacted teeth into distinct instances (`Fig. 4 Example 3`), cannot perform `FDI` tooth numbering or multi-class pathology detection, uses a single `61`-image test split without k-fold CV or external validation, and omits inter-rater reliability ($\kappa / \text{DSC}$).

## 5. Relevance to Our Thesis (`STEP-007` Synthesis Links)
- **Direct Cross-Paradigm Benchmark Against `P0018` on the Same `407`-OPG Dataset (`G1 / G2`):** Pairs directly with `Küçük et al., 2025 [P0018]` (`YOLOv8L + RT-DETR + WBF` box detection: `mAP@0.5 = 98.3%, F1 = 96.0%`) vs. `Durmuş et al., 2025 [P0035]` (`U-Net + EfficientNetB7` binary pixel segmentation: `IoU = 85.29%, F1 = 92.02%`), illustrating why **instance-level detection/segmentation (`YOLO / RT-DETR`) is strictly required** for separating adjacent teeth and assigning class/FDI labels in multi-pathology OPG screening (`G3`).
- **Backbone scaling observation (`Table 8`):** Demonstrates across 4 segmentation decoders that compound-scaled `EfficientNetB7` (`84.41%` true mean `IoU`) consistently outperforms older `ResNet101` (`78.79%`) and `VGG16` (`79.09%`) encoders.
