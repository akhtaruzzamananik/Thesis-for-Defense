## 1. Problem & Clinical / Technical Motivation
- **Clinical motivation (pp. 1–2, 9):** Patients undergoing head and neck radiotherapy or cardiac surgery require mandatory pre-treatment dental screening on 2D panoramic radiographs (`OPGs`) to identify and extract compromised teeth (`periapical lesions`, `root fragments`, `carious lesions`, `periodontal bone loss`, `furcation lesions`) before radiation therapy, preventing post-radiation extractions that trigger osteoradionecrosis (`ORN`) or infective endocarditis.
- **Technical problem (pp. 2–3, 8–9):** While commercial tools (`CranioCatch`, `DiagnoCat`, `VelmeniAI`, `Denti.AI`) and academic detectors perform well on internal datasets or high-contrast metallic objects (`implants`, `endodontic treatments`, `surgical plates`), performance degrades under multi-center external domain shift and on subtle radiolucent/diffuse pathologies (`periapical lesions`, `caries`, `alveolar bone loss`, `furcation involvement`).
- **Objective (pp. 2–3):** Develop and validate a **`YOLOv8`** object detector at **`1024 × 1024`** resolution on **`1,628` internal OPGs** and **`180` multi-center external OPGs** across **`13` dental pathology and treatment classes** (foundational study to `P0015` [`Eftimie et al., 2025, MethodsX`], which later released this exact `UMFIH + External` cohort on Zenodo `doi:10.5281/zenodo.15487429`).

## 2. Dataset, Annotators & Splits
- **Cohort & acquisition (`Sections 2.1, 2.3`, pp. 2–3, 10):**
  - **Internal dataset (`1,628` OPGs):** Retrospectively collected (`April 2021 – December 2023`) at the Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery and Radiology, Iuliu Hațieganu University of Medicine and Pharmacy (`UMFIH`), Cluj-Napoca, Romania (Ethics approval `117/04.06.2024`), acquired on a **`Vatech PCH-2500`** panoramic unit (`.jpeg` format).
  - **Internal split (`Section 2.3`, p. 3):**
    - **Training set:** `1,375` OPGs (`84.5%`)
    - **Validation set:** `153` OPGs (`9.4%`)
    - **Internal testing set:** `100` OPGs (`6.1%`, containing **`1,369` annotated instances**, `Table 2`, p. 3)
  - **Multi-center external validation set (`Sections 2.1, 2.3`, pp. 2–4):** **`180` OPGs** acquired from multiple independent medical centers in Cluj-Napoca, Romania, containing **`2,980` annotated instances** (`Table 3`, p. 4).
  - **Inclusion / Exclusion criteria (`p. 2`):** Included adult permanent-dentition OPGs of diagnostic quality (Importantly, images with prosthetic/metal artifacts and superpositions were **retained**, `p. 7`); excluded large cysts, tumors, or severe technique errors impedirg radiological interpretation.
- **Annotation protocol & observer calibration (`Section 2.2`, p. 3):**
  - Annotated with axis-aligned bounding boxes in **`Makesense.ai v1.11.0`** (`YOLO` format) by **3 calibrated researchers (`S.M.`, `M.H.`, `R.E.`)**, achieving an inter-observer **`ICC = 0.91`** (`p. 3`).
  - **13 Evaluated Classes in `Tables 2–3` (`pp. 3–4`):**
    1. `IMP` — Dental implant (`25` internal test / `16` external instances)
    2. `PRR` — Prosthetic restoration (`89` internal / `206` external)
    3. `OBT` — Dental filling / obturation (`391` internal / `897` external)
    4. `END` — Root canal / endodontic treatment (`267` internal / `528` external)
    5. `CAR` — Carious lesion (`141` internal / `296` external)
    6. `BON` — Alveolar bone loss (`118` internal / `680` external)
    7. `IMT` — Impacted tooth (`58` internal / `111` external)
    8. `API` — Periapical lesion (`80` internal / `106` external)
    9. `ROT` — Root fragment (`33` internal / `93` external)
    10. `FUR` — Furcation lesion (`18` internal / `19` external)
    11. `APS` — Apical surgery (`4` internal / `17` external)
    12. `ORD` — Orthodontic treatment / brackets (`25` internal / `6` external)
    13. `SRD` — Surgical device (`osteosynthesis plates`, `TADs`; `120` internal / `5` external)
    - *(Note: `Section 2.2` on `p. 3` mentions 12 classes including `root resorption`, whereas `Tables 2–3` and `Figs. 1–2, A1–A2` evaluate the `13` classes above).*

## 3. Methodology & Experimental Design
- **Model architecture & training setup (`Section 2.3`, p. 3; `Section 4`, p. 7):**
  - **Model:** **`YOLOv8`** (`CSPDarknet` backbone, anchor-free decoupled head with `CIoU + DFL` box loss and `BCE` classification loss, `p. 7`). Exact variant size (`n/s/m/l/x`) is `not reported` in `P0023` *(later identified as `YOLOv8x` in the authors' follow-up paper `P0015`, `Eftimie et al., 2025`)*.
  - **Input image size:** Resized to **`1024 × 1024` pixels** (`p. 3`).
  - **Hyperparameters (`p. 3`):** Trained for **`75` epochs**, `batch size = 8`, `EarlyStopping patience = 5` epochs (restoring best weights). Optimizer, learning rate, and data augmentations are `not reported`.
  - **Hardware & runtime latency (`FPS` / `ms`):** `not reported` in `P0023`.

## 5. Limitations
### 5.1 Author-Stated Limitations (`Section 4.6`, p. 9)
1. **Severe class imbalance & rare-class underfitting (`p. 9`):** Rare classes such as `Apical Surgery (APS)` (`4` internal test instances) and `Furcation Lesion (FUR)` (`18` instances) failed (`AP@0.5 = 0.000` and `0.355`), while `Carious Lesion (CAR)` was frequently confused with or overshadowed by `Dental Filling (OBT)` (`p. 9`).
2. **Inadequacy of axis-aligned bounding boxes for `Alveolar Bone Loss (BON)` (`pp. 8–9`):** `BON` achieved very low recall (`0.169` internal, `0.107` external; `AP@0.5 = 0.165 / 0.154`) because local bounding boxes lack broader periodontal/tooth context, motivating future polygon/keypoint or tooth-level staging strategies (`pp. 8–9`).
3. **External domain shift across centers (`pp. 1, 4, 7–9`):** Overall `mAP@0.50` dropped from `0.600` (internal) to `0.451` (external), and `Periapical Lesion (API)` `AP@0.50` dropped from `0.547` to `0.315` (`P = 0.367, R = 0.396`).

### 5.2 Reviewer Methodological Critique (`OS §3.2`)
1. **Metric terminology & reporting errors in `Section 3` (`p. 3`) vs. `Appendix A` (`pp. 11–12`):**
   - On `p. 3` (lines 186–190), the authors write *"recall = 0.657 for mAP@50"* (internal) and *"recall = 0.451 for mAP@50"* (external), conflating `Recall` with `mAP@0.50`. Inspection of `Figure A2(b)` (`p. 12`) confirms that **`0.451` is the exact 13-class `mAP@0.5`** on the external set, while `Figure A2(a)` (`p. 11`) shows **`0.600 mAP@0.5`** on the internal test set (`0.657` likely came from the `153`-image validation set or from excluding `APS`).
   - In `Table 1` (`p. 3`, line 184), `TP`, `FP`, and `FN` are misdefined as `"rates"` rather than instance counts, and in `Table 3` (`p. 4`), `SRD` (`5` external instances) is reported with `Precision = 1, Recall = 0` (`AP@0.5 = 0.000` in `Fig. A2b`), which is Ultralytics' default `P = 1.0` convention when `TP = 0, FP = 0`.
2. **Strict `EarlyStopping patience = 5` at `75` epochs (`p. 3`) & single-model evaluation:** Setting `patience = 5` epochs on a complex 13-class `1024 × 1024` detection task risks premature termination before hard radiolucent classes (`API`, `CAR`, `FUR`, `BON`) converge. Moreover, `P0023` evaluates only a single unspecified `YOLOv8` variant without `mAP@0.50:0.95` or comparator architectures (later addressed in their follow-up `P0015` [`Eftimie et al., 2025`]).
3. **Missing hardware and runtime benchmarks (`G6`):** No GPU/CPU specs, model parameter count, or inference time (`ms/FPS`) are reported (`not reported`).

## 6. Relevance to Our Thesis (`STEP-007` Synthesis Links)
- **Complete per-class `AP@0.50` companion to `P0015` (`Eftimie et al., 2025`, open Zenodo `UMFIH` dataset):** Because `P0015` did not tabulate per-class `AP` for its 12/13 classes, `P0023`'s `Tables 2–3` and `Figure A2(a,b)` (`pp. 3–4, 11–12`) provide the exact class-by-class internal (`100` OPGs) and multi-center external (`180` OPGs) `Precision`, `Recall`, and `AP@0.50` baseline on the public `UMFIH` dataset (`doi:10.5281/zenodo.15487429`)!
- **Direct motivation for our small-lesion (`API`, `CAR`) and periodontal context (`BON`) improvements (`G2` / `G3`):** Highlights why standard full-OPG `YOLOv8` fails on `Periapical Lesions` (`external AP@0.5 = 0.315, R = 0.396`), `Caries` (`0.373`), `Furcation Lesions` (`0.131`), and `Bone Loss` (`0.154`), directly motivating our thesis's contrast enhancement, high-resolution tiling, and tooth-context association pipeline (`S7`–`S15`).
