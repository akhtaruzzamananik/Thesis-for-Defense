## Verified citation

Khurshid, Z., Waqas, M., Hasan, S., Nasir, G., Shire, M. A., Faheemuddin, M., Silkosessak, O. C.-U.-D., Trachoo, V., Alharbi, A. S., & Porntaveetus, T. (2026). A Multinational Study of Orientation-Aware Deep Learning for Automated Detection of Retained Roots and Periodontal Bone Loss. *International Dental Journal*, 76, 111200. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.identj.2026.111200 (PDF p. 1).

## question/task/modality

- **Modality:** 2D multinational panoramic dental radiographs (`OPGs`, pp. 1, 3–4).
- **Task:** Automated 7-class object detection of **Retained Roots (`RR`, 4 classes)** and **Periodontally Compromised Teeth (`PCT`, 3 classes)** (`Table 1`, p. 4), systematically comparing **Oriented Bounding Box (`OBB`, 4-point polygon `(x1,y1..x4,y4)`)** vs **Axis-Aligned Bounding Box (`AABB`, area-shrinkage-corrected `(x,y,w,h)`)** annotation strategies across CNN (`YOLOv11`) and Transformer (`RT-DETR`) detectors (pp. 1, 4–7).
- **7 Clinical Classes (`Table 1`, p. 4):**
  - `class_0: Retained root clinical` (broken crown with root fragment exposed in oral cavity; non-surgical extraction)
  - `class_1: Retained root soft tissue` (root fragment in soft tissue, not clinically visible; surgical extraction)
  - `class_2: Retained root` (root fragment in bone with overlying soft tissue; surgical extraction)
  - `class_3: Retained root bone` (completely embedded in alveolar bone with `≥2 mm` radiographic bone coverage from crest; not indicated for extraction)
  - `class_4: PCT mild` (alveolar bone loss `<15%` of root length)
  - `class_5: PCT moderate` (bone loss `15%–30%` of root length)
  - `class_6: PCT severe` (bone loss `>30%` of root length, hopeless prognosis).

## data/counts

- **Source cohort (`pp. 1, 4, 6, 12`):** **`2,405` original multinational OPGs** assembled from three populations:
  - `837` OPGs from Liaquat College of Medicine and Dentistry (`LCMD`), Karachi, Pakistan;
  - `568` OPGs from Chulalongkorn University, Bangkok, Thailand `[20]`;
  - `1,000` OPGs from the public Tufts Dental Database, USA `[21]`.
- **Inclusion/exclusion (`p. 4`):** Permanent dentition, ages `12–75` years, full maxillary/mandibular arch coverage; excluded mixed dentition, severe motion/noise artifacts, or major metallic prostheses obscuring roots (minor metallic restorations retained, p. 10).
- **Post-augmentation count (`pp. 1, 6`):** Selective offline augmentation expanded `2,405` original OPGs to **`4,768` images** (`519` validation instances across `477` val images; `537` test instances across `477` test images, Tables 3–4, p. 8).
- **Public availability (`p. 12`):** Annotated dataset and code publicly released on **Mendeley Data V2: `doi:10.17632/y7dkrpv7g9.2`** (`https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/y7dkrpv7g9/2`, Ethics Ref `HREC-DCU 2024-093`).

## annotation

- **Tool & protocol (`pp. 4–5, 10`):** Annotated in `CVAT` with OBBs initially by one investigator (`G.N.`), then reviewed and verified via consensus with two senior prosthodontic specialists (`Z.K.` and `M.F.`, `>10` years clinical experience) following a pre-annotation calibration session.
- **AABB conversion (`pp. 5–6, Fig. 3`):** Each OBB was enclosed in its minimum bounding rectangle and rescaled by the square root of the area ratio `S = A_{OBB} / A_{AABB}` (`w' = w * sqrt(S)`, `h' = h * sqrt(S)`) to reduce excess background.
- **Inter-rater / intra-rater agreement (`kappa`):** **`not reported`** (`not evaluated`, explicitly acknowledged as a limitation on pp. 5 and 10).

## split

- **Allocation (`p. 6`):** `80:10:10` iterative stratified split (fixed random seed) on the **`4,768` augmented images** -> `3,814` training, `477` validation, and `477` testing images.
- **Leakage & grouping audit (`p. 10, lines 1232–1253`):** Authors explicitly state two major partitioning caveats: (1) **data augmentation was performed prior to the train-validation-test split**, so augmented variants of the same OPG may have crossed partitions, and (2) due to strict anonymization, **patient-level disjointness could not be verified**.

## model

- **Evaluated architectures (`pp. 6–8, Table 2`):** Implemented in Ultralytics `8.3.191` / PyTorch `2.6.0`:
  - `M1`: `YOLOv11-OBB-nano` (OBB)
  - `M2`: `YOLOv11-OBB-small` (OBB)
  - `M3`: `YOLOv11-nano` (AABB)
  - `M4`: `YOLOv11-small` (AABB)
  - `M5`: `RT-DETR-large` (AABB)
  - `M6`: `RT-DETR-xlarge` (AABB — overfit within first 10 epochs, p. 7).
  - Exact parameter counts (`M`) and `GFLOPs`: `not reported`.

## preprocessing

- **Standardization (`p. 4`):** Metadata stripping, resizing to `1024 × 1024` (`p. 7`), intensity normalization, and orientation alignment (no CLAHE reported).
- **Augmentation (`pp. 5–7`):**
  - *Offline selective augmentation (`pp. 5–6`):* Horizontal flipping, minor Gaussian blurring, random brightness/contrast adjustment, affine transformations, and elastic deformations (`2,405 -> 4,768` images).
  - *Online training augmentation (`p. 7`):* Mosaic, horizontal flip (`50%`), and HSV perturbations (`hue = 0.015`, `saturation = 0.7`, `brightness = 0.4`).

## training

- **Hyperparameters (`p. 7`):** `500` max epochs with early stopping (`patience = 100`), batch size `4`, image size `1024`, learning rate `0.01` with `3`-epoch linear warmup, momentum `0.937`, weight decay `0.0005`, deterministic random seed `0`.

## metrics/IoU

- **Reported metrics (`pp. 7–9, Tables 2–4`):** Precision (`P`), Recall (`R`), `mAP@0.5` (labeled `Accuracy (mAP)` in Table 2), `mAP@0.5:0.95` (`80.6%` reported in text for `M2`, p. 9), and average per-image inference latency (`ms`, averaged over 10 runs, p. 7).

## timing/hardware

- **Hardware (`p. 7`):** **NVIDIA Tesla P100 GPU (`16 GB`)** with CUDA acceleration.
- **Training time per epoch (`p. 10`):** `YOLOv11-nano`: `2.52 s/epoch`; `YOLOv11-small`: `4.15 s/epoch`; `RT-DETR-large`: `~10 min/epoch`.
- **Inference latency & FPS (`Table 2`, p. 8 & `p. 10`):**
  - `M1 (YOLOv11-OBB-nano)`: **`6.90 ms/image`** (`~144.9 FPS`)
  - `M2 (YOLOv11-OBB-small)`: **`14.2 ms/image`** (`~70.4 FPS`, explicitly cited as exceeding the `30 FPS` real-time threshold, p. 10)
  - `M3 (YOLOv11-nano, AABB)`: **`6.60 ms/image`** (`~151.5 FPS`)
  - `M4 (YOLOv11-small, AABB)`: **`14.4 ms/image`** (`~69.4 FPS`)
  - `M5 (RT-DETR-large, AABB)`: **`44.6 ms/image`** (`~22.4 FPS`)
  - `M6 (RT-DETR-xlarge, AABB)`: **`80.1 ms/image`** (`~12.5 FPS`).

## limitations

- **Author-stated (`pp. 5, 9–12`):** (1) **Pre-split data augmentation** (`2,405 -> 4,768` before the `80:10:10` split), creating potential optimistic bias across partitions (`p. 10`); (2) **unverified patient-level disjointness** due to anonymization (`p. 10`); (3) **no formal inter-rater or intra-rater kappa** evaluated (`pp. 5, 10`); (4) poor test recall (`0.506`) on `Retained root clinical` because clinical oral mucosal exposure cannot be reliably determined from 2D OPG contrast alone (`p. 9`); (5) AABB labels were mathematically shrunk from OBBs rather than independently drawn (`p. 11`); (6) radiographic PCT grading omits clinical probing depth/CAL (`pp. 5, 11–12`).

## critique

- **High-value multi-faceted study for our thesis:**
  1. Direct head-to-head benchmark of **`YOLOv11` vs `RT-DETR`** at `1024 × 1024` on an NVIDIA P100 GPU showing that `YOLOv11-small` is **`3.1×` to `5.6×` faster at inference** (`14.2 ms` vs `44.6–80.1 ms`), **`~145×` faster per training epoch** (`4.15 s` vs `~10 min`), and achieves **higher test mAP** (`0.833` vs `0.745–0.767`) where `RT-DETR-xlarge` overfits within 10 epochs (`p. 7, 10`).
  2. Transparently admits **pre-split augmentation leakage** (`p. 10`), providing textbook evidence for **Gap `G5` / `RF06`** (why our pipeline strictly splits raw radiographs *before* any augmentation and hashes images across splits).
  3. Demonstrates that `class_0 (Retained root clinical)` has low recall (`0.506`) because oral cavity soft-tissue exposure is not radiographically distinct on 2D OPG (`p. 9`), directly supporting **`UD04` (OPG-visible class ontology)**.
  4. Releases a public multinational Mendeley dataset (`doi:10.17632/y7dkrpv7g9.2`, `2,405` base OPGs from Pakistan, Thailand, and USA Tufts) to inspect during **`STEP-010`** (where we can filter to the `2,405` unaugmented originals!).

## reuse

- Primary reference for: (1) `RQ1` (`YOLOv11` vs Transformer `RT-DETR` accuracy/latency trade-off and `30 FPS` real-time threshold on P100 GPU), (2) `UD04` (collapsing soft-tissue/clinical retained-root subtypes into a single OPG-visible `Retained Root` class or radiographic bone-loss severity), (3) `G5`/`RF06` (author-confirmed pre-split augmentation bias), and (4) `STEP-010` candidate public dataset (`doi:10.17632/y7dkrpv7g9.2`).

## conflicts

- None; directly reinforces both our architecture choice (`YOLO` over heavy `DETR` on limited data) and our anti-leakage protocol (`RF06`).
