## 1. Problem & Clinical / Technical Motivation
- **Registration & Framework (`pp. 1, 3, 5`):** Registered in **`PROSPERO` (`CRD42024607099`)** and structured around the **PRISMA statement (`[9, 10]`)** combined with **Kitchenham** systematic-review and **Basili Goal–Question–Metric (`GQM`)** guidelines.
- **Objective (`p. 29`):** Systematically synthesize recent (`2019–August 2024` / `2018–2024`) deep learning methods for automated dental lesion detection across radiographic and dental imaging modalities, structured around four research questions (`RQ1–RQ4`): target dental lesion types (`RQ1`), deep learning models (`RQ2`), preprocessing and data augmentation techniques (`RQ3`), and dataset/detection challenges with proposed solutions (`RQ4`).

## 4. Limitations & Reviewer Methodological Critique (`OS §3.2`)

### 4.1 Author-Stated Limitations & Validity Threats (`Section 4.2, pp. 29–30`)
1. **Explicit Scope Boundaries (`p. 29`, 5 bullets):**
   - **Date window:** Covered primary studies from `2019 to August 2024` (note internal inconsistency with `2018–2024` on `p. 5`).
   - **Publication types:** Restricted to peer-reviewed journals and conference/workshop/symposium proceedings; excluded secondary systematic reviews, gray literature, and survey studies.
   - **Automated search filtering (`EC-4`):** Automatic database filtering eliminated non-tooth lesion categories under `EC-4`.
   - **Augmentation & deep learning focus:** Concentrated on data augmentation for class imbalance/labeling scarcity and recent deep learning architectures rather than classical machine learning.
2. **Validity Threat Discussion (`p. 30`, citing `Petersen et al. [86]` and `Wohlin et al. [6]`):** Addresses candidate pool bias (`internal validity`), extraction bias (`construct validity`), scoring consistency (`conclusion validity`, citing scarcity of negative outcomes in primary papers), and **`external validity`**, where the authors assert their review covers all deep learning models and data augmentation methods used in dental imaging.

### 4.2 Reviewer Methodological Critique (`OS §3.2` Forensic Audit)
1. **Severe Search Truncation Bias (`p. 5`):** Explicitly screening **only the first two search-result pages** across `6` databases (`350` total -> `29` included) violates PRISMA completeness and captures `>14×` fewer YOLO studies (`4–5` vs. `73` YOLO-only studies from `1,159` records in `P0027` Hartman et al., 2026). Furthermore, `Fig. 2` (`p. 5`) mislabels the post-deduplication node as **`Verified records(n = 350)`** after subtracting `n = 37` duplicates (should be `313`).
2. **Inclusion of a Non-AI Clinical Study (`S20`) & Self-Citation (`S7`):**
   - **`S20` (`Al-Awasi et al., 2022 [30]`):** Not an AI/deep learning study at all, but a prospective clinical prevalence survey of apical periodontitis; `Table 8` (`p. 16`) counts `S20` under periapical lesions, whereas `Table 10` (`p. 22`) silently drops `S20` from deep learning model counts!
   - **`S7` (`Demir et al., 2023 [17]`):** The review authors' own ASYU conference paper is included and scored `9.0 / 10` without conflict-of-interest disclosure.
3. **Catastrophic Citation Swaps & Domain Terminology Blunders (`pp. 18, 24, 26, 29, 33, 35`):**
   - **Wrong Ref `[85]` (`p. 29` & `p. 35`):** Discussion on `p. 29` attributes a `GRADE`-rated `7`-study dental periapical lesion meta-analysis (`VGG16, ResNet-18`) to **`Liu et al. [85]`**, whereas Ref `[85]` (`p. 35`) is **`Liu et al. (2020), "PSGAN: A generative adversarial network for remote sensing image pan-sharpening" (*IEEE Trans. Geosci. Remote Sens.*)`**!
   - **Wrong Ref `[48]` (`p. 18` & `p. 33`):** Text cites `"In the study S27, Sajad et al. [48]..."`, yet Ref `[48]` is **`Ünsal & Adem (2022)` on Turkish dental caries classification** (`S27` is `[37]`).
   - **Radiology & ML Misnomers:** Calls **`PA`** **`"posteroanterior (PA) radiographs"`** (`p. 29`) instead of **periapical radiographs**; prints **`"positive-similarity ratio, negative-similarity ratio"`** alongside `"positive-likelihood ratio"` (`p. 29`); mischaracterizes OPG radiolucencies as **`"abnormally colored lumps"`** vs `"healthy tissue colors"` when citing `Mureșanu et al. [81]` (`p. 26`); repeatedly calls image **flipping `"the translation process"`** (`p. 24`); and misspells Retinex as **`"Retinax"`** (`p. 23–24`).
4. **Systematic Quality-Rubric & Arithmetic Contradictions (`Tables 6–7`, `Figs. 3–8`, `pp. 10–13`):**
   - **Category–Question Mismatch (`Table 6` vs `Table 7`):** `Table 6` (`p. 10`) defines `Q4–Q6` as `Relevance`, `Q7–Q8` as `Rigor`, and `Q9–Q10` as `Credibility`, whereas `Table 7` (`p. 11`) maps `Q4–Q6` under `RIGOR`, `Q7–Q8` under `CREDIBILITY`, and `Q9–Q10` under `RELEVANCE`.
   - **Contradictory Prose & Chart Counts:** `p. 12` says *"no studies below 2.0"* on Overall Quality (`Fig. 4` minimum is `6.5`), calls Rigor *"all four questions"* (`p. 12`) despite `2` or `3` questions, writes *"12 articles received a score of 1.5, exceeding the maximum score of 2.0"* (`p. 12`), and claims (`p. 13`, `Fig. 8`) **`0` studies reached `2.0` on Relevance (`max = 1.0`, `23` at `1.0`, `6` at `0.5`)** while `Table 7` assigns `Rel. = 2` to `21` studies!
   - **Inconsistent Table Counts & Classifications:** `p. 18` mentions `5` YOLO studies but lists `4` (`S1, S4, S12, S7`), while `Table 10` counts `4` and misses `S25` (`YOLOv3 + MobileNetV2`, `p. 22`). `Table 9` (`p. 17`) mislabels `YOLOv3/5/8`, `VGG16/19`, `SqueezeNet`, and `MobileNetV2` as `Detection` only (despite `p. 22` describing `MobileNetV2` as classification). `Table 4`, `Table 5`, and `Fig. 3` disagree on conference vs journal counts (`4` conference papers vs `3` in `Fig. 3` because `Scientific Reports` is plotted separately as `"Reports"`), and multiple publication years drift between `Table 4` and the bibliography (`S15, S18, S19, S27`). Uses **Kitchenham SE rubric** instead of **`QUADAS-2`**.
