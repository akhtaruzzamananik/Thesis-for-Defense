## 1. Problem & Clinical / Technical Motivation
- **Clinical motivation (pp. 1–2, 16):** Accurate identification of dental restorations, endodontic treatments, posts, crowns, fixed bridges, and dental implants on 2D panoramic radiographs (OPGs) is essential for prosthodontic treatment planning, longitudinal monitoring, dental record auditing, and population-level screening, especially in low-resource or remote clinics lacking specialist prosthodontists.
- **Technical problem (pp. 2–3, 8):** Different deep learning paradigms offer distinct trade-offs on panoramic radiographs: single-stage detectors (`YOLOv11`) prioritize fast end-to-end bounding-box localization, two-stage detectors (`Faster R-CNN`) refine region proposals for small or overlapping structures, and Vision Transformers (`ViT`) model global self-attention across image patches (`16 × 16`) for classification without explicit bounding-box localization (`p. 12`). Most prior studies relied on single-center datasets lacking multi-regional diversity.
- **Objective (pp. 1–3):** Curate a multi-regional dataset of **`2,235` adult panoramic radiographs** from three countries (Pakistan, Thailand, and the USA) annotated across **6 restoration and prosthesis classes** (`Crown`, `Dental Fillings`, `Endodontic Post`, `Root Canal Treated Tooth`, `Bridge`, and `Implant`), and benchmark **`YOLOv11`**, **`Faster R-CNN`**, and **`Vision Transformer (ViT)`** following the `CLAIM` (Checklist for Artificial Intelligence in Medical Imaging) guidelines (`p. 2`).

## 2. Dataset, Annotators & Splits
- **Multi-regional dataset provenance (pp. 2–3, Table 1, p. 17):**
  - Total: **`2,235` anonymized adult panoramic radiographs (`OPGs`)** collected from three geographic regions (`Table 1`, p. 3):
    1. **Pakistan (`1,135` images):** Public Mendeley Data OPG dataset (`Waqas et al., 2024 [13]`).
    2. **Thailand (`800` images):** Faculty of Dentistry, Chulalongkorn University, Bangkok (Ethics approval `HREC-DCU 2024-093`, p. 17).
    3. **United States (`300` images):** Public Tufts Dental Database (`Panetta et al., 2022 [14]`).
  - **Public dataset citation (p. 3, Ref. `[15]`, p. 17):** The authors cite an annotated Mendeley Data release (`Khurshid et al., 2025 [15]: "Annotated OPG dataset for dental Fillings, prostheses (Crowns & Bridges), endodontic Treatments, endodontic Posts, and dental implants"`), although the `Data availability` statement on `p. 17` states that data are available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request.
- **Inclusion and exclusion criteria (p. 3):**
  - **Included:** High-resolution adult panoramic radiographs with clear visibility of dental arches and surrounding structures containing at least one of the 6 target restoration/prosthesis classes.
  - **Excluded:** Implant-supported bridges, long-span bridges, orthodontic mini-implants, radiographs showing dental trauma, deciduous or mixed dentition, and images with severe noise, motion blur, or projection artifacts.
- **Dataset split (`Table 1`, p. 3):**
  - **Training set:** `1,835` OPGs (`82.1%`)
  - **Validation set:** `200` OPGs (`8.9%`)
  - **Testing set:** `200` OPGs (`8.9%`)
  - *Note on split granularity:* Whether the `82.1% / 8.9% / 8.9%` split was stratified by country/institution (`Pakistan / Thailand / USA`) or whether the `200` test OPGs were held out from a specific center is `not reported`.

- **Annotation protocol & inter-annotator reliability (pp. 3–4, Fig. 1):**
  - Annotated using **`Roboflow`** and **`CVAT`** (Computer Vision Annotation Tool) in YOLO `.txt`, Pascal VOC `.xml`, and COCO `.json` formats (`p. 3`).
  - **Annotators:** **3 qualified dental specialists** (a prosthodontist, an endodontist, and a restorative dentistry consultant), each with **`>10` years of clinical experience** (`pp. 3–4`).

## 3. Methodology & Experimental Design
- **Preprocessing & resolution asymmetry across models (pp. 5–6, Fig. 2):**
  - **Aspect-ratio-preserving letterbox resize (zero-padding) + contrast normalization (`p. 5`):**
    - **`YOLOv11` and `ViT`:** Resized to **`640 × 640` pixels**.
    - **`Faster R-CNN`:** Resized to **`1024 × 1024` pixels** (`2.56×` more pixels than `YOLOv11`).
  - **Data augmentation (`pp. 5–6, Fig. 2`):** `HSV` color-space adjustments, random scaling (`±10%`), random rotation (`±15°`), shearing, translation, horizontal and vertical flipping, and Gaussian noise injection.
- **Evaluated architectures & training hyperparameters (`pp. 6–9, Figs. 3–5`):**
  1. **`YOLOv11` (Single-stage object detection, `pp. 6–8, Fig. 3`):**
     - **Architecture:** `C3k2` CSP backbone blocks, `SPPF` (Spatial Pyramid Pooling — Fast), `C2PSA` (Cross-Stage Partial with Spatial Attention), PANet neck, and multi-scale detection head (`P3/P4/P5`) with `CIoU` box loss, `BCE` classification loss, and `DFL` (Distribution Focal Loss); initialized with COCO pretrained weights (`p. 8`). Exact variant size (`n/s/m/l/x`) is `not reported`.
     - **Training (`pp. 8–9`):** `400` epochs, batch size `16`, `AdamW` optimizer (`lr = 0.0003`, weight decay `0.0005`, linear decay schedule), input `640 × 640`.
  2. **`Faster R-CNN` (Two-stage object detection, `pp. 8–9, Fig. 4`):**
     - **Architecture:** CNN backbone with `FPN` (Feature Pyramid Network), `RPN` (Region Proposal Network), `RoI Align`, and dual classification / bounding-box regression heads; initialized with COCO pretrained weights (`pp. 8–9`).
     - **Training (`p. 9`):** `500` epochs, batch size `8`, `SGD` optimizer (`lr = 0.01`, weight decay `0.0001`, `MultiStep` LR scheduler), input `1024 × 1024`.
  3. **`Vision Transformer (ViT)` (Global patch-attention classification, `pp. 8–9, 12, Fig. 5`):**
     - **Architecture:** Splits input image into non-overlapping **`16 × 16` pixel patches**, adds positional embeddings and a learnable `[CLS]` token, processes through `12` Transformer encoder layers (`Multi-Head Self-Attention + MLP`), and outputs class probabilities via a classification head; initialized with `ImageNet-21k` pretrained weights (`pp. 8–9`). As explicitly noted on `p. 12`, `ViT` performs **classification without explicit bounding-box localization**.
     - **Training (`p. 9`):** `200` epochs, batch size `24`, `AdamW` optimizer (`lr = 0.00002`, weight decay `0.01`, constant LR), input `640 × 640`.
- **Compute hardware & software environment:** `not reported` (no GPU/CPU model, VRAM, or deep learning framework version is stated anywhere in the paper).

### 4.2 Class-Wise & Scale-Wise Performance Findings (`pp. 9–12, Figs. 6–11`)
- **`YOLOv11` class-wise behavior (`p. 9, Figs. 6–7`):**
  - Highest class Average Precision (`AP@0.50`) on **`Crown` (`AP = 0.88`)**, followed by **`Bridge`** and **`Implant`** (`p. 9, Fig. 7`).
  - Lower detection performance on **`Dental Fillings`**, **`Endodontic Post`**, and **`Root Canal Treated Tooth`** (`p. 9`), driven by small object size, variable restoration morphology, and overlapping radiopaque structures in `640 × 640` images.
- **`Faster R-CNN` class-wise precision and scale-wise recall (`p. 11, Figs. 8–9`):**
  - **Per-class precision (`p. 11`):**
    - `Implant`: **`0.9459` (`94.59%`)**
    - `Dental Fillings`: **`0.9358` (`93.58%`)**
    - `Crown`: **`0.9333` (`93.33%`)**
    - `Bridge`: **`0.8913` (`89.13%`)**
    - `Endodontic Post`: **`0.8800` (`88.00%`)**
    - `Root Canal Treated Tooth`: **`0.8500` (`85.00%`)**
  - **Scale-wise Average Recall (`AR@100`, `p. 11`):**
    - **Medium objects:** `66.7%` (`0.667`)
    - **Small objects:** `59.6%` (`0.596`)
    - **Large objects:** `50.9%` (`0.509`)
- **`Vision Transformer (ViT)` class-wise AP & loss (`pp. 11–12, Figs. 10–11`):**
  - Validation loss converged to **`0.2282`** at epoch `200` (`p. 11`).
  - Reported class-wise `AP` (`p. 12`): **`Crown = 0.95`**, **`Implant = 0.92`**, **`Bridge = 0.89`**, with lower scores on `Endodontic Post` and `Root Canal Treated Tooth` due to overlapping radiopaque boundaries (`p. 12`).

## 5. Limitations
### 5.1 Author-Stated Limitations (pp. 12–16, Table 4)
1. **Severe class imbalance (`Table 2`, pp. 3, 13, 16):** `Dental Fillings` (`6,206` instances, `54.1%`) heavily outnumber `Bridge` (`626` instances, `5.5%`), `Endodontic Post` (`670` instances, `5.8%`), and `Implant` (`851` instances, `7.4%`), degrading minority-class recall and motivating future GAN-based synthetic balancing (`p. 16`).
2. **Small-object and overlapping-structure errors (`pp. 9, 12–15, Table 4`):** `YOLOv11` struggles to localize small, thin `Endodontic Posts` and small `Dental Fillings` (`Recall = 60.6%`), whereas `ViT` struggles when prostheses and endodontic fillings overlap and lacks explicit bounding-box localization (`p. 12`).
3. **2D panoramic radiograph projection limits (`pp. 3, 16`):** Superimposition, geometric distortion, and the exclusion of mixed/deciduous dentition, trauma, and complex implant-supported bridges limit generalizability to complex clinical cases (`pp. 3, 16`).

### 5.2 Reviewer Methodological Critique (`OS §3.2`)
1. **Confounded resolution & training-budget comparison (`640 × 640` vs `1024 × 1024`, `pp. 5, 8–9, 14`):** `Faster R-CNN` was trained and evaluated at **`1024 × 1024` resolution for `500` epochs**, whereas `YOLOv11` was restricted to **`640 × 640` resolution (`2.56×` fewer pixels) for `400` epochs** (and the exact `YOLOv11` variant `n/s/m/l/x` is `not reported`). Because `Endodontic Posts`, `Root Canal Treated Teeth`, and small `Dental Fillings` occupy only a few pixels in a `640 × 640` OPG, this resolution asymmetry directly confounds the reported `mAP@0.50` gap (`82.0%` for `Faster R-CNN` vs `70.7%` for `YOLOv11`).
2. **Incommensurable task comparison in `Table 3` (`Object Detection` vs `Classification`, `pp. 11–14`):** `Table 3` (`p. 14`) and the Abstract (`p. 1`) rank `ViT` as the "highest-performing" model (`94.15%` accuracy, `94.64%` precision, `94.15%` recall) alongside `YOLOv11` (`69.0%` accuracy, `70.7%` `mAP@0.50`) and `Faster R-CNN` (`75.1%` accuracy, `82.0%` `mAP@0.50`). However, as the authors themselves acknowledge on `p. 12`, `ViT` only performs classification without bounding-box localization (`IoU` matching is not required), making direct accuracy/precision/recall comparisons against object detectors methodologically invalid.
3. **Incomplete reporting of test-set instance counts and per-class `AP` tables (`Table 2, p. 3; Table 3, p. 14`):** `Table 2` only lists instance counts for the training (`10,896`) and validation (`566`) sets, omitting the `200`-image test set instance distribution. Furthermore, per-class `AP@0.50` is only partially mentioned in prose (`p. 9, 11, 12`) and plotted in bar/PR charts (`Figs. 7, 9, 11`) rather than tabulated across all 6 classes for all models, and `YOLOv11` `mAP@0.50:0.95` and `F1-score` are omitted from `Table 3`.
4. **Zero hardware or runtime benchmarks (`G6` gap):** Claims of real-time suitability for `YOLOv11` vs offline-only speed for `Faster R-CNN` (`pp. 13–15, Table 4`) are unsupported by any empirical `ms/image`, `FPS`, `Params`, or `GFLOPs` measurements (`not reported`).

## 6. Relevance to Our Thesis (`STEP-007` Synthesis Links)
- **Direct evidence for resolution sensitivity (`G2` / `S9`):** Demonstrates why evaluating `YOLOv11` at `640 × 640` on full panoramic radiographs underperforms a `1024 × 1024` detector (`Faster R-CNN`) on small endodontic posts and fillings (`Recall = 60.6%`, `mAP@0.50 = 70.7%` vs `82.0%`), directly motivating our thesis's controlled resolution/tiling and small-lesion enhancement experiments (`S9`–`S12`) under matched input resolutions.
- **Multi-regional benchmark comparator for restorative/endodontic classes (`G1` / `G5`):** Provides multi-country (`Pakistan`, `Thailand`, `USA`, `2,235` OPGs, `κ = 0.89`) baselines for `Dental Fillings`, `Crown`, `Bridge`, `Implant`, `Endodontic Post`, and `Root Canal Treated Tooth`, while illustrating the methodological pitfall of comparing classification metrics (`ViT`) against bounding-box detection metrics (`YOLOv11`, `Faster R-CNN`).
