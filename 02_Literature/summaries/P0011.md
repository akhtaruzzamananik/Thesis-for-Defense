## Verified citation

Scarano, G., Agostinelli, S., Amerini, I., & Papi, P. (2026). YoLeTooth: A Unified Framework for Joint Tooth Segmentation and Periapical Lesion Detection in Panoramic Radiographs. *Journal of Imaging*, 12(6), 272. https://doi.org/10.3390/jimaging12060272 (PDF p. 1).

## question/task/modality

- **Modality:** 2D dental panoramic radiographs (`OPTs`, `2444 × 1292` and `2316 × 1292` px, pp. 1, 10–11).
- **Task:** Unified 4-stage cascaded framework (**`YoLeTooth`**) performing:
  1. *Stage 1:* Coarse vertical dental ROI cropping (`σ = 0.75` middle height, p. 11).
  2. *Stage 2:* Individual tooth instance segmentation and FDI numbering (`32` adult teeth `0–31` + supernumerary class `32`/`91`) using **`YOLOv12-turbo-m-seg` + `Powerful IoU v2 (PIoUv2)` loss**, compared against `YOLOv9e`, `YOLOv11n/s/m/l-seg`, `YOLOv8n/s/m/l-seg`, `YOLO-DentSeg`, `ResNeSt-50`, `YOLOv5m`, `Mask R-CNN`, and `U-Net` (pp. 5, 10, 12–13, 19).
  3. *Stage 3:* Segmentation-guided periapical ROI expansion (`α = 0.30` vertical, `β = 0.05` horizontal) and **3-class periapical lesion detection (`PAI 3, 4, 5`)** using **`YOLOv12-m` + `PIoUv2` loss**, compared against `RF-DETR-Base`, `YOLOv9-c`, `YOLOv11-m`, `YOLOv11-x`, `YOLOv8-m`, `YOLOv8-x`, `YOLOv12-turbo-m`, and `YOLOv12-m + GhostConv` (pp. 5, 11, 15–17).
  4. *Stage 4:* Geometric overlap tooth-to-lesion association (`Shoelace` polygon intersection `≥ τ = 0.04` [`4%` of tooth mask area], achieving `90.32%` association accuracy across `5,288 / 5,857` lesions, p. 11).

## data/counts

- **Dataset 1 — Tooth Segmentation (`pp. 10–11`):** Public **`DualLabel Dataset`** (`Zhou et al., BMC Oral Health 2024, 24:1201`, Ref `[48]`) comprising **`2,066` panoramic radiographs** with pixel-level segmentation masks for up to `32` FDI tooth classes + supernumerary teeth (`class 91`).
- **Dataset 2 — Periapical Lesion Detection (`p. 11, Table 1`):** Public Hanoi Medical University dataset (`Do et al., Data in Brief 2024, 54:110486`, Ref `[49]`; same base source as `P0008`) containing `3,926` base radiographs (`3,399` lesion-bearing images with **`5,857` lesions** in `Table 1`, p. 11: `PAI 3 = 3,587 [61.2%]`, `PAI 4 = 1,765 [30.1%]`, `PAI 5 = 505 [8.6%]`).
- **Critical Pre-Augmented Dataset Usage (`p. 11, lines 554–557`):** Unlike `P0008` (which discarded pre-augmented copies), `P0011` explicitly states it used the **augmented version totaling `17,004` images** created via random scaling (`0.8–1.2×`), random rotation (`-90° to 180°`), mirroring (horizontal, vertical, combined), and noise injection.

## annotation

- **Tooth segmentation (`p. 10`):** Labeled by `2` postgraduate students and `2` dentists (`>5` years experience) and reviewed by `1` senior dentist (`>15` years experience).
- **Periapical lesions (`p. 11`):** Bounding-box `PAI 3/4/5` annotations established by `3` experienced dentists (`>5` years clinical practice).
- **Inter-annotator agreement (`kappa`):** `not reported`.

## split

- **Split ratios (`pp. 11, 12, 23–24`):** Mentions training, validation, and test splits (fixed across 5 random seeds in `Table 15`, p. 24), but **exact train/val/test image counts, split percentages, and whether the `17,004` pre-augmented copies were grouped by original radiograph are `not reported`**.

## model

- **Stage-2 Tooth Segmentation (`Tables 2–3, 6–7`, pp. 13, 19):**
  - *Proposed:* `YOLOv12-turbo-m-seg` + `PIoUv2` loss (`22.3M` params).
  - *Comparators:* `YOLOv9e` (`60.4M`), `YOLOv12-turbo-n/l/x-seg` (`2.7M / 27.6M / 62.0M`), `YOLOv11n/s/m/l-seg` (`2.8M / 10.0M / 22.3M / 27.6M`), `YOLOv8n/s/m/l-seg` (`3.2M / 11.8M / 27.2M / 45.9M`), `YOLO-DentSeg` (`25.2M`), `ResNeSt-50` (`33.0M`), `YOLOv5m` (`21.8M`), `Mask R-CNN` (`29.0M`), and `U-Net`.
- **Stage-3 Periapical Lesion Detection (`Table 4`, p. 17):**
  - *Proposed:* `YOLOv12-m` + `PIoUv2` loss (`20.1M` params).
  - *Comparators:* `RF-DETR-Base` (`29.0M`), `YOLOv9-c` (`25.5M`), `YOLOv11-m` (`20.1M`), `YOLOv11-x` (`56.9M`), `YOLOv8-m` (`25.9M`), `YOLOv8-x` (`68.2M`), `YOLOv12-turbo-m` (`19.6M`), and `YOLOv12-m + GhostConv` (`17.4M`).

## preprocessing

- **ROI & resolution scaling (`pp. 11, 17, 21, Table 11`):** Trained at `1280 × 1280` pixels (`640, 1024, 1280, 1600` ablated in `Table 11`, p. 21); during inference, vertical ROI cropping (`σ = 0.75`) reduces the input tensor from `1280 × 1280` to `640 × 640` (`~4×` spatial pixel reduction).
- **Enhanced preprocessing ablation (`pp. 15–17, Fig. 5, Table 4`):** Tested an enhanced pipeline combining **`sharpening + CLAHE + Gaussian blur`** (`Enh. = Yes`) vs unenhanced standard images (`Enh. = No`).

## training

- **Segmentation (`p. 12, Table 3`):** `300` epochs, `AdamW` (`lr = 2.7 × 10^-4`, momentum `0.9`, weight decay `1 × 10^-4`, batch `8`, `1280 × 1280`; also swept `SGD`/`Adam`/`NAdam`/`RMSProp` in `Tables 10–14`).
- **Detection (`p. 15, Table 4`):** `300` epochs (`80–300` across baselines), `SGD` (`lr = 1 × 10^-2`, warmup `5` epochs, batch `6`, `1280 × 1280`, early stopping `patience = 30`).

## metrics/IoU

- **Segmentation (`pp. 12–13, Tables 2–3`):** Mask/box `mAP@50`, `mAP@50-95`, Precision, Recall, `mDice`, and `mIoU` (plus 5-seed mean ± SD and coefficient of variation `CV %` in `Table 15`, p. 24).
- **Detection (`pp. 15–17, Table 4`):** 3-class `PAI 3/4/5` `mAP@50` and `mAP@50-95`.

## limitations

- **Author-stated (`pp. 11, 25–26`):** (1) Cascading error risk: if Stage-2 misses a tooth (`2.63%` miss rate at `97.37%` recall), any lesion on that tooth is missed (`≤154 / 5,857` lesions, p. 25), and `569 / 5,857` (`9.7%`) lesions fell outside the expanded ROI or were partially cut (`p. 11`); (2) omits early `PAI 1–2` stages and external prosthetics (`bridges, crowns, braces`); (3) ROI margins (`α, β, τ`) are tuned for adult dentition rather than pediatric mixed dentition; (4) lacks prospective clinical validation.

## critique

- **CRITICAL CROSS-STUDY AUDIT (`P0011` vs `P0008` — `OS §3.2`, `G5`, `RF06`):**
  1. **Direct proof of pre-split augmentation inflation on the exact same dataset:** Both `P0008` (`Oztekin 2026`) and `P0011` (`Scarano 2026`) use `Do et al. (2024)` (`Data in Brief 54:110486`). `P0008` discarded the `17,004 - 3,924` pre-augmented copies to prevent train/test leakage and obtained **`YOLO12m mAP@50 = 0.494` (no tiling) / `0.615` (tiled) / `0.664` (final)** (`mAP@50-95 = 0.230–0.333`). In contrast, `P0011` (`p. 11`) used all **`17,004` pre-augmented images** without deduplicating by parent radiograph and reported **`YOLOv12-m mAP@50 = 0.9194` and `mAP@50-95 = 0.7794`**! This `0.615 vs 0.9194` (`+30.4%` `mAP@50` and `+50.2%` `mAP@50-95`) gap on the same model (`YOLOv12m`) and same source dataset is the single strongest empirical proof in our thesis for **`G5` and `RF06`**.
  2. **Unfair epoch budget in `Table 4` (`p. 17`):** `YOLOv12-m` was trained for `300` epochs while `YOLOv8-m/x`, `YOLOv9-c`, and `YOLOv11-m/x` were trained for only `80` epochs!
  3. **Negative effect of bundled `sharpening + CLAHE + Gaussian blur` (`Table 4`, p. 17):** Adding Gaussian blur alongside CLAHE/sharpening reduced `YOLOv12-m` `mAP@50` from `0.9194` to `0.9023` (`-1.71%`) and `mAP@50-95` from `0.7794` to `0.7403` (`-3.91%`), corroborating `P0004` (`Roopitha 2026`) that Gaussian smoothing blurs periapical/tooth borders.

## reuse

- **High-impact evidence for:**
  - **`G5` & `RF06` (Data leakage audit):** Paired with `P0008` to prove how pre-augmented public datasets inflate `YOLOv12m` `mAP@50` from `0.615` to `0.9194`.
  - **`RQ1` (`YOLO` vs `RF-DETR`, `Mask R-CNN`, `U-Net`):** Shows `YOLOv12-m` beats `RF-DETR-Base` by `+12.31%` on strict `mAP@50-95` (`0.7794` vs `0.6563`), and `YOLOv12-turbo-m-seg` beats `Mask R-CNN` (`0.9773` vs `0.7620 mAP@50`) and `U-Net` (`0.9354` vs `0.8803 mDice`).
  - **`RQ2` (Preprocessing & resolution):** Shows `1280–1600` px improves `mAP@50-95` by `+7.1% to +8.3%` over `640` px (`Table 11`), while bundled `CLAHE + sharpening + Gaussian blur` reduces `mAP@50-95` by `-3.91%` (`Table 4`).
  - **`STEP-010` Open Dataset:** Identifies the public **`DualLabel Dataset` (`2,066` OPGs, Ref `[48]`)**.
