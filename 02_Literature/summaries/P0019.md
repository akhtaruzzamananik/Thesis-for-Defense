## 1. Problem & Clinical / Technical Motivation
- **Clinical motivation (pp. 1–2):** In pediatric dentistry (`ages 5–13`, mixed dentition period), simultaneously detecting and FDI-numbering both erupted primary teeth and unerupted underlying permanent tooth germs on 2D panoramic radiographs (`PRs`) is essential for orthodontic and interceptive treatment planning. Manual charting of `40+` overlapping primary and permanent teeth per radiograph is tedious and observer-dependent.
- **Technical problem (pp. 2, 5):** Earlier YOLO detectors require Non-Maximum Suppression (`NMS`) post-processing, which adds inference latency and can suppress valid overlapping boxes when unerupted permanent tooth germs closely underlie resorbing primary roots.
- **Objective (pp. 1–2, 5):** Evaluate the **`YOLOv10x` (Extra Large)** NMS-free object detector (using consistent dual-assignment one-to-many training and one-to-one inference heads) across **all `52` FDI tooth classes** (`32` permanent + `20` primary teeth) on pediatric mixed-dentition panoramic radiographs, following `CLAIM` and `STARD` reporting standards (`p. 2`).

## 2. Dataset, Annotators & Splits
- **Cohort & acquisition (`Sections 2.2–2.3`, pp. 3–4, 15):**
  - **Source:** **`200` retrospective pediatric panoramic radiographs** (`patients aged 5–13 years`, collected `2019–2024`) from the Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Radiology, Trakya University Faculty of Dentistry, Edirne, Turkey (Ethics approval `TÜTF-GOBEAK 2024/450`, `04.11.2024`, `pp. 2, 15`; note that `p. 3` line 138 inadvertently left `(Blinded for Review) University` in the published text).
  - **Scanner:** Acquired by a single operator using a **`PaX-Flex` panoramic unit (`Vatech`, Seoul, Republic of Korea)** at `50–90 kV`, `4–10 mA`, and `10.1 s` exposure (`p. 4`).
  - **Inclusion / Exclusion criteria (`pp. 3–4`):**
    - **Included:** Radiographs graded as `Grade 1` or `Grade 2` (UK National Radiological Protection Board criteria) containing **at least `10` primary teeth with underlying permanent tooth germs**. Radiographs with caries, restorations, root canal treatments, rotated teeth, or supernumerary teeth were **retained** to reflect routine clinical complexity (`p. 4`).
    - **Excluded:** Motion or metal artifacts, jaw cysts or tumors, cleft lip/palate or developmental anomalies, and patient positioning errors.
- **Annotation protocol (`Section 2.4`, pp. 4–5, Fig. 2):**
  - Total instances: **`8,153` manually labeled tooth bounding boxes** (`mean = 40.77` teeth/germs per OPG) across **`52` FDI classes** (`11–18, 21–28, 31–38, 41–48` permanent; `51–55, 61–65, 71–75, 81–85` primary). Per-class instance counts are `not reported`.
  - **Annotator:** Single oral and maxillofacial radiologist (`R.B.P.`, `7` years of experience) using **`Roboflow`** (`.txt` YOLO format), who re-evaluated all `200` radiographs after a 2-week interval (`p. 4`). Intra-observer agreement (`κ` / `ICC`) is `not reported`.
- **Dataset split (`Section 2.7`, p. 6):**
  - **Training set:** `140` OPGs (`70%`)
  - **Validation set:** `30` OPGs (`15%`)
  - **Testing set:** `30` OPGs (`15%`)

## 3. Methodology & Experimental Design
- **Automated preprocessing pipeline (`Section 2.6`, p. 6):**
  1. **Single-channel grayscale conversion:** Explicit conversion to 1-channel grayscale to eliminate metadata/channel inconsistencies.
  2. **Image cropping:** Cropping to focus on the dentoalveolar region (exact crop method or coordinates `not reported`).
  3. **Contrast enhancement:** Applied to highlight low-contrast tooth germs (exact algorithm—e.g., histogram equalization vs. `CLAHE`—and parameters `not reported`).
  4. **Pixel normalization:** Scaled to `[0, 1]`.
  5. **Image resizing:** Resized to **`640 × 640` pixels**.
- **Model architecture & training configuration (`Sections 2.5, 2.8`, pp. 5, 7–8):**
  - **Architecture:** **`YOLOv10x` (Extra Large)** with enhanced `CSPNet` backbone, `PAN` neck, lightweight classification heads, spatial-channel decoupled downsampling, rank-guided block design, large-kernel convolutions, partial self-attention (`PSA`), and NMS-free dual label assignment (`one-to-many` + `one-to-one` heads, `p. 5`).
  - **Initialization (`Section 2.8.1`, p. 7):** `model: None` (trained from scratch without a pretrained checkpoint, `p. 7`).
  - **Hyperparameters (`Section 2.8`, pp. 7–8):** Max `200` epochs, `EarlyStopping patience = 10` epochs on validation loss (**best weights saved at `epoch 71`** as `best.pt`, `pp. 7–8, Fig. 3`), `batch size = 16`, `imgsz = 640`, `optimizer = Auto`, `cos_lr = False`, `amp = True` (Automatic Mixed Precision), `freeze = None` (`52` classes).
  - **Hardware (`p. 7`):** **`NVIDIA RTX 4070 Ti` GPU**.

## 5. Limitations
### 5.1 Author-Stated Limitations (`Section 4`, pp. 14–15)
1. **Single-center, single-scanner cohort (`pp. 14–15`):** All `200` OPGs came from a single institution (Trakya University, Thrace region) and a single `Vatech PaX-Flex` machine, limiting generalizability across different X-ray sensors and exposure protocols.
2. **Selective inclusion criterion & small sample size (`200` OPGs, `p. 14`):** Restricting inclusion to `Grade 1–2` OPGs with `≥10` primary teeth and underlying permanent germs reduced dataset diversity and led to class imbalance across tooth positions.
3. **Misclassification of primary incisor `51` (`p. 8, Fig. 10`):** Highlighted as a specific detection limitation in mixed dentition.

### 5.2 Reviewer Methodological Critique (`OS §3.2`)
1. **No experimental comparator models (`pp. 7–14`):** Only a single model (`YOLOv10x`) was trained and evaluated. All comparisons to `YOLOv4` (`Kaya et al., 2022 [23]`; `Putra et al., 2023 [24]`), `YOLOv5` (`Beşer et al., 2024 [5]`), and `Faster R-CNN` (`Bilgir et al., 2021 [25]`) in the Discussion (`p. 14`) are indirect literature comparisons across different datasets.
2. **Overlooked severe primary-incisor failures in `Table 1` (`pp. 8–9`):** On `p. 8`, the authors state that *"The mAP50 for tooth 51 was found to be 0.712, significantly lower than the mAP50 values of other teeth, all of which exceeded 0.9"*. In fact, `Table 1` (`p. 9`) shows that **`tooth 52` had `mAP50 = 0.835` (`< 0.90`) and `F1 = 0.697`**, **`tooth 81` had `Precision = 0.450` and `F1 = 0.621`**, and **`tooth 71` collapsed to `mAP50-95 = 0.0995`**. Because children aged `6–13` frequently have already exfoliated their primary central/lateral incisors (`51, 52, 61, 71, 72, 81, 82`), the `30`-image test set likely contained very few instances of these teeth, causing extreme metric volatility and poor bounding-box tightness (`mAP50-95 = 0.0995–0.502`).
3. **Single annotator without inter-observer validation & un-reproducible preprocessing (`pp. 4, 6`):** Ground truth was labeled by a single radiologist (`R.B.P.`) without multi-reader consensus or reported intra-rater `κ`/`ICC` (`p. 4`), and the "image cropping" and "contrast enhancement" steps (`Section 2.6`, p. 6) lack algorithmic definitions or parameters.
4. **Zero runtime/efficiency metrics (`G6` gap) & editorial oversights (`pp. 2–3, 7`):** No inference `ms/image`, `FPS`, or parameter counts are reported (`not reported`); `YOLOv10x` was trained from scratch (`model: None`, `p. 7`) rather than fine-tuned from COCO weights on only `140` training images; `p. 3` line 138 retains `(Blinded for Review) University`; and the seminal `YOLOv10` architecture is cited via a secondary solar-panel review (`Hussain & Khanam, 2024 [11]`) rather than the original Tsinghua University paper (`Wang et al., 2024`).

## 6. Relevance to Our Thesis (`STEP-007` Synthesis Links)
- **Direct pediatric mixed-dentition comparator alongside `P0007` (`Ozcelik et al., 2026`, `YOLOv11`) and `P0016` (`Şevik & Mutlu, 2025`):** Provides a `52`-class FDI `YOLOv10x` benchmark (`mAP50 = 0.968`, `mAP50-95 = 0.696`, `F1 = 0.919` on `200` OPGs / `8,153` teeth) that complements `P0007` (`44` FDI classes on `1,378` pediatric OPGs, `YOLOv11 mAP50 = 0.8715, mAP50-95 = 0.5613`).
- **Evidence on `IoU` sensitivity (`mAP50` vs. `mAP50-95`) for small/resorbing dental structures (`G2` / `G5`):** The dramatic gap on primary incisors (`tooth 71 mAP50 = 0.995` vs. `mAP50-95 = 0.0995`; `tooth 51 mAP50 = 0.712` vs. `mAP50-95 = 0.337`) proves that `mAP@0.50` alone masks severe localization errors when primary roots resorb over permanent tooth germs, reinforcing our protocol's mandatory reporting of both `mAP@0.50` and `mAP@0.50:0.95` plus per-class instance counts.
