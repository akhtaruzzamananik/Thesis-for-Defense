## 1. Problem & Clinical / Technical Motivation
- **Clinical & Dataset Gap (`p. 2`):** Prior OPG tooth-segmentation and numbering pipelines often rely on small or undisclosed private datasets (`e.g., Jader [16]`, `Tuzoff [17]`, `Panetta [18] = P0042`) and stop at tooth numbering without downstream clinical tooth-condition charting and reporting.
- **Objective (`p. 2`):** Curate a large **`5,000`-OPG** dual-labeled dataset (simultaneous **FDI numbering polygons** + **`7`-class tooth state** labels) and couple a **`YOLOv9-e`** instance-segmentation model with an **`EfficientNetV2-L`** (`1.5×`-expanded crop) classifier for automated OPG visualization and structured reporting.

## 2. Dual-Labeled Dataset, Taxonomy & Training Design (`pp. 3–6, 11`, `Table 1`, `Figs. 1–3`)

### 2.1 Multi-Source Cohort & Annotation (`pp. 3–4, 11`, `Table 1`, `Figs. 1–2`)
- **IRB & Collection (`pp. 3, 11`):** Ethics Committee of China-Japan Union Hospital of Jilin University (`#2024011704`): **`5,000` OPGs (`PNG`, `124,568` annotated teeth; `5`-month annotation in `Labelme v5.4.1`)**:
  - **Self-collected (`2,000` OPGs, `Jan 2015–Dec 2023`):** Three Chinese hospitals (China-Japan Union Hospital of Jilin Univ., Wuxi Stomatology Hospital, People's Hospital of Zhengzhou) + `3` dental clinics, on **Orthophos XG** (Dentsply Sirona), **Planmeca ProMax**, and **Bondream 1020** (`>= 16 yrs`, permanent dentition only; mean age `36.6 yrs`, `989` males / `1,011` females; excludes deciduous/mixed dentition and severe blur).
  - **Re-annotated public OPGs (`3,000` OPGs):** Sourced from **`Nie / Wang et al. (2023)` (`OII-DS`, `[22]`)** and **`Silva et al. (2023)` (`[23]`)**.
- **Annotator Workflow (`p. 3`, `Fig. 1`):** `4` primary labelers (`2` postgraduates + `2` clinicians `> 5 yrs` exp) drew polygons, FDI numbers (`11–48` + `91` supernumerary = `33` classes; **dental implants excluded / unlabeled**), and state tags (`0–6`), reviewed by `1` senior clinician (`> 15 yrs` exp). Disagreements resolved by discussion (`inter-rater kappa / Dice`: `not reported`).

### 2.2 Two-Stage Fusion Pipeline & Training Protocols (`pp. 4–6`, `Fig. 3`)
- **Stage 1 (`YOLOv9-e` Instance Segmentation + FDI Numbering):** `5,000` OPGs split **`3:1:1`** (`3,000 train / 1,000 val / 1,000 test`). Input resized to **`1280 × 1280`**, `SGD` (`LR 0.01 → 0.0001`, `momentum 0.937`, `weight decay 0.0005`), `batch size = 2`, `80` epochs (`early stop patience = 30`); augmentations = rotation, scaling, translation, mosaic.
- **Stage 2 (`EfficientNetV2-L` 7-Class Tooth State Classifier):** Ground-truth tooth regions cropped after **`1.5×` ROI expansion** (`Fig. 3`), randomly subsampling **`2,000` teeth per category = `14,000` balanced crops**, then split **`3:1:1`** (`8,400 train / 2,800 val / 2,800 test`). Input `224 × 224`, `SGD` (`LR 0.01 → 0.0001`), `batch size = 16`, `50` epochs; augmentations = horizontal flip, **vertical flip**, random rotation.
- **Compute Environment (`p. 6`):** `NVIDIA RTX 4070 Ti`, Intel `"i14700"` CPU, `32 GB RAM`, Windows 10, Python `3.8`, PyTorch `2.3.0`. (`Model selection experiments`: deferred to Supplementary Material 1.)

## 3. Quantitative Results (`pp. 6–9`, `Tables 2–3`, `Figs. 4–5`)

### 3.1 Stage 1 — `YOLOv9-e` Instance Segmentation & FDI Numbering (`Table 2`, p. 6; `1,000` Test OPGs)
- **Overall (`all` row, `33` classes):** **`Precision = 0.989`**, **`Recall = 0.955`**, `FNR = 0.045`, `FPR = 0.010`, **`mean IoU = 0.872`**, **`mAP50 = 0.975`**, **`mAP50-95 = 0.840`**.
- **Permanent Dentition (`11–48`) vs. Supernumerary (`91`):** All `32` standard FDI teeth achieved `mAP50 = 0.945–0.995` and `mAP50-95 = 0.768–0.909` (lowest `41`: `0.768`, `28`: `0.783`, `31`: `0.790`; highest `36`: `0.909`, `33`: `0.896`, `21`: `0.894`). **Supernumerary teeth (`91`, `n = 292`)** dropped to **`Recall = 0.565`** (`FNR = 0.435`, `Precision = 1.000`, `mean IoU = 0.788`, **`mAP50 = 0.726`**, **`mAP50-95 = 0.588`**).

## 4. Limitations & Reviewer Methodological Critique (`OS §3.2`)

### 4.1 Author-Stated Limitations (`pp. 7, 10`)
1. **Single-label mutually exclusive hierarchy (`p. 10`):** Cannot represent multi-state teeth (e.g., an `RCT`-treated tooth with secondary caries is forced solely into `RCT`) via strict label priority: `Residual root > RCT > Crown > RCT+Crown > Fillings > Caries > Normal`.
2. **Weak classes (`pp. 7, 10`):** Supernumerary teeth (`91`) exhibit `Recall = 0.565` due to extreme scarcity (`292 / 124,568`) and ectopic positional diversity, while **superficial caries (`F1 = 0.876, Recall = 0.828`)** proved difficult to label/detect on `2D` OPGs.
3. **Scope omissions & vanilla models (`pp. 3, 10–11`):** Excludes dental implants, primary/mixed dentition, periapical lesions, root fractures, and root resorption; uses unmodified baseline architectures tuned only by hyperparameters.

### 4.2 Reviewer Methodological Critique (`OS §3.2` Audit)
1. **Severe Stage-2 Cross-Patient Crop Leakage & Decoupled Evaluation (`p. 4`, `Table 1`, `Fig. 3`):**
   - For Stage 2 (`EfficientNetV2-L`), the authors pooled **`2,000` GT crops per class (`14,000` total — drawing `75.5%` of ALL `2,648` `RCT` teeth across the entire `5,000`-OPG dataset)** and split them **randomly `3:1:1` at the crop level** (`p. 4`). Consequently, teeth from the **same radiograph and same patient** sit in both Stage-2 training (`8,400`) and test (`2,800`) splits, and Stage-2 test crops do not align with Stage-1's `1,000`-OPG test split!
   - **Zero End-to-End Fusion Metrics:** Despite marketing an end-to-end fusion pipeline (`YOLOv9-e → 1.5× crop → EfficientNetV2-L`), **no end-to-end joint `(FDI + state)` performance metric is evaluated on Stage-1 predicted crops across the `1,000` test OPGs** under natural clinical base rates (where `74.3%` of teeth are normal and `2.1%–4.2%` have `RCT/fillings`, rather than `1:1:…:1` balanced `400/class` crops).
2. **Table 2 Aggregation Discrepancy (`p. 6`) & Missing Preliminary Benchmarks:**
   - Unweighted macro-averaging of all `33` per-class rows in `Table 2` gives `R = 0.964`, `FNR = 0.036`, `mAP50 = 0.981`, and `mAP50-95 = 0.847`, slightly differing from the printed `all` row (`0.955, 0.045, 0.975, 0.840`). Preliminary architecture ablation tables comparing `YOLOv9-e` and `EfficientNetV2-L` against alternatives are absent from the main manuscript (deferred to external Supplementary Material 1).
3. **Unphysiological Vertical-Flip Augmentation & Unverifiable Reproducibility (`pp. 4, 11–12`):**
   - Stage-2 training applies **random vertical flipping** (`p. 4`), inverting mandibular/maxillary dental anatomy upside-down (`cf. P0038`, which flags vertical OPG flipping as clinically impossible).
   - **Broken Data Availability Link & Missing Code (`p. 11`):** Prints unlinked placeholder `[A dual-labeled dataset (kaggle.com)]` with no active URL/DOI, releases no code/weights repository, cites a **Cardamom plant disease paper (`Sunil et al., 2022 [21]`)** for `EfficientNetV2` instead of `Tan & Le (2021)`, prints `"Intel i14700"` CPU (`p. 6`) and `"Tooh"` (`Fig. 3`), omits inter-annotator $\kappa$/Dice, omits patient-level split guarantees, and reports zero inference runtime (`ms/OPG` or `FPS`).

## 5. Relevance to Our Thesis (`STEP-007` Synthesis Links)
- **`YOLOv9` Tooth Segmentation & Numbering Benchmark (`G1 / G5`):** Establishes a strong `5,000`-OPG multi-center/multi-scanner benchmark for **`YOLOv9-e`** (`1280 × 1280`, `mAP50 = 0.975`, `mAP50-95 = 0.840`, `mIoU = 0.872`) and documents extreme long-tail recall degradation (`91: R = 0.565` vs `>= 0.935` for regular teeth) and titanium hardware false positives (`Fig. 5F`).
- **Critical Pipeline & Evaluation Design Safeguards (`G2 / G3 / G4 / S12`):** Reinforces four strict rules for our own two-stage / multi-label OPG architecture: (1) evaluate Stage 2 **end-to-end on Stage-1 predicted crops** on strictly **patient-disjoint OPG test splits** (never random crop-level splitting), (2) report **natural-prevalence PR/F1** alongside balanced sets, (3) support **multi-label states** so caries under restorations/RCTs is not erased, and (4) avoid **vertical flips** during OPG training.
