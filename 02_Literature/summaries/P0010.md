## Verified citation

Rahimi, H., Naeimi, S. M., Darvish, S., Nazemi Salman, B., Razzaghi, P., Luchian, I., & Budala, D. G. (2026). Detection of Pediatric Dental Caries in Panoramic Radiograph Using Deep Learning: A Benchmark Study on MD-OPG. *Sensors*, 26(8), 2481. https://doi.org/10.3390/s26082481 (PDF p. 1).

## question/task/modality

- **Modality:** 2D pediatric mixed-dentition panoramic radiographs (`OPGs`, `1920 × 1535` px PNGs, ages `3–12` years, mean age `7.2`, pp. 1, 4–5, Table 1).
- **Task:** Introduces the public **`MD-OPG` (Mixed Dentition Orthopantomogram Dataset)** and benchmarks two non-YOLO formulations on cropped **`Smile Zone` (`Z`)** dental crown regions (pp. 1, 4–12):
  1. *Patch-based Binary Classification (`pp. 5–10`):* Classifying `64 × 64` sliding-window patches (stride `32`, Algorithm 1, p. 6) as normal (`0`) vs caries-bearing (`1`, pooling proximal `P` and occlusal `O` caries) using `ResNet-18`.
  2. *Semantic Segmentation (`pp. 7–8, 10–12`):* Pixel-level caries segmentation on zero-padded `440 × 1000` `Smile Zone` images using a lightweight 4-stage `U-Net` and 5-stage `Attention U-Net` under `Dice Loss`, `Focal Loss`, and `DiceFocal Loss`.
- **Group Reclassification (`OS §3.2`):** Reclassified from `Core-GroupA` to **`Core-GroupB`** because no bounding-box object detector (`YOLO`/`Faster R-CNN`/`DETR`) or detection `mAP` is evaluated; retained in Core because `MD-OPG` is an open Zenodo dataset (`doi:10.5281/zenodo.17786082`) with JSON caries polygons convertible to bounding boxes.

## data/counts

- **Dataset size & provenance (`pp. 4–5, 15, Table 1`):** **`170` pediatric OPGs** (`50%` female / `50%` male, ages `3–12` years, mean `7.2`, Persian and Turkic ethnicity) acquired `2022–2024` at a single private dental clinic in Zanjan, Iran (Zanjan University of Medical Sciences Ethics Ref `IR.ZUMS.REC.1404.242`).
- **Lesion counts (`Table 1`, p. 5):** **`895` annotated caries polygons** across the `170` OPGs:
  - `P (Proximal caries)`: **`716` (`80%`)**
  - `O (Occlusal caries)`: **`179` (`20%`)**
  - `Z (Smile Zone)`: `170` rectangular bounding boxes (`[xmin, ymin, xmax, ymax]`, `2:1` aspect ratio) isolating erupted tooth crowns.
- **Public availability (`p. 15`):** Dataset released on **Zenodo (`https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17786082`)** with code on **GitHub (`https://github.com/IASBS-AI-LAB/MD-OPG/tree/main`)**.

## annotation

- **Workflow (`p. 4`):** Initially annotated with JSON polygons (`P`, `O`) and rectangular `Smile Zone` (`Z`) boxes by two trained mid-level annotators, then reviewed and validated via consensus by two experienced dental specialists.
- **Inter-annotator agreement (`kappa`):** `not reported`.

## split

- **Patch classification split (`p. 9`):** Explicitly partitioned **at the patient/OPG level prior to `64 × 64` patch extraction** to prevent data leakage: `20` OPGs held out as an independent test set, and the remaining `150` OPGs evaluated via 5-fold cross-validation.
- **Segmentation split (`pp. 10–11`):** `40` OPG `Smile Zone` images reserved for testing; the remaining `130` images augmented (`shift-scale`, `rotation`, `horizontal flip`) to `390` training samples. No separate validation set is reported for segmentation (`Tables 3–4` report test-set Dice across epochs `0–100` and `71–100`).

## model

- **Patch Classifier (`pp. 6–7`):** `ResNet-18` (ImageNet pretrained backbone + global average pooling + dense ReLU layers + dropout + 2-class softmax).
- **Segmentation Models (`pp. 7, 10–11`):**
  - `Modified U-Net`: `4` encoder and `4` decoder stages (`1` encoder/decoder removed to reduce overfitting on `N = 170`), kernel size `2`, filters `64 -> 512`, bilinear upsampling.
  - `Attention U-Net`: `5` encoder and `4` decoder stages with Attention Gates (`AG`, Eqs. 1–3, p. 7), kernel size `3 × 3`, filters reduced to `32 -> 512` due to GPU VRAM limits (`p. 11`).
- **Parameter counts and `GFLOPs`:** `not reported`.

## preprocessing

- **Classification (`pp. 6, 9`):** Crop to `Smile Zone` rectangle `Z`, extract `64 × 64` patches with stride `32` (Algorithm 1), and augment abnormal (caries) patches to balance classes.
- **Segmentation (`p. 10`):** Crop to `Smile Zone` `Z` and zero-pad to the maximum `Smile Zone` dimension (`440 × 1000` pixels) without aspect-ratio distortion. No `CLAHE` or contrast filtering applied.

## training

- **Segmentation hyperparameters (`pp. 10–11`):** `100` epochs, batch size `8`, learning rate `0.0001`, `Adam` optimizer, evaluated under `Dice Loss` (Eq. 5), `Focal Loss` (Eq. 6), and `DiceFocal Loss` (Eq. 7). Classification training epochs, batch size, and learning rate: `not reported`.

## metrics/IoU

- **Classification (`Table 2`, p. 10):** Patch-level Accuracy, ROC-AUC, Precision, Recall (Sensitivity), F1-score, and Matthews Correlation Coefficient (`MCC`).
- **Segmentation (`Tables 3–4`, pp. 11–12):** Pixel-level Dice Score (`DS`, mean ± SD over epochs `0–100` and `71–100`, `95% CI`, `Best DS`, and `Last DS` at epoch `100`).
- **Object detection metrics (`IoU`, `mAP@0.5`, `mAP@0.5:0.95`):** `not reported`.

## timing/hardware

- **Software (`p. 9`):** Python `3.11`, Keras, PyTorch, OpenCV.
- **GPU/CPU hardware and inference speed (`FPS` / ms per image):** **`not reported`** (only notes "GPU VRAM limitations" on p. 11).

## results with locators

- **Patch-Level Binary Caries Classification (`Table 2`, p. 10 — `5-Fold CV` / `Independent Test Set`):**
  - Accuracy: `0.784 ± 0.022` / **`0.790`**
  - AUC: `0.879 ± 0.011` / **`0.899`**
  - Precision: `0.313 ± 0.033` / **`0.318`**
  - Recall (Sensitivity): `0.818 ± 0.024` / **`0.857`**
  - F1-score: `0.452 ± 0.033` / **`0.464`**
  - MCC: `0.413 ± 0.024` / **`0.436`**.
- **`U-Net` Segmentation on `40` Test Smile-Zone Images (`Table 3`, p. 11 — `Last 30 Epochs Mean DS [95% CI]` / `Best DS` / `Last Epoch 100 DS`):**
  - `Dice Loss`: `0.7664 ± 0.0566 [0.7453, 0.7876]` / `0.8604` / `0.8344`
  - **`Focal Loss`:** **`0.8991 ± 0.0048 [0.8973, 0.9009]`** / **`0.9093`** / `0.8923`
  - `DiceFocal Loss`: `0.8808 ± 0.0193 [0.8736, 0.8880]` / `0.9071` / `0.8921`.
- **`Attention U-Net` Segmentation on `40` Test Smile-Zone Images (`Table 4`, p. 12 — `Last 30 Epochs Mean DS [95% CI]` / `Best DS` / `Last Epoch 100 DS`):**
  - **`Dice Loss`:** **`0.9314 ± 0.0071 [0.9287, 0.9340]`** / **`0.9461`** / `0.9179`
  - `Focal Loss`: `0.9076 ± 0.0038 [0.9062, 0.9090]` / `0.9147` / `0.9067`
  - `DiceFocal Loss`: `0.9215 ± 0.0047 [0.9198, 0.9232]` / `0.9343` / `0.9208`.

## limitations

- **Author-stated (`p. 14`):** (1) Small dataset size (`N = 170` OPGs); (2) single-clinic provenance (Zanjan, Iran); (3) no human reader study comparing model output against dentists; (4) severe class imbalance between proximal (`716`, `80%`) and occlusal (`179`, `20%`) caries forced binary merging (`normal` vs `abnormal`) rather than subtype classification.

## critique

- **Valuable open dataset (`MD-OPG`, Zenodo `doi:10.5281/zenodo.17786082`), but exhibits three important methodological caveats (`OS §3.2`):**
  1. **Patch classifier precision collapse (`Table 2`, p. 10):** While `ResNet-18` achieves `Recall = 0.857` and `AUC = 0.899`, its **`Precision = 0.318` and `F1 = 0.464`** prove that sliding `64 × 64` patch classification generates بیش از `2` false-positive patches for every true caries patch — demonstrating why end-to-end object detection (`YOLO`) is preferable to naive patch classification.
  2. **Ground-truth `Smile Zone` dependency & test-set epoch tracking (`pp. 10–12`):** Segmentation models were evaluated on `Smile Zone` crops extracted using ground-truth `Z` coordinates, and `Tables 3–4` report `Best DS` and `Mean DS` across test-set epochs `0–100` without a held-out validation set for checkpoint selection.
  3. **Internal text-vs-table contradiction (`p. 1 vs p. 12`):** Abstract (`p. 1`) and Fig. 4 caption (`p. 14`) state that `Attention U-Net` with `Focal Loss` achieved the best Dice score of `0.94`, whereas `Table 4` (`p. 12`) reports `Best DS = 0.9461` (`Mean 0.9314`) under **`Dice Loss`** and `0.9147` (`Mean 0.9076`) under `Focal Loss`.

## reuse

## conflicts
