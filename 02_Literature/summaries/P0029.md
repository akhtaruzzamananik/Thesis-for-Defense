## 1. Problem & Clinical / Technical Motivation
- **Limitations of global OPG enhancement (`Section 1–2, Table 1`, pp. 1–5):** Panoramic radiographs suffer from inter-image exposure variability, intra-tooth contrast variation across enamel/pulp/periodontal ligament, background noise in bone/air spaces, and soft boundaries. Applying global `CLAHE` with a fixed clip limit or global Fast Fourier Transform (`FFT`) across the entire OPG amplifies non-dental background noise and causes spectral mixing between tooth and background regions (`pp. 2, 5`).
- **Objective (`pp. 2–3, 5`):** Propose **`ToothLens`**, a two-stage leak-free framework that localizes a pixel-level tooth Region of Interest (`ROI`) via a Stage-1 `Mask R-CNN`, applies a four-module ROI-restricted spatial–frequency–edge enhancement pipeline (`ROI decomposition -> Entropy-driven adaptive CLAHE -> ROI-constrained Gaussian FFT -> Regularized Canny edge refinement`), and performs final tooth instance segmentation with a Stage-2 `Mask R-CNN`.

## 2. Dataset, Annotation Protocol & Experimental Setup
- **Primary internal cohort (`Section 3`, pp. 6–8, `Tables 2–3`):**
  - **Source:** **`110` OPG radiographs** (`1` OPG per patient, age `18–28` years, routine checkups) from **A.B. Shetty Memorial Institute of Dental Sciences (`ABSMIDS`), Mangalore, India** (NITTE Ethics exemption `CEC-250`, Ref `NU/CEC/2024/688`, 30 Oct 2024). Sample size (`N = 110`) determined via Walter et al. (`1998 [47]`) binomial reliability formula ($R_0 = 0.9798, R_1 = 0.99, \alpha = 0.05, \text{power} = 0.95, k = 2$, `p. 6`).
  - **Strict exclusion criteria (`p. 6`):** Excluded OPGs with dental caries, root distortion, bone disorders, craniofacial abnormalities, chronic periodontal diseases, unerupted teeth, dilaceration, and high noise/artifacts (notably, `Fig. 2d`, p. 8 still displays an `"Unerupted teeth OPG"`).
  - **Annotation & QA (`pp. 7–8`):** Annotated in `LabelMe` (single class: `1 = tooth`, polygon masks with manually split boundaries between overlapping teeth) by 1 computer-science researcher (`B.E., M.Tech`) under supervision of 2 orthodontists (`MDS`). Annotations exceeding a `15%` inter-operator variability threshold were adjudicated by consensus; **pre-adjudication masks were not retained (`pp. 7, 38`), so formal Cohen's $\kappa$ or ICC was not reported**.
  - **5-Fold Cross-Validation & `8×` Augmentation (`pp. 13–18, Tables 2–3`):** `88` training + `22` unaugmented validation OPGs per fold. Each training OPG is augmented `8×` (`horizontal flip, shift=0.05/scale=0.1/rotate, brightness/contrast, Gaussian noise var=5–15, Gaussian blur, elastic transform, grid distortion limit=0.08, gamma=85–115`), yielding **`792` training pairs (`88` original + `704` augmented)** and **`22` raw validation OPGs** per fold.
- **External zero-shot cohort (`p. 28, Table 19`):** **`30` randomly selected OPGs** and masks from the **Tufts Dental Database**.

## 3. Methodology: Four-Module `ToothLens` Enhancement & Two-Stage `Mask R-CNN` (`pp. 8–18`)
1. **Stage-1 Raw OPG ROI Localization (`p. 8`):** `Detectron2 Mask R-CNN` (`ResNet-50-FPN`, COCO weights, `batch size = 1`, `LR = 0.00025`, `2,000` iterations [`3,500` in retrained leak-free Stage-1], `ROI batch = 32`, `score threshold = 0.5`, Tesla T4 GPU) predicts out-of-fold binary tooth masks merged into a single ROI mask $M(x,y) \in \{0, 255\}$.
2. **Module 1 — Mask Normalization & Lossless ROI Decomposition (`Eqs. 3–6`, pp. 8–9):** Normalizes $\text{OPGMask}(x,y) = M(x,y)/255 \in \{0,1\}$ and separates $\text{OPGTooth}(x,y) = \text{OPG}(x,y)\cdot\text{OPGMask}(x,y)$ from $\text{OPGBg}(x,y) = \text{OPG}(x,y)\cdot(1-\text{OPGMask}(x,y))$.
3. **Module 2 — Entropy-Driven Adaptive `CLAHE` (`Eqs. 7–12`, pp. 9–10, `Tables 22–25`, pp. 30–31):**
   - Computes global grayscale Shannon entropy $H(I) = -\sum p_i \log_2 p_i$ (`Eq. 7`), normalizes $H_{\text{norm}} = H(I)/8 \in [0,1]$ (`Eq. 8`), and sets the `CLAHE` clip limit via inverse linear mapping (`Eq. 9`):
     $$\text{Clip} = C_{\min} + (1 - H_{\text{norm}})(C_{\max} - C_{\min}), \quad C_{\min} = 1.0, \; C_{\max} = 5.0$$
   - Low-entropy (flat/underexposed) OPGs receive stronger contrast enhancement ($\text{Clip} \to 5.0$), while high-entropy OPGs receive milder enhancement ($\text{Clip} \to 1.0$).
   - Uses an **`8×8` tile grid** (`Table 25`, p. 31: at `Clip = 3.0`, `8×8` tiles maximize entropy at `7.5032` with low blockiness `4.278`, `PSNR = 30.664 dB, SSIM = 0.9894`, compared to `2×2, 4×4, 16×16, 32×32, 64×64`).
   - Applies `CLAHE` exclusively to `OPGTooth` (`Eq. 10`) and fixed gamma correction ($\gamma = 0.6$, `Eq. 11`) to `OPGBg`.
4. **Module 3 — ROI-Constrained Gaussian `FFT` (`Eqs. 13–19`, pp. 11–12, `Tables 26–28`, pp. 33–34):**
   - Applies 2D FFT to the tooth ROI, filters with Gaussian low-pass $H_{lp}(u,v) = \exp(-D^2/2\sigma_{lp}^2)$ and complementary high-pass $H_{hp} = 1 - H_{lp}$ (`optimal high-pass cutoff = 30`, `Table 27`, p. 34: `PSNR = 17.72 dB, SSIM = 0.944`), combines $I_{HT} = \alpha I_{hp} + (1-\alpha)I_{lp}$, and masks the output back into the tooth ROI (`Eq. 19`).
5. **Module 4 — Regularized Canny Edge Refinement (`Eqs. 20–21`, pp. 12–13):**
   - Extracts smoothed Canny edges ($T_{\text{low}} = 50, T_{\text{high}} = 150$) inside the tooth ROI and blends conservatively (`Eq. 21`): $T_e(x,y) = \alpha T_f(x,y) + (1-\alpha)C_{\text{smooth}}(x,y)$ with **`\alpha = 0.92`** (`8%` edge contribution).

### 4.2 Sensitivity to ROI-Mask Granularity (`Table 13`, p. 26) & Quality (`Table 15`, p. 26)
- **Granularity (`Table 13`):** Pixel-level `Predicted-ROI` achieved `IoU = 0.7974 ± 0.0432`. Downsampling the ROI mask `8×` (`IoU = 0.7842 ± 0.0468, d = 1.044, p < 0.001`) and `16×` (`IoU = 0.7018 ± 0.0509, d = 3.238, p < 0.001`) degraded accuracy, while using a **Bounding-Box-only ROI (`BBox-only ROI`) caused catastrophic failure (`IoU = 0.1489 ± 0.1259`, $\Delta\text{IoU} = -0.6485$, `Cohen's d = 4.778, p < 0.001`)**.
- **Stage-1 to Stage-2 Correlation (`p. 26, Table 15`):** Per-image Stage-1 and Stage-2 `IoU` strongly correlate ($r = 0.9925, \rho = 0.9882, p < 0.001$); blending predicted and GT masks (`0.0` to `1.0`) monotonically raised Stage-2 `IoU` from `0.7974` (`ROI IoU = 0.8061`) to `0.9505` (`ROI IoU = 0.9985`; $r = 0.9776, \rho = 0.9801, p < 0.001$).

### 4.3 `ROI-FFT` vs. `Global FFT` (`Table 26`, pp. 33–34) & Six-Config Ablation (`Table 30`, p. 36)
- **`ROI-FFT` vs. `Global FFT` (`Table 26`):** Within the tooth ROI, `ROI-FFT` achieved higher `PSNR` (`19.587` vs. `18.511 dB`), higher `SSIM` (`0.951` vs. `0.905`), and lower `MSE` (`715.089` vs. `916.153`). In the background, `ROI-FFT` preserved zero distortion (`Background MSE = 0.000, MAE = 0.000, SSIM = 1.000`), whereas `Global FFT` severely corrupted the background (`Background MSE = 9461.234, MAE = 89.509, SSIM = 0.522`).
- **Module-Wise Ablation (`Table 30`, p. 36, single unified run under deployable predicted-ROI protocol):**
  - `C1` (**Baseline `Mask R-CNN`, no enhancement**): `PSNR = ∞, SSIM = 1.000, Entropy = 7.246, SNR = 226.86` -> **`IoU = 0.8173, Dice = 0.8993, Precision = 0.9126, Recall = 0.8877`**.
  - `C2` (`Standard CLAHE, fixed clip = 3.0`): `PSNR = 26.587 dB, SSIM = 0.9831, MSE = 142.69` -> `IoU = 0.8104, Dice = 0.8951, P = 0.9132, R = 0.8793`.
  - `C3` (`Entropy-driven Adaptive CLAHE`): `PSNR = 26.663 dB, SSIM = 0.9834, MSE = 140.21` -> `IoU = 0.8134, Dice = 0.8969, P = 0.9104, R = 0.8853`.
  - `C4` (`Entropy CLAHE + Global FFT`): `PSNR = 16.573 dB, SSIM = 0.9011, MSE = 1431.57, SNR = -11.395` -> `IoU = 0.8005, Dice = 0.8890, P = 0.9058, R = 0.8743` (worst config).
  - `C5` (`Entropy CLAHE + ROI-Adaptive Gaussian FFT`): `PSNR = 16.52 dB, SSIM = 0.9271, MSE = 1365.11, SNR = -11.091` -> `IoU = 0.8131, Dice = 0.8886, P = 0.9108, R = 0.8863`.
  - `C6` (`Full Proposed Pipeline + Augmentation`): `PSNR = 16.811 dB, SSIM = 0.9295, MSE = 1355.05, SNR = -11.088` -> `IoU = 0.8151, Dice = 0.8980, P = 0.9119, R = 0.8866`.

### 4.4 External Zero-Shot Generalization on Tufts (`Table 19`, p. 28)
- Tested zero-shot on **`30` unseen Tufts OPGs**, `IoU` dropped from `79.74%` to **`69.48%` (`-10.26%`)**, `Dice/F1` dropped from `88.66%` to **`81.90%` (`-6.76%`)**, and `Recall` dropped from `87.00%` to **`71.90%` (`-15.10%`)**, while `Precision` rose from `90.74%` to **`95.45%` (`+4.71%`)**.

## 5. Limitations & Reviewer Methodological Critique (`OS §3.2`)
### 5.1 Author-Stated Limitations (`pp. 26–28, 37–38`)
1. **Stage-2 bottlenecked by Stage-1 ROI localization error (`pp. 26–28`):** Under the original deployable protocol, Stage-2 `IoU` (`0.7974`) is slightly lower than the raw Stage-1 baseline (`0.8061`), and under the retrained fully leak-free protocol (`0.8105` vs. `0.8095`), the `+0.0010` `IoU` margin is not statistically significant (`p. 27`).
2. **No formal inter-rater reliability metric (`p. 38`):** Pre-adjudication masks were not saved and the basis of the `15%` variability threshold was not documented, preventing post-hoc Cohen's $\kappa$ or `ICC` calculation.
3. **Single-hospital cohort (`N = 110`) & preliminary external validation (`N = 30`, `pp. 28, 38`):** Ablation runs (`C1–C6`) were also executed only once (`p. 37`), with differences comparable to run-to-run variance.

### 5.2 Reviewer Methodological Critique (`OS §3.2` Audit)
1. **Enhancement does NOT outperform the unenhanced `Mask R-CNN` baseline under fair deployable conditions (`Tables 16, 17, 30`):**
   - In `Table 17` (`p. 27`), the original deployable pipeline (`IoU = 0.7974`) is **statistically significantly worse** than the raw unenhanced `Mask R-CNN` (`IoU = 0.8061`, `Wilcoxon W = 1.0, p < 0.001, Cohen's d = -1.596`).
   - In `Table 16` (`p. 27`), the retrained leak-free pipeline (`IoU = 0.8105, Dice = 0.8953`) gains only **`+0.0010` IoU (`+0.0005` Dice)** over the retrained unenhanced Stage-1 `Mask R-CNN` (`IoU = 0.8095, Dice = 0.8948`).
   - In `Table 30` (`p. 36`), the unenhanced baseline `C1` (`IoU = 0.8173, Dice = 0.8993`) outperforms **every single enhanced configuration (`C2–C6`, max `IoU = 0.8151` in `C6`)**!
2. **`7.9×` inference time penalty (`Table 14`, p. 26):** The two-stage pipeline + CPU enhancement requires **`3,366.7 ± 519.6 ms/image`** (`2,457.5 ms` on CPU enhancement + two `Mask R-CNN` passes) vs. **`426.0 ± 30.9 ms/image`** for single-pass raw `Mask R-CNN` — nearly `8×` slower for zero statistically significant deployable segmentation improvement.
3. **Internal tables & bibliography inconsistencies:**
   - *Oracle Upper-Bound contradiction:* Reported as `IoU = 0.9507, Dice = 0.9746` in `Tables 12, 18, 20`, as `IoU = 0.9420, Dice = 0.9702` in `Table 16`, and as `IoU = 0.9505` in `Table 15`.
   - *Swapped columns in `Table 26` (`p. 33`):* The `"Background PSNR (dB)"` row lists `8.371` under `ROI FFT` (which has `Background MSE = 0.000`) and `∞ (zero error)` under `Global FFT` (`Background MSE = 9461.234`).
   - *Learning rate & exclusion contradictions:* `p. 17` reports base `LR = 0.000235`, while `Table 3` (`p. 18`) reports `0.00025`; `p. 6` excludes unerupted teeth, while `Fig. 2d` (`p. 8`) shows an `"Unerupted teeth OPG"` in the dataset.
   - *Duplicate/mismatched references (`p. 38`):* Refs `15` and `16` are duplicates of `Kalita et al. (2025)` (while `Gamara et al. [15]` cited on `p. 4` is missing), and Ref `13` is cited on `p. 4` as `Walid Brahmi et al.` (`107` OPGs) but listed in References and `Table 32` as `Widyaningrum et al. (2022)`.

## 6. Relevance to Our Thesis (`STEP-007` Synthesis Links)
- **Empirical benchmark for our `CLAHE` parameter selection (`G2 / S7`):** Provides systematic grid-search tables across `CLAHE` clip limits `0.5–10.0` (`Table 22`, showing over-enhancement and noise amplification above `clip = 5.0` and optimal stability in `1.0–5.0`) and tile grids `2×2` to `64×64` (`Table 25`, confirming **`8×8` tiles** achieve peak Shannon entropy `7.5032` with low blockiness).
- **Crucial negative/cautionary finding on two-stage ROI enhancement & BBox masking (`G2 / G3`):** Proves that (a) applying frequency/contrast enhancement inside rectangular bounding boxes (`BBox-only ROI`) causes catastrophic boundary artifacts (`IoU = 0.1489`), and (b) when a deep network (`Mask R-CNN`) is properly trained with data augmentation (`8×`), complex multi-stage `CLAHE + FFT + Canny` preprocessing adds `7.9×` latency (`3.37 s/img`) without outperforming raw baseline segmentation (`0.8105` vs. `0.8095` IoU in `Table 16`; `0.8151` vs. `0.8173` in `Table 30`). This directly motivates testing lightweight global/adaptive `CLAHE` against raw inputs in an honest ablation (`S7`) rather than assuming preprocessing always improves modern detectors.
