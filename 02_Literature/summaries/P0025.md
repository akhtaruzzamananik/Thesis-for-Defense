## 1. Problem & Clinical / Technical Motivation
- **Clinical motivation (pp. 1–4, 9–10):** Overworked or novice dentists can make costly treatment-planning errors on 2D panoramic radiographs (`OPGs`), including wrong-tooth extraction (`21.1%` prevalence cited from `Jan et al., 2019 [50]`, `p. 9`). While most dental AI studies detect existing restorations or single pathologies, very few directly predict **needed multi-stage dental treatment plans** across both diseased teeth and edentulous (missing-tooth) spaces.
- **Technical problem (pp. 3–4, 9–10):** Prior treatment-planning frameworks such as `DENTECT` (`Yüksel et al., 2021 [37]`) covered only 5 first-stage treatments on diseased teeth (`mAP = 59.0%`) and ignored missing-tooth prosthetic/implant planning and multi-stage follow-up treatments (e.g., Stage 1 extraction -> Stage 2 implant -> Stage 3 crown/bridge).
- **Objective (pp. 1–4, 10, Table 1):** Formulate autonomous **8-class, 3-stage dental treatment planning** as an object detection task on panoramic radiographs (`OPGs`) using **`YOLOv7`** (and comparing against **`YOLOv6n`** and **`YOLOv8n`**, `Table 4`, p. 28).

## 2. Dataset, Annotators & Splits
- **Cohort & curation pipeline (`Section 1`, p. 2; `Section 4.1.1`, p. 15; `Section 4.1.2`, p. 16):**
  - **Initial collection (`p. 15`):** `~1,200` anonymized multi-clinic panoramic radiographs scanned from electronic medical records in Iran (`Quds Hospital Polyclinic` acknowledged on `p. 32`).
  - **Quality & age filtering (`p. 15`):** Excluded unclear images and deciduous/mixed-dentition images (retaining male and female patients **aged `> 14` years**), and cropped out the maxillary sinus region (`p. 15`), leaving `835` OPGs.
  - **Clinical eligibility screening (`p. 16`):** **`687` original OPGs** were selected as medically eligible for treatment-plan labeling.
- **Pre-split data augmentation & Stratified 4-Fold CV (`Sections 1, 4.2.1, 4.2.3`, pp. 2, 16–17):**
  - In `Roboflow`, random brightening, darkening, noise injection, and horizontal/vertical flips (plus `CutMix` and `CutBlur` regularization, `pp. 14, 17`) expanded the `687` labeled OPGs to **`1,025` OPGs** (`p. 2, 16`). Following brightness/contrast augmentation, the dentist re-inspected augmented images to verify whether altered contrast changed the required root/restoration treatment (`pp. 16–17`).
  - **Stratified 4-Fold Cross-Validation (`p. 17`):** The `1,025` images were partitioned into `4` stratified folds (`205` test images held out per fold, with the remaining images split `80:20` into training and validation sets; metrics averaged across 4 runs, `p. 17`).
- **Expert annotation & 8 treatment classes (`Section 3, Table 1`, p. 10; `Section 4.1.2`, p. 16; `Table 3`, p. 20):**
  - Annotated with bounding boxes in **`MakeSense`** (`YOLO` `.txt` format) by a **senior dentist with `> 25` years of experience** (`Dr. Mohammad Rashidi Ranjbar`, Head of Dental Unit, Quds Hospital Polyclinic) in consultation with a team of dentists (`pp. 1, 16, 32`). Inter-annotator kappa (`κ`) is `not reported`.
  - **Multi-stage treatment logic (`Table 1`, p. 10):** A single damaged tooth or edentulous gap can receive sequential treatment boxes across up to 3 stages (e.g., `Cavities` -> Stage 1 `Restoration` or `Endo` -> Stage 2 `Crown/Bridge`; `Untreatable tooth` -> Stage 1 `Conventional Extraction` -> Stage 2 `Implant` or `Bridge` -> Stage 3 `Crown/Bridge`).
  - **Instance distribution across `8` treatment classes (`Table 3`, p. 20 — `2,799` total instances):**
    1. `Implant` (required implant in edentulous/post-extraction space): **`552` instances (`19.7%`)**
    2. `Crown` (required crown): **`521` instances (`18.6%`)**
    3. `Endo` (`Root canal treatment and filling`): **`492` instances (`17.6%`)**
    4. `Extraction` (`Conventional extraction`): **`457` instances (`16.3%`)**
    5. `Filling` (`Restoration`): **`452` instances (`16.1%`)**
    6. `Bridge` (required bridge prosthesis): **`181` instances (`6.5%`)**
    7. `Surgical extraction` (impacted/wisdom tooth surgical extraction): **`82` instances (`2.9%`)**
    8. `Reendo` (`Root canal retreatment and filling`): **`62` instances (`2.2%`)**

## 3. Methodology & Experimental Design
- **Preprocessing & regularization (`Sections 3.3, 4.2`, pp. 14, 16–17):**
  - Cropped sinus region (`p. 15`) and resized all OPGs to **`640 × 640` pixels** (`p. 17`).
  - Regularization: Batch normalization, `AutoDrop`, `CutMix`, `CutBlur`, and `EarlyStopping` (`patience = 7` validation checks, `p. 14`).
- **Model architectures & training (`Sections 3.2, 5.1, 5.3`, pp. 12–13, 18, 22, 28, Table 2):**
  - Primary model: **Pretrained `YOLOv7`** (`E-ELAN` backbone, compound model scaling, module-level re-parameterization, coarse-to-fine auxiliary + lead head, `SiLU` activation; `bbox loss + cls loss + DFL loss`, `pp. 12–13, 23–25`).
  - Comparator models (`Table 4`, p. 28): **`YOLOv6n`** and **`YOLOv8n`** trained on the same `1,025`-image dataset with identical hyperparameters.
  - **Hardware & hyperparameters (`Table 2`, p. 18):** Trained on **Google Colaboratory Pro** (`NVIDIA Tesla K80, P100, or T4` GPU, `32 GB` RAM), `batch size = 16`, `image size = 640 × 640`, **`680` epochs** (`Table 2`, p. 18 and `Fig. 9`, p. 22; note `p. 28` line 1046 contains a digit-transposition typo `"860 epochs"`).

## 5. Limitations
### 5.1 Author-Stated Limitations (`Section 6`, pp. 28–31; `Section 7`, pp. 31–32)
1. **Difficulty of localizing future `Implant` and `Bridge` treatments on empty edentulous spaces (`pp. 27–30`):** Unlike existing metallic implants, predicting *needed* `Implant` treatment requires placing a bounding box over an empty alveolar bone gap (`TP = 55%, Background FN = 44%, AP@0.5 = 0.524`), which frequently blends into the background when no tooth is present to extract (`p. 28`).
2. **Inter-dentist subjectivity in treatment planning (`pp. 30–31`):** Clinicians often disagree on borderline teeth (e.g., whether a root-canal-treated tooth with a fractured crown should receive a `Crown` or `Conventional Extraction`).
3. **Colab GPU session time limits (`pp. 18, 22, 30`):** Training had to be stopped at `680` epochs before the loss and `mAP` curves fully plateaued (`Fig. 9`, p. 22) due to Google Colab Pro runtime limits.
4. **Class imbalance (`pp. 20, 22, 31–32`):** `Surgical extraction` (`82` instances) and `Reendo` (`62` instances) had `< 100` instances compared to `Implant` (`552`) and `Crown` (`521`), and an earlier attempt to pretrain on `2,000` periapical radiographs aggravated class imbalance (`p. 32`).

### 5.2 Reviewer Methodological Critique (`OS §3.2`)
1. **Reporting `Precision = 100%`, `Recall = 96%`, and `F1 = 76%` from three incompatible confidence thresholds (`Abstract`, p. 1; `Figs. 11–13`, pp. 24–25; `Table 4`, p. 28):** Throughout the Abstract (`p. 1`), Introduction (`p. 4`), Results (`pp. 24–25`), `Table 4` (`p. 28`), and Conclusion (`p. 31`), the authors claim `Precision = 100%, Recall = 96%, F1 = 76%` simultaneously. Inspection of `Figs. 11–13` (`pp. 24–25`) proves that:
   - `Precision = 1.00` occurs only at **`confidence = 0.905`** (`Fig. 12`), where Recall approaches `0%`;
   - `Recall = 0.96` occurs only at **`confidence = 0.000`** (`Fig. 13`), where Precision approaches `~10%`;
   - Peak `F1 = 0.76` occurs at **`confidence = 0.319`** (`Fig. 11`), where both Precision and Recall are `~0.75–0.77`.
   Reporting these three curve extrema as simultaneous operating metrics is a fundamental misreading of Ultralytics diagnostic plots.
2. **Pre-split data augmentation leakage (`Sections 1, 4.2.1, 4.2.3`, pp. 2, 16–17):** The `687` labeled OPGs were first augmented (`brightening, darkening, noise, horizontal/vertical flips, CutMix, CutBlur`) to **`1,025` OPGs**, and *then* partitioned via 4-fold stratified cross-validation (`205` test images per fold, `p. 2, 17`), leaking augmented variants of the same patient radiograph across train, validation, and test folds.
3. **`YOLOv8n` outperformed `YOLOv7` by `+7.9% mAP50` and `+9.0% F1` in `Table 4` (`p. 28`) but was ignored in the paper's narrative:** In `Table 4` (`p. 28`), `YOLOv8n` achieved **`mAP50 = 0.898` and `F1 = 0.85`** vs. `YOLOv7`'s `mAP50 = 0.819` and `F1 = 0.76`, yet the title, abstract, and conclusions exclusively promote `YOLOv7`.
4. **Architectural and loss-function misdescriptions (`pp. 12, 23–25`) & missing runtime (`G6`):** `Section 3.1.2` (`p. 12, Fig. 4`) describes `YOLOv1` (`24` conv layers + `2` FC layers, `7 × 7 × 30` output tensor) as the general YOLO architecture, and `Section 5.3` (`pp. 23–25, Fig. 10`) attributes `dfl_loss` (Distribution Focal Loss from YOLOv8) to `Hossain et al., 2021 [65]` (Dual Focal Loss) while describing `YOLOv7` training; no inference latency (`ms/image` or `FPS`) is measured on the dental dataset (`not reported`).

## 6. Relevance to Our Thesis (`STEP-007` Synthesis Links)
- **Key benchmark for treatment-planning detection & `YOLOv6n vs. YOLOv7 vs. YOLOv8n` scaling (`G1` / `G4`):** Demonstrates that `YOLOv8n` (`mAP50 = 0.898, F1 = 0.85`) substantially outperforms `YOLOv7` (`mAP50 = 0.819, F1 = 0.76`) and `YOLOv6n` (`mAP50 = 0.730, F1 = 0.68`) on 8-class panoramic treatment planning (`Table 4`, p. 28).
- **Important clinical insight on "empty-space" bounding boxes (`G2` / `S15`):** Explains why object detectors achieve high accuracy when a physical tooth/pathology is present (`Surgical Extraction AP@0.5 = 0.935`, `Endo 0.893`, `Reendo 0.883`, `Extraction 0.872`, `Filling 0.847`) but struggle when asked to place a bounding box on an *edentulous gap* for future `Implant` planning (`AP@0.5 = 0.524`, `44%` missed as background, `Figs. 14–15`).
