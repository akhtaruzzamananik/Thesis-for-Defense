## 1. Problem & Clinical / Technical Motivation
- **Clinical Modality Gap (`pp. 1–2`):** Periapical lesions (`PLs`, ~`52%` patient-level and `5%` tooth-level global prevalence `[1]`) frequently present silently as apical radiolucencies, yet `2D` orthopantomograms (`OPGs`) suffer from anatomical superimposition, geometric distortion, and the need for `30–50%` mineral bone loss before radiographic visibility (`OPG` sensitivity `28%–48.8%` `[12–15]`).
- **Objective (`p. 2`):** Directly benchmark the commercial cloud AI platform **Diagnocat** against experienced human readers on **same-patient paired `OPG` and `CBCT` scans** using **human consensus on `CBCT`** as the reference standard.

---

## 2. Cohort, Acquisition & Reference Standard Protocol (`pp. 2–4`)
- **IRB & Patient Selection (`pp. 2–4, 11`):** Retrospective single-center cohort from a private dental center in Poland (Ethics Committee of Collegium Medicum, Nicolaus Copernicus University, Torun, `KB 227/2023`; `Jan–Sep 2023`, `IRB date typo 10.04.20223` on `p. 11`):
  - **`92` consecutive patients** with paired OPG + CBCT (`<= 30`-day interval) → excluded `43` (small `CBCT` FOV not covering all present tooth apices) → excluded `6` (motion artifacts / poor image quality) → **`49` included patients (`1,223` total evaluated teeth)**.
  - **Demographics (`p. 4`):** `37` females (`mean 42.11 ± 13.39 yrs`, range `12–65`) and `12` males (`mean 51.75 ± 12.46 yrs`, range `31–70`); text states overall mean `41 yrs` (range `12–70` — arithmetically inconsistent; see Section 4.2).
  - **Sample Size Calculation (`p. 3`):** Buderer formula (`[35]`) assuming `85%` expected sensitivity, `95%` expected specificity, `70%` disease prevalence, `0.1` precision, and `90%` confidence.
- **Scanner & Workstation (`p. 3`):** All OPGs and CBCTs acquired on **MyRay `Hyperion X9 PRO 13 × 16`** (`CBCT Regular: 90 kV, 36 mAs, CTDI/Vol 4.09 mGy, 13 cm / 10 × 13 cm FOV, 0.3 mm` slice thickness; `OPG` exposure parameters `not reported`). Graded on certified **Eizo `RadiForce MX243W`** monitors in **iRYS Viewer v6.2** (`WW = 1048`, `WC = 4096 HU`).
- **Readers & AI (`pp. 3–4`):** Two readers (`1` orthodontist + `1` radiologist, both `> 8 yrs` exp; note Abstract mentions `3` clinicians) graded `OPGs` first, then `CBCT` `>= 1 month` later independently, followed by consensus reading on `CBCT` (`reference standard`). Anonymized OPGs and CBCTs were uploaded separately to cloud **Diagnocat** (`Diagnocat Ltd., San Francisco, CA, USA`; positive threshold **`p > 50%`**; CNN version/architecture `not reported`).

## 3. Quantitative Diagnostic Performance (`pp. 4–8`, `Tables 1–4`, `Figs. 1–4`)

### 3.1 Detected Periapical Lesion (`PL`) Counts (`Table 1`, p. 4; `1,223` Total Teeth)
- **AI Tool:** `OPG = 28` PLs (`2.29%` of teeth) | `CBCT = 23` PLs (`1.88%`)
- **Human Readers' Consensus:** `OPG = 51` PLs (`4.17%`) | **`CBCT = 26` PLs** (`2.13%`, Reference Standard per `Table 1`)

## 4. Limitations & Reviewer Methodological Critique (`OS §3.2`)

### 4.1 Author-Stated Limitations (`p. 10`)
1. Small cohort size (`49` patients), limiting statistical generalizability.
2. Evaluation restricted to a single proprietary commercial AI platform (`Diagnocat`); performance depends on the undisclosed algorithm and training data.
3. Single-pathology focus (`PLs` only; caries, bone loss, cysts, etc. excluded).
4. Single imaging scanner (`MyRay Hyperion X9 PRO`).
5. Low ethnic diversity (exclusively white Polish patients).

### 4.2 Reviewer Methodological Critique (`OS §3.2` Audit)
1. **Arithmetic Contradictions Across `Tables 1–4` and Prose (`pp. 4, 8`):**
   - Exact algebraic verification shows **`Table 2 (CBCT)` corresponds to `TP = 21, FP = 2, FN = 6, TN = 1,194` (`N = 1,223`)**, which yields `21/27 = 77.78%` sensitivity and `21/23 = 91.30%` PPV — directly contradicting **`Table 1` (`CBCT GT = 26`, not `27`)** and **`p. 4` prose (`"four false negative results and one false positive result"` instead of `FN = 6, FP = 2`)**!
   - Likewise, **`Table 2 (OPG)` (`9/27`, `9/28`)**, **`Table 3` (`18/27`, `18/57`)**, and **`Table 4` (`12/57`, `12/28`)** consistently compute metrics using **`27` CBCT positives (vs. `26` in `Table 1`)** and **`57` human OPG positives (vs. `51` in `Table 1`)**, while `Accuracy`, `Specificity`, and `NPV` imply shifting denominators (`N = 1,236–1,239` in `Table 2 OPG`, `1,255–1,258` in `Table 3`, and `1,252–1,253` in `Table 4` instead of `1,223`!).
2. **Prevalence Distortion, Clustered Teeth & Incorporation Bias (`pp. 3–4, 8`):**
   - Only `~2.1%–2.2%` of evaluated teeth (`26–27 / 1,223`) had PLs — far below the `70%` disease prevalence assumed in Buderer's sample-size formula (`p. 3`) — guaranteeing artificially high `Specificity` (`96.8%–99.8%`) and `NPV` (`96.3%–99.5%`) even when `PPV` is ~`32%`.
   - `1,223` teeth from `49` patients are analyzed assuming tooth independence without patient-clustered `95% CIs`; large-FOV CBCT referral criteria (suspected PLs/impacted teeth) introduce spectrum bias; and the same two clinicians produced both the OPG consensus (`Table 3`) and the CBCT reference consensus despite the `1`-month washout. Inter-reader $\kappa$ before consensus is `not reported`.
3. **Internal Text Contradictions & Irrelevant/Swapped References (`pp. 1–4, 9–13`):**
   - **Readers count (`p. 1` vs `p. 3`):** Abstract states **`three experienced clinicians`**, whereas Methods (`Section 2.3`) describes **`two readers` (`1` orthodontist, `1` radiologist)**.
   - **Impossible Overall Mean Age (`p. 4`):** `37` females (`mean 42.11`) and `12` males (`mean 51.75`) yield a true weighted mean of **`44.47 yrs`**, not **`41 yrs`** (`<` both subgroup means!).
   - **Mis-cited References & Figure (`pp. 2, 9, 12–13`):** `p. 2` cites **`[18]` (`Lo et al., 2016`, *J. Vasc. Surg.* on abdominal aortic endoleaks!)** and **`[17]` (`Arias et al., 2019`, soft-tissue facial asymmetry)** as evidence for CBCT endodontic value; `p. 9` cites three **OPG papers `[50–52]`** (`Vinayahalingam 2021`, `Başaran 2022`, `Zhu 2023`) as supporting CBCT AI sensitivity, and mistakenly refers to **`Figure 3`** (OPG FP) instead of **`Figure 4`** (CBCT+OPG metal-artifact FN).
4. **Missing Technical Transparency:** Diagnocat version/architecture, OPG acquisition kV/mA/resolution, tooth-matching criteria, and inference speed are `not reported`; dataset is private.

## 5. Relevance to Our Thesis (`STEP-007` Synthesis Links)
- **Empirical Ceiling of `2D` OPG Periapical Screening (`G4`):** Crucial primary benchmark establishing why `2D` OPGs systematically under-detect periapical pathology relative to `3D` CBCT (`Diagnocat OPG Sens = 33.33%, F1 = 32.73%` vs `CBCT Sens = 77.78%, F1 = 84.00%`), while **human OPG clinicians trade higher sensitivity (`66.67%`) for high false-positive rates (`PPV = 31.58%`)**. Justifies framing our OPG detector as a **first-line triage aid** rather than a replacement for periapical radiographs or CBCT, and directly corrects `P0028` (`Lutescu 2026`), which swapped this citation (`[22]`) with Kwiatek et al. (`[21]`).
- **Statistical Rigor Lesson (`G4/G5`):** Reinforces reporting exact `TP/FP/FN/TN` counts per split, keeping denominators consistent (`N = 1,223`), computing **patient-clustered CIs**, and avoiding `Accuracy` overemphasis under `<3%` tooth-level lesion prevalence.
