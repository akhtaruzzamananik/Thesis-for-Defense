## 1. Problem & Clinical / Technical Motivation
- **Clinical & anatomical context (`pp. 1–2`):** The mandibular condyle is the primary osseous component of the temporomandibular joint (`TMJ`), exhibiting high morphological diversity across populations (including Bangladeshi populations, `Anisuzzaman et al., 2019 [2]`, `p. 2`). While `CBCT` is the 3D gold standard, panoramic radiography (`OPG`) is the routine clinical workhorse despite magnification, geometric distortion, and glenoid fossa / articular eminence superimposition (`Arayapisit et al., 2023 [3]`).
- **Literature gap (`p. 2`):** Prior condyle AI studies (`e.g., Abd Elaziz et al., 2025 [7]`) focused on bounding-box ROI extraction + shape classification (`YOLOv8 + MobileViT`) rather than pixel-level boundary segmentation, and no prior dental study had benchmarked `RT-DETR-Seg`, `YOLOv9-Seg`, and `YOLOv11-Seg` head-to-head against two-stage and modern CNN backbones (`Mask R-CNN`, `ConvNeXt`, `EfficientNet`).

## 2. Dataset, Acquisition Protocol & Two-Radiologist Annotation QA (`p. 3`)
- **Single-center cohort (`p. 3`, Ethics: Gülhane Scientific Research Ethics Committee `2025/480`):**
  - **Size:** **`1,300` panoramic radiographs (`2,600` bilateral mandibular condyles)** retrospectively curated from institutional archives in Türkiye.
  - **Scanner & exposure:** **Planmeca ProMax** (`Planmeca, Helsinki, Finland`) operated by a single experienced technician at fixed **`66 kVp`, `8 mA`, and `15.8 s` exposure**; exported in `JPEG` format (`total test-set pixel count in Fig. 1 = 579,731,631 pixels: 574,392,123 background + 5,339,508 condyle pixels [0.921% positive pixels]`).
  - **Exclusions (`p. 3`):** Excluded OPGs with motion artifacts, truncated anatomy, metallic overlap obscuring the TMJ, or pathologies visibly altering condylar morphology (trained/tested on clinically normal condyles, `p. 11`).
  - **Patient-level split (`70 / 15 / 15`, `p. 3`):** Fixed radiograph-level split into **`910` training (`70%`)**, **`195` validation (`15%`)**, and **`195` test (`15%`, `390` condyles)** OPGs.
- **Annotation protocol & Inter-Observer `DSC` (`p. 3`):**
  - Annotated in **`Makesense.ai`** using fine polygonal contours tracing the superior cortical margin, posterior curvature, anterior slope, and medial–lateral extensions (excluding articular eminence, glenoid fossa, and mandibular ramus).
  - Performed by **2 dentomaxillofacial radiologists (`> 8 years` and `> 15 years` experience)**.
  - **Blinded 10% reliability audit (`n = 130` OPGs):** Independently segmented by both radiologists, achieving a **mean inter-observer Dice Similarity Coefficient (`DSC`) of `0.92 ± 0.03`**. Thereafter, the primary radiologist annotated the remaining images and the senior radiologist reviewed all masks with consensus adjudication.

## 3. Evaluated Architectures, Training Setup & Quantitative Results (`pp. 4–9`, `Tables 1–2`, `Figs. 1–3`)
- **Six segmentation architectures (`p. 4`):**
  1. **`RT-DETR` (`RT-DETR-Seg`):** Hybrid attention-based encoder + NMS-free decoupled detection/mask head (`validation proxy 1 − AP50:95 = 0.604`, `p. 5`).
  2. **`YOLOv9-Seg`:** One-stage instance segmentation with Programmable Gradient Information (`PGI`) and `GELAN` (`1 − AP50:95 = 0.574`).
  3. **`YOLOv11-Seg`:** One-stage segmentation with dynamic spatial feature alignment (`1 − AP50:95 = 0.565`).
  4. **`Mask R-CNN`:** Two-stage `RPN + RoIAlign` detection and mask branches.
  5. **`ConvNeXt`:** `Mask R-CNN` with large-kernel `ConvNeXt + FPN` backbone.
  6. **`EfficientNet`:** `Mask R-CNN` with compound-scaled `EfficientNet + FPN` backbone (`trained 300 epochs, min val loss = 0.504 at epoch 11`, `p. 4`).
- **Hardware (`p. 4`):** Dedicated workstation with `Intel Core i9-12900K`, `64 GB RAM`, and **`NVIDIA RTX 3080 Ti (12 GB)`** (`exact input resolution, batch size, learning rate, and model scale variants not reported`).

## 4. Limitations & Reviewer Methodological Critique (`OS §3.2`)
### 4.1 Author-Stated Limitations (`p. 11`)
1. **Single-institution, single-scanner dataset (`Planmeca ProMax`) without external validation.**
2. **Clinically normal condyles only (`p. 3, 11`):** Radiographs with condylar pathology (e.g., osteoarthritis, osteophytes, erosion) were excluded.
3. **Lack of `CBCT` 3D anatomical ground truth (`p. 11`).**
4. **Statistical ceiling (`p. 8, 11`):** `RT-DETR` (`DSC = 0.866`), `YOLOv9-Seg` (`DSC = 0.862, p = 0.178`), and `Mask R-CNN` (`p = 0.734`) showed no statistically significant difference in `Dice` or `IoU`.

### 4.2 Reviewer Methodological Critique (`OS §3.2` Audit)
1. **Severe Mathematical Discrepancies Between `Fig. 1` Pixel Confusion Matrices and `Table 1` / Prose (`pp. 5–7`):**
   - Visual inspection of `Fig. 1` (`p. 6`, total test pixels $= 579,731,631$; true condyle pixels $= 5,339,508$) reveals that for `RT-DETR`, `YOLOv9-Seg`, `ConvNeXt`, and `YOLOv11-Seg`, the `Table 1` "Mean" metrics equal the exact pooled pixel ratios from `Fig. 1`, **but for `Mask R-CNN` and `EfficientNet`, `Fig. 1` and `Table 1` strongly contradict each other**:
     - **`Mask R-CNN` in `Fig. 1` (`TP = 4,663,197, FP = 730,289, FN = 676,311`):** Pooled `Precision = 0.8646`, **`Recall = 0.8733`** (vs. `0.804` in `Table 1`), **`Dice/F1 = 0.8689`** (vs. `0.833` in `Table 1`), and **`IoU = 0.7682`** (vs. `0.714` in `Table 1`) — meaning in `Fig. 1`, `Mask R-CNN` actually has **higher pooled `Dice` (`0.8689` vs. `0.8660`) and `IoU` (`0.7682` vs. `0.7637`) than `RT-DETR`**, which explains why the Wilcoxon test in `Table 2` found `p = 0.734` (`Dice`) and `p = 0.725` (`IoU`)!
     - **`EfficientNet` in `Fig. 1` (`TP = 2,718,919, FP = 422,235, FN = 2,620,589`):** Pooled `Precision = 0.8656`, **`Recall = 0.5092`** (`49.1%` of condyle pixels missed!), **`Dice/F1 = 0.6412`**, and **`IoU = 0.4719`** — yet `Table 1` reports `Recall = 0.801, Dice = 0.831, IoU = 0.710`, and `p. 5` (line 469) falsely describes `EfficientNet` as *"recall-oriented"* while `p. 7` (line 621) calls it *"precision-oriented"*!
   - **Self-contradiction on `YOLOv11-Seg` (`p. 1, 6, 7`):** On `p. 6` (lines 489–491), the authors claim `YOLOv11` *"produced cleaner, more conservative masks with reduced FP and slightly lower recall"*, whereas `Fig. 1` shows `YOLOv11` had the **highest `FP` count of all 6 models (`1,561,111` pixels)** and `Table 1` (`p. 6`) shows it had the **lowest Precision (`0.754`)**!
2. **Validation `AP50:95` vs. Test `Dice` Ranking Reversal (`p. 5` vs. `Table 1`):** On `p. 5` (lines 383–384), the validation error proxy $1 - \text{AP}_{50:95}$ is `0.604` for `RT-DETR` ($\text{AP}_{50:95} = 0.396$), `0.574` for `YOLOv9-Seg` ($\text{AP}_{50:95} = 0.426$), and `0.565` for `YOLOv11-Seg` ($\text{AP}_{50:95} = 0.435$) — meaning on COCO instance `AP50:95`, **`YOLOv11-Seg > YOLOv9-Seg > RT-DETR`**, the exact opposite of the binary pixel `Dice` ranking in `Table 1`!
3. **Complete Omission of Hyperparameters, Model Scales, and Inference Speed (`FPS / ms`):** Despite claiming `"real-time"` feasibility for `YOLOv9-Seg` and `YOLOv11-Seg`, the paper never reports model size variants (`e.g., YOLOv9c-seg vs. YOLOv9e-seg`, `RT-DETR-L vs. X`), input image dimensions, batch size, optimizer, learning rate, or inference latency (`ms/OPG`).

## 5. Relevance to Our Thesis (`STEP-007` Synthesis Links)
- **Direct head-to-head benchmark of `RT-DETR`, `YOLOv9-Seg`, `YOLOv11-Seg`, and `Mask R-CNN` on `1,300` OPGs (`G2 / S10`):** Demonstrates that `RT-DETR` (`DSC = 0.866, P = 0.859, R = 0.873`) and `YOLOv9-Seg` (`DSC = 0.862, P = 0.825, R = 0.902`) achieve statistically tied overlap (`p = 0.178`), with `RT-DETR` favoring higher precision (`p = 0.001`) and `YOLOv9-Seg` favoring higher recall (`p < 0.001`).
- **Exemplary 10% blinded inter-radiologist `DSC` QA protocol (`G3`):** Quantifying inter-observer `DSC = 0.92 ± 0.03` on a random 10% subset (`130` OPGs) prior to full-corpus annotation is a directly reusable QA template for our thesis dataset.
