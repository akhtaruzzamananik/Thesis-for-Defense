## 1. Problem & Clinical / Technical Motivation
- **Clinical context (`pp. 354–355`):** Pediatric mixed-dentition panoramic radiographs (`OPGs`) are critical for monitoring tooth eruption and detecting developmental anomalies such as supernumerary teeth (`mesiodens`), but overlapping primary and permanent tooth germs make manual charting error-prone.
- **Domain-shift gap (`pp. 354–355, 362`):** Most pediatric dental object-detection studies train and test on single-institution cohorts (`e.g., Mine et al., 2022 [8]; Beser et al., 2024 [9]; Peker & Kurtoglu, 2025 [29] = our P0019`). When deployed across institutions with different X-ray machines, patient age distributions, and **imaging protocols (specifically the presence vs. absence of radiographic bite blocks)**, high aggregate `mAP@0.5` can mask severe shifts in class-specific error modes.
- **Objective (`p. 355`):** Quantify bidirectional cross-domain performance and failure-mode shifts between an internal Japanese dataset (**without bite blocks**) and an external Chinese open-source dataset (**with bite blocks**) across **`13` Palmer tooth classes (`A–E, 1–7, S`)**, comparing **`YOLOv8m`** and **`YOLOv10m`**.

## 2. Datasets, Annotation Protocol & Three-Experiment Design (`pp. 355–357`, `Fig. 1`, `Table 1`)
### 2.1 Internal vs. External Cohorts (`Total = 392 OPGs, 16,564 bounding boxes`)
1. **Internal Dataset (`Hiroshima University Hospital, Japan`, Ethics `E-2023-0230`, `p. 355`):**
   - **Size & population:** **`200` pediatric OPGs** (`8,411` annotated tooth boxes), early mixed dentition aged **`6 years 0 months to 9 years 6 months`**.
   - **Scanner & protocol:** **Asahi Roentgen `Hyper-X` or `SOLIO XZ`** (`Kyoto, Japan`), acquired **WITHOUT bite blocks**.
   - **Case composition:** Controlled 1:1 case-control design — **`100` normal OPGs** (no dental anomalies) + **`100` OPGs each with exactly `1` supernumerary tooth (`100` `S` instances total)**; all other anomalies excluded.
2. **External Dataset (`Hangzhou Xiangsha Dental Hospital, China`, open-source `Zhang et al., Sci Data 2023 [24]`, `p. 356`):**
   - **Size & population:** **`192` pediatric OPGs** (`8,153` annotated tooth boxes; `1` manipulated/incomplete image excluded from the original `193`), aged **`2 to 13 years`** (`100` with caries, periodontitis, gingivitis, developmental anomalies, or deep pits/fissures + `93` without supplementary metadata).
   - **Protocol & quality:** Acquired **WITH bite blocks**; no exclusions for anterior unsharpness, head tilt/rotation, or positioning errors. Contains **`16` OPGs each with `1` supernumerary tooth (`16` `S` instances total)**.
3. **ROI Cropping & Annotation (`p. 356`):**
   - **Manual ROI crop:** All `392` OPGs were manually pre-cropped to the rectangular region encompassing the entire mandible and maxillary teeth prior to annotation and training.
   - **13-Class Palmer Notation (`LabelImg v1.8.6`):**Quadrant-agnostic tooth classes: **`A–E`** (primary central incisor to second primary molar), **`1–7`** (permanent central incisor to second permanent molar; third molars `8` excluded), and **`S`** (supernumerary tooth).
   - **Two-Stage Specialist Consensus (`p. 356`):** Annotated by 1 pediatric dentist (`3 years` experience) and reviewed with consensus adjudication by a senior pediatric dentist (`16 years` experience). Formal inter-annotator $\kappa$ or `IoU` is `not reported`.

### 2.2 Three Cross-Domain Experimental Splits (`Fig. 1`, `Table 1`, pp. 355–357)
- **Experiment 1 (Combined Domain-Aligned Split, `n = 392`):** Randomly split (`50%` internal / `50%` external in each subset) into **`136` train (`5,768` boxes; `38` `S`)**, **`60` val (`2,558` boxes; `24` `S`)**, and **`196` test (`8,238` boxes; `54` `S`)**.
- **Experiment 2 (Internal $\to$ External Domain Shift):** Trained/validated (`7:3`) on **Internal (`140` train [`5,916` boxes; `70` `S`] + `60` val [`2,495` boxes; `30` `S`])** -> Tested on **External (`192` test [`8,153` boxes; `16` `S`], with bite blocks)**.
- **Experiment 3 (External $\to$ Internal Domain Shift):** Trained/validated (`7:3`) on **External (`134` train [`5,710` boxes; `10` `S`] + `58` val [`2,443` boxes; `6` `S`])** -> Tested on **Internal (`200` test [`8,411` boxes; `100` `S`], no bite blocks)**.

### 3.2 Domain-Specific Failure Mode Shifts (`pp. 357–362`, `Figs. 5B, 6, 8B`)
1. **Protocol-artifact hallucination (`Exp 2`, `Fig. 6`, p. 360):** When trained on internal OPGs *without* bite blocks and tested on external OPGs *with* bite blocks, both `YOLOv8m` and `YOLOv10m` **falsely detected the radiopaque plastic bite block between the anterior incisors as a Primary Central Incisor (`class A`)**!
2. **Shift in Primary Central Incisor (`class A`) failure mode (`Exp 2 vs. Exp 3`, `pp. 357, 362`):**
   - In **Exp 2 (`Int -> Ext`)**, `YOLOv8m` misclassified **`21%` of true `class A` instances as adjacent Primary Lateral Incisors (`class B`)** (`AP = 0.734`).
   - In **Exp 3 (`Ext -> Int`)**, the primary failure mode for `class A` shifted from inter-tooth confusion to **missed detections (`false negatives` into `Background`)**: **`21%` (`YOLOv8m`, `AP = 0.738`) and `27%` (`YOLOv10m`, `AP = 0.725`)** of true `class A` teeth were missed completely (`p. 358`).
3. **Collapse on rare Supernumerary Teeth (`class S`) under domain shift + training scarcity (`pp. 357–358, 362`):**
   - In **Exp 2 (`70` train / `30` val / `16` test `S`)**, `50%` (`YOLOv8m`, `AP = 0.584`) and `56%` (`YOLOv10m`, `AP = 0.503`) of true `S` teeth were missed as background.
   - In **Exp 3 (`10` train / `6` val / `100` test `S`)**, missed detections surged to **`57%` (`YOLOv8m`, `AP = 0.378`) and `74%` (`YOLOv10m`, `AP = 0.372`)** — even though aggregate `mAP@0.5` remained above `0.90` (`0.906 / 0.901`)!
4. **Architecture parity (`YOLOv8m` vs. `YOLOv10m`, `p. 362`):** `YOLOv8m` slightly outperformed `YOLOv10m` in all three experiments (`0.958 vs. 0.941`, `0.910 vs. 0.906`, `0.906 vs. 0.901`), demonstrating that newer YOLO architectural tweaks alone do not overcome cross-institution domain shift or class imbalance.

## 4. Limitations & Reviewer Methodological Critique (`OS §3.2`)
### 4.1 Author-Stated Limitations (`p. 363`)
1. **Limited institutional diversity:** Evaluated across only two institutions (`Hiroshima` and `Hangzhou`).
2. **Poor sensitivity to rare supernumerary teeth (`class S`):** Requires class-imbalance-aware losses (`Focal Loss [31]`, `Class-Balanced Loss [32]`), oversampling, active learning, or domain adaptation.

### 4.2 Reviewer Methodological Critique (`OS §3.2` Audit)
1. **Transposed Confusion-Matrix Axis Explanation in Text and Captions (`p. 357` & `Figs. 3B, 5B, 8B`, pp. 359–362):**
   - In `Materials and Methods` (`p. 357`) and the captions of `Figs. 3B, 5B, 8B`, the authors state: *"Each row represents the true class, and each column represents the predicted class... The 'background' column represents the rate of missed detections... while the 'background' row represents the rate of false positive detections."*
   - Visual inspection of `Figs. 3B, 5B, 8B` proves the exact opposite: they are standard Ultralytics column-normalized confusion matrices with **`True` on the X-axis (columns)** and **`Predicted` on the Y-axis (rows)**! Thus, the **bottom `"Background"` row** represents missed detections (`FN`), and the **rightmost `"Background"` column** represents background false positives (`FP`).
2. **Uncontrolled Training Asymmetry Between `YOLOv8m` and `YOLOv10m` (`p. 357`):**
   - `YOLOv8m` was trained with initial `LR = 5.0 × 10^-5` on an `RTX 3070 Laptop GPU (8 GB)` with `PyTorch 2.1.2`, whereas `YOLOv10m` was trained with **`2×` higher initial `LR = 1.0 × 10^-4`** on an `RTX 3090 (24 GB)` with `PyTorch 1.12.1`. Neither input image resolution (`imgsz`), batch size, nor data augmentation settings are reported, confounding the architectural comparison.
3. **Manual Pre-Cropping of OPGs (`pp. 355–356`):** All internal and external OPGs were manually cropped to the dentition ROI before training and inference, meaning the pipeline is not fully automated end-to-end on raw full-frame OPGs.
4. **Omission of `mAP@0.5:0.95`, Precision, Recall, F1, and Inference Latency:** Reports only `AP@0.5` and `mAP@0.5`, omitting `mAP@0.5:0.95`, operating-point Precision/Recall/F1, and inference runtime (`ms/image` or `FPS`). Also contains a minor typo on `p. 362` (line 793) attributing `YOLOv10m`'s Exp 3 `class S AP = 0.372` to `YOLOv8` (`0.378` in `Table 4`).

## 5. Relevance to Our Thesis (`STEP-007` Synthesis Links)
- **Direct empirical proof of how aggregate `mAP@0.5` hides clinical failure under domain shift (`G1 / G4`):** Demonstrates that a model with **`mAP@0.5 = 0.906–0.910`** under cross-hospital testing can simultaneously suffer **`50%–74%` missed detections on rare anomalies (`class S`)** and **hallucinate primary incisors on plastic bite-block artifacts (`Fig. 6`)**.
- **Actionable mitigation strategies for our Bangladeshi OPG pipeline (`G2 / G3`):** Motivates (1) documenting imaging-protocol artifacts (`bite blocks, chin rests, positioning markers`) in our dataset metadata, (2) reporting per-class `AP` and confusion-matrix `Background FN / FP` rates alongside overall `mAP`, and (3) using quadrant-specific `FDI` numbering (`32/52` classes, as in `P0019`) rather than quadrant-collapsed Palmer notation (`13` classes).
