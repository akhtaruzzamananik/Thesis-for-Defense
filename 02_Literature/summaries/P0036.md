## 1. Problem & Clinical / Technical Motivation
- **Clinical challenge (`pp. 1–2`):** Dental caries is frequently under-detected on panoramic radiographs (`OPGs`) due to lower spatial resolution, proximal enamel overlap, and panoramic distortion compared with intraoral bitewing (`BW`) radiographs (the gold standard for proximal caries). Furthermore, single-stage full-OPG models miss tiny enamel/dentine lesions that occupy a minuscule fraction of pixels (`p. 14`).
- **Objective (`pp. 2–4`):** Establish a high-reliability **`500`-OPG multistage caries dataset** confirmed against same-day **intraoral bitewing radiographs**, and evaluate a two-stage deep learning pipeline (`YOLOv5s` tooth localization + `Attention U-Net` binary caries segmentation) across enamel, dentine, and pulp-involvement stages.

## 2. Dataset, Bitewing-Confirmed GT & Calibration Protocol (`pp. 2–5`, `Fig. 1`)
- **Cohort & Acquisition (`pp. 2, 5`, IRB `MU-MOU CoA.2022/060.1511`):**
  - **`500` patients (`317` females, mean age `25.7 yrs`; `183` males, mean age `26.2 yrs`)** with **same-day OPG + `>= 4` posterior bitewings** (`Jan–Aug 2022`, Mahidol University, Bangkok, Thailand). Ages `> 13 years`, each patient had `>= 1` BW-confirmed caries and proximal overlap `< outer half of enamel`; excluded jawbone pathology, distortion, full-mouth crowns, brackets, and `>= 2` implants/quadrant.
  - **Scanners:** OPG on **Carestream `CS9000C`** (`68–72 kV, 8–10 mA, 15.1 s`, anonymized `DICOM`); posterior BWs on **Planmeca `ProX`** or **Belmont `PHOT-XIIS 505`** with **VistaScan Mini Plus** (`20 line-pairs/mm`).
- **Two-Stage Calibration & Ground-Truth Labeling (`p. 2`, `Fig. 1`):**
  - **Calibration on `100 / 500` random OPGs (`469` caries):** Independently read by Radiologist `#1` and Radiologist `#2` (`21 yrs` exp, J.K.) on `2560 × 1600` Eizo `RadiForce RX430` monitors, with adjudication by Radiologist `#3` (`25 yrs` exp, R.A.). Two months later, `27-yr`-experienced OMFR (`S.P.D.`) repeated the `100` OPGs. **Reliability vs. GT:** Radiologist `#1 = 0.832`, `#2 = 0.864`; **intrarater correlation (`#1`) = `0.734`**; **interrater correlation (`#1 vs. #2`) = `0.707`**.
  - **Full `500`-OPG Annotation (`pp. 2, 5`):** Using **`AIM v4.6.0.7` (`AIM20` markup standard)** and paired `BW` confirmation, `S.P.D.` (`27 yrs` exp) labeled **`1,792` carious posterior teeth** out of **`9,053` posterior teeth** (`14,997` total teeth detected across OPGs; anterior teeth were NOT labeled due to lack of anterior confirmatory radiographs). Caries stages totaled **`1,916` surfaces**: **`519` (`27.1%`) enamel**, **`1,163` (`60.7%`) dentine**, and **`234` (`12.2%`) pulp involvement**.
- **Data Split (`p. 3`, `Fig. 1`):** From `14,997` extracted single-tooth crops, `2,161` crops formed the `Training` set and `737` crops were set aside for `Validation` and `Testing` (`p. 3` text vs. separate `Validation n = 737` and `Testing n = 737` boxes in `Fig. 1`), with the remaining crops reserved for full-system evaluation across all `500` OPGs.

## 3. Two-Stage Model Pipeline & Quantitative Results (`pp. 3–13`, `Tables 1–7`, `Figs. 2–7`)
1. **Stage 1 — `YOLOv5s` Tooth Detection (`7.2M` params, `p. 3`):** Trained on **`DENTEX CHALLENGE 2023`** to crop individual teeth: **`Precision = 99.6%`, `Recall = 98.9%`, `mAP50 = 99.5%`, `mAP50-95 = 79.9%`**.
2. **Stage 2 — Model Selection on `100` Cropped Tooth Images (`Table 1`, p. 4):** Tested `6` segmentation models from scratch using **`mIoU` per cropped image**: **`Attention U-Net` (`0.484`)** > `Nested U-Net` (`0.477`) > `U-Net` (`0.417`) > `TransUNet` (`0.384`) > `Swin U-Net` (`0.204`) > `R2U-Net` (`0.027`).
3. **Training Setup (`p. 4`):** Intensity normalization preprocessing; `Attention U-Net` trained with binary cross-entropy loss, **`Adam`**, **`LR = 1 × 10^-4`**, **`batch size = 8`**, **`100` epochs** on `NVIDIA RTX 2080 SUPER` (`Ubuntu 22.0`).

## 4. Limitations & Reviewer Methodological Critique (`OS §3.2`)
### 4.1 Author-Stated Limitations (`pp. 10–14`)
1. **Substantial False-Positive Overprediction (`p. 10, 12`):** `519` false-positive posterior teeth (`826` overall) yielded moderate precision (`0.77` posterior, `0.68` all teeth), preventing standalone clinical use (`p. 12`).
2. **Unlabeled anterior teeth (`p. 7, 14`):** Lack of anterior confirmatory radiographs left anterior caries unlabeled, penalizing valid AI detections on anterior teeth as `FPs`.
3. **Underestimation of early enamel caries (`p. 10`), single-center CS9000C data without external validation, and no dentist-with-AI assistance trial (`p. 14`).**

### 4.2 Reviewer Methodological Critique (`OS §3.2` Audit)
1. **System-Level Evaluation Across All `500` OPGs Includes the Training Cohort (`p. 3`, `Fig. 1`, `Tables 2–6`, `Fig. 5`):**
   - `2,161` tooth crops from the `500` OPGs were used to train `Attention U-Net`, yet **`Fig. 5`, `Table 6`, and `Tables 2–5` report metrics across ALL `500` OPGs (`1,792` carious teeth / `9,053` posterior teeth)** rather than strictly isolating an unseen patient-level test split (`n_val/test`). By contrast, on the clean `100`-image comparison in `Table 1` (`p. 4`), `Attention U-Net`'s true per-crop pixel `mIoU` was only **`0.484`**.
2. **Exclusion of ALL `519` False Positives from `Weighted κ` (`Tables 2–5`) and `Bland–Altman` (`Fig. 4`) Analysis (`pp. 5–9`):**
   - Forensically checking column totals in `Table 2` (`p. 6`) reveals **`0` rows where `AI (N) > Ground truth (N)`** — every disagreement is `GT > AI`, with total AI count $= 1,725$ (`= TP` in `Fig. 5a`), total GT count $= 1,792$ (`= TP + FN`), difference $= 67$ (`= FN`), and **`67 / 500 = +0.134` = the exact `Mean +0.13` bias in `Fig. 4a`**!
   - Thus, **`Tables 2–5` ($\kappa = 0.907–0.981$) and `Fig. 4` completely excluded all `519` posterior false positives** (`Fig. 5a`), counting only matched GT-positive teeth and drastically overstating clinical count agreement (`true AI flagged teeth = 1,725 + 519 = 2,244`, a `-0.90` tooth/OPG overprediction bias!).
3. **Conflation of Tooth-Level Binary Classification `TP/FP/FN` with Semantic-Segmentation `IoU / DSC` (`pp. 5, 7, 10, 13`):**
   - Although `p. 5` defines `TP, FP, FN, TN` as **pixels**, `Fig. 5` and `Table 6` actually compute `TP = 1,725, FP = 519, FN = 67` at the **binary tooth level (`carious vs. healthy tooth`)** (`p. 7`). Comparing this tooth-level `"IoU = 0.75, DSC = 0.85"` directly against pixel-level mask overlap in `Table 7` (`CariesNet DSC = 0.94, Dayi DSC = 0.68–0.71`) mixes two incompatible metrics, while `Fig. 5b` sums to `14,993` teeth instead of `14,997` reported on `p. 5, 7`.
4. **No Multiclass Severity Model & Moderate Calibration Reliability (`pp. 2–4`):** Despite `"multistage"` in the title, `Attention U-Net` was trained strictly as a **binary classifier (`caries vs. non-caries`, `p. 4`)**; stage breakdowns in `Tables 3–5` simply stratify binary mask hits over BW-derived GT labels. Additionally, `Table 1` (`p. 4`) does not state whether `mIoU` is binary foreground `IoU` or 2-class mean `IoU`, initial inter-rater ICC (`0.707`) and intra-rater ICC (`0.734`) were only moderate, and `YOLOv5s + Attention U-Net` inference runtime (`ms/OPG`) is `not reported`.
