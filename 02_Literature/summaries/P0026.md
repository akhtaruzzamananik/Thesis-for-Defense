## 1. Problem & Clinical / Technical Motivation
- **Clinical & dataset motivation (pp. 1–4):** Publicly available panoramic radiograph (`OPG`) datasets rarely combine tooth-level **diagnosis** and **treatment planning**, often lack transparent radiographic annotation rules (leading to errors such as labeling unerupted immature third molars as `impacted` in `DENTEX` [`Fig. 1`, p. 2] or labeling non-cavitated teeth as needing `fillings` in `Yüksel et al. [15]` [`Fig. 2`, p. 4]), and rely on axis-aligned bounding boxes (`AABBs`) that enclose excessive background pixels around tilted teeth (`p. 5`).
- **Technical problem (pp. 2, 6–8):** Tooth-level diagnosis and treatment planning depend both on fine-grained internal tooth structure and on surrounding alveolar/adjacent-tooth context, as well as the tooth's spatial location and tilt. However, naively concatenating a tooth crop, its neighborhood crop, and a binary location mask into a 3-channel image causes standard cross-channel CNNs or ViTs to conflate their distinct semantics in the first layer (`pp. 7–8`).
- **Contributions (pp. 1–2, 4–8):**
  1. **Public `AU-OPG` Dataset & Code (`https://github.com/akvnn/guiding-neural-nets`):** `901` adult OPGs (`7,006` tooth-aligned **Oriented Bounding Boxes [OBBs]**) jointly labeled for **6 diagnostic classes** and **4 radiographic-evidence-based treatment classes** with explicit clinical annotation rules (`Section III-B`, p. 6).
  2. **Oriented Object Detection Benchmark (`Table 4`, p. 8):** Compares **`YOLOv11-OBB` (`extra-large`)** against `Oriented R-CNN`, `SASM RepPoints`, `Rotated RetinaNet`, and `Rotated RepPoints`.
  3. **Implicit Spatial Guidance + Dual Frozen `MAE` + `Depth-wise ViT-tiny` (`Figs. 4–5`, pp. 6–8):** Combines two self-supervised `ViT-Base/16` Masked Autoencoders (`MAE-sub` and `MAE-crop`) with a 3-channel (`sub`, `crop`, `binary mask`) `Depth-wise ViT-tiny` classifier, validated via 8 ablation experiments (`Table 5`) and `10,000`-resample bootstrap `95%` confidence intervals (`Section IV-D`, p. 10) following **`TRIPOD+AI`** guidelines (`p. 4`).

## 2. Dataset, Annotators & Splits
- **Cohort & acquisition (`Section III-A`, pp. 4–5):**
  - **Source:** **`901` anonymized adult panoramic radiographs** collected at the Dental Clinic, **Ajman University, UAE** (Ethics approval `Application No. D-F-H-6-Mar`, `pp. 1, 4`), acquired on a **`Cranex`** panoramic X-ray unit.
  - **Inclusion / Exclusion criteria (`pp. 4–5`):** Excluded pediatric, completely edentulous, and serial radiographs, but **intentionally retained distorted radiographs** to evaluate robustness on realistic clinical acquisitions (`pp. 4–5`).
  - **Open repository (`p. 1`):** `https://github.com/akvnn/guiding-neural-nets`.
- **Two-stage annotation workflow & explicit radiographic criteria (`Sections III-A, III-B`, pp. 5–6, Fig. 3, Table 2):**
  - Annotated in **`Label Studio`** with **tooth-aligned Oriented Bounding Boxes (`OBBs`)** rotated along each tooth's long axis (`p. 5`).
  - **Annotators (`p. 5`):** Dental graduates created initial annotations, and an **experienced oral and maxillofacial radiologist (`> 20` years of experience, `A.M.L.`)** reviewed and corrected every annotation against explicit radiographic rules (`p. 5–6`). Because of this sequential single-rater + expert-review workflow, inter-rater Cohen's `κ` is `not reported` (`acknowledged in Section IV-F`, p. 11).
  - **Target-only annotation (`pp. 5, 7, 12`):** Only teeth belonging to at least one of the 6 diagnostic classes were annotated (**`7,006` annotated teeth** across `901` OPGs; `N = 1,032` teeth in the held-out test set, `p. 10`), while healthy/untargeted teeth were left unannotated (serving as implicit background during OBB detector training).
  - **Class distributions & explicit radiographic criteria (`Table 2` & `Fig. 3`, pp. 5–6):**

## 3. Methodology & Experimental Design
- **Stage 1 — Oriented Object Detection (`Section III-D`, p. 6; `Table 4`, p. 8):**
  - Trains a single-class (`Tooth`) OBB detector on full radiographs to localize all `7,006` target teeth.
  - Compares 5 oriented detectors: **`YOLOv11-OBB` (`extra-large` / `YOLOv11x-OBB`)**, **`Oriented R-CNN`**, **`SASM RepPoints`**, **`Rotated RetinaNet`**, and **`Rotated RepPoints`**.
- **Stage 2 — Implicit Spatial Guidance & Dual-MAE + Depth-Wise ViT Classification (`Sections III-E to III-G`, pp. 6–8, Figs. 4–5):**
  - **Affine OBB rectification & 3-channel input (`224 × 224`, `Section III-F`, pp. 7–8, Fig. 5):** Each detected OBB is rotated upright about its center and converted into a heterogeneous 3-channel tensor:
    1. `sub` (Channel 1): Upright target tooth ROI.
    2. `crop` (Channel 2): Zoomed-out (`3×–5×`) neighborhood ROI capturing adjacent teeth and bone.
    3. `binary` (Channel 3): Binary mask (`1` inside the target tooth OBB within `crop`, `0` elsewhere) encoding implicit spatial position and orientation.
  - **Dual Frozen `MAE` Feature Extractors (`Section III-E`, p. 7):** Two **`ViT-Base/16`** encoders (`12` blocks, `768` dim, `12` heads, `16×16` patches, `25%` visible patches / `75%` masked) pre-trained via self-supervised masked image reconstruction on `sub` (`MAE-sub`) and `crop` (`MAE-crop`), then frozen.
  - **`Depth-wise ViT-tiny` (`Section III-G`, pp. 7–8):** Extends `ViT-tiny` (`12` blocks, `192` dim, `3` heads, MLP `768`) by adding a third parallel residual branch in every transformer block (`reshape tokens to 14×14 -> GELU -> 2D BatchNorm -> 3×3 depth-wise convolution -> reshape back`) to preserve independent per-channel semantics across `sub`, `crop`, and `binary`.
  - **Shared trunk & dual heads (`p. 6, Fig. 4`):** Concatenates features from `MAE-sub`, `MAE-crop`, and `Depth-wise ViT-tiny` into separate `Diagnosis` (`6`-way) and `Treatment` (`4`-way) classification heads.
- **Compute hardware & runtime latency (`FPS` / `ms`):** `not reported`.

## 5. Limitations
### 5.1 Author-Stated Limitations (`Section IV-F`, pp. 11–12)
1. **No inter-annotator agreement (`κ`) reported (`p. 11`):** The two-stage graduate-annotation + single-expert-radiologist review workflow prevented calculation of Cohen's kappa across independent raters.
2. **No head-to-head clinician or prospective clinical validation (`p. 11`):** Model predictions have not yet been compared against practicing dentists on the test set.
3. **Radiographic-only scope & heuristic `2/3` pulp-proximity boundary (`pp. 6, 11–12`):** Distinguishing `Filling` (`F1 = 0.32`) vs. `Root Canal` (`F1 = 0.46`) via a `2/3` dentin-depth cutoff on 2D OPGs is an approximation when clinical pulp vitality tests and periapical X-rays are absent, exacerbated by severe minority class imbalance (`263` `Filling` and `326` `Root Canal` instances vs. `4,769` `None`).
4. **Unannotated sound teeth treated as detector background (`pp. 7, 12`):** Because only diseased/treated teeth (`7,006` instances) were annotated, sound teeth act as background during `YOLOv11-OBB` training, which may suppress detections of borderline cavitated teeth that resemble sound teeth.

### 5.2 Reviewer Methodological Critique (`OS §3.2`)
1. **Statistical indistinguishability vs. `EfficientNet` & `Single-Channel` baselines (`Section IV-D`, p. 10; `Table 5`, p. 10):** The authors commendably report `10,000`-resample bootstrap CIs (`p. 10`), which show that the `Depth-wise ViT` and `EfficientNet (feature extraction)` have overlapping `95%` difference CIs spanning zero (`Δ macro F1 = -0.008 [-0.04, +0.03]` for diagnosis, `+0.047 [-0.03, +0.12]` for treatment). Moreover, in `Table 5(c)`, the `Single Channel` ablation achieves `0.84` diagnosis `F1` and `0.87` treatment `F1`—only `0.01–0.02` below the full 3-channel framework (`0.86 / 0.88`).
2. **Stage-1 to Stage-2 error propagation not quantified end-to-end (`pp. 6, 8–10`):** `Table 3` and `Fig. 6` evaluate Stage-2 classification on the `N = 1,032` ground-truth test teeth rather than on false-positive / imperfect OBB crops predicted by `YOLOv11-OBB` (`Precision = 0.937, Recall = 0.906`, meaning `9.4%` of target teeth are missed at Stage 1).
3. **Minority treatment performance remains low (`Filling F1 = 0.32`, `Root Canal F1 = 0.46`) & runtime/hardware omitted (`G6`):** Neither focal loss nor class-balanced reweighting was applied during training (`acknowledged on p. 11–12`), and train/val/test radiograph counts, training epochs, GPU hardware, and end-to-end inference latency (`ms/OPG` for `YOLOv11x-OBB + 2× ViT-Base MAE + Depth-wise ViT-tiny` across `~8` teeth/OPG) are `not reported`.

## 6. Relevance to Our Thesis (`STEP-007` Synthesis Links)
- **High-value open dataset (`AU-OPG`, `901` OPGs, `7,006` OBBs) & `YOLOv11-OBB` benchmark (`G1` / `G4`):** Alongside `P0006` (`Khurshid et al., 2026`, `YOLOv11s-OBB` `mAP50 = 0.833`), `P0026` proves that **`YOLOv11x-OBB` (`mAP50 = 0.954, P = 0.937`)** outperforms `Oriented R-CNN` (`0.881`), `SASM RepPoints` (`0.848`), and `Rotated RetinaNet` (`0.799`) for oriented tooth detection on OPGs (`Table 4`, p. 8).
- **Methodological exemplar for annotation guidelines (`G2`), neighborhood context (`S11`), and bootstrap CI reporting (`G5`):** Directly supports our thesis's explicit annotation protocol (avoiding DENTEX's immature third-molar impaction errors, `Fig. 1`), local-plus-neighborhood context fusion (`sub + crop + binary mask`), and `10,000`-bootstrap confidence interval reporting.
