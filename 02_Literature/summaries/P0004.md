## Verified citation

Roopitha, C. H., Mayya, V., Sivakumar, V., Patil, V., Pai, D., & Varchas, P. (2026). Enhancing AI-based oral decision support systems: Hybrid image processing for detecting impacted maxillary canines in orthopantomograms. *Intelligence-Based Medicine*, 13, 100345. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ibmed.2026.100345 (PDF p. 1).

## question/task/modality

- **Modality:** 2D grayscale Orthopantomograms (`OPGs`, native `2943 × 1435` px, p. 5).
- **Task:** Controlled ablation of **6 image preprocessing techniques** on (1) Stage-1 **2D OPG bounding-box detection of impacted maxillary canines (`IMC`) using `YOLOv5`**, and (2) Stage-2 4-class horizontal canine position classification (`Pitt TDI Scores 1–4`) on YOLOv5-cropped ROIs (`256 × 256` px) using `ResNet50` with `Grad-CAM` explainability (pp. 1, 3–10).

## data/counts

- **Source cohort (`pp. 4–5, 9, Table 1`):** `500` OPGs collected at Manipal College of Dental Sciences, India (Jan 2017 – Jun 2024; Ethics approval `189/2023`, Kasturba Hospital; `280` male / `220` female, age `≥14` years).
- **Instance counts (`Table 1`, p. 5 & `p. 9`):** `660` cropped impacted canine ROIs across Yamamoto Types (`Type I: 416`, `Type II: 75`, `Type III: 42`, `Type IV/V: 660` [note: Table 1 lists non-additive/total ROI count], `Type VI: 63`, `Type VII: 150`); horizontal overlap score distribution on the `660` cropped ROIs: `Score 1 = 416`, `Score 2 = 69`, `Score 3 = 75`, `Score 4 = 100` (p. 9).
- **Availability:** Private clinical dataset ("available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request", p. 15).

## annotation

- **Tool & format (`pp. 3, 5`):** `Make Sense` web-based annotation tool exporting YOLO `.txt` bounding boxes `(class, x, y, w, h)`. Every annotated image was scrutinized and endorsed by participating oral medicine and orthodontic specialists (p. 5).
- **Inter-annotator agreement (`kappa`):** `not reported`.

## split

- **Detection split (`p. 6, §3.3.7`):** Fixed `80% / 20%` split = `400` training OPGs and `100` testing OPGs (separate validation partition for YOLOv5 is `not reported`, though training/validation loss curves are plotted in Figs. 10–11). Authors explicitly critique pre-split augmentation leakage in prior work (`Almalki et al. [13]`, pp. 2, 10).
- **Classification split (`p. 10, Table 9`):** Stratified 5-fold cross-validation on the `660` cropped ROIs. Patient-level grouping across bilateral canines is `not reported`.

## model

- **Detection (`pp. 3–4, 6`):** `YOLOv5` (PyTorch, `CSPDarknet53` backbone + `PANet/FPN` neck + `GIoU` loss; specific scale `s/m/l/x` is `not reported`).
- **Classification (`p. 9, Table 6`):** `ResNet50` (ImageNet weights) + `Grad-CAM`.

## preprocessing

- **Controlled experimental factor (`pp. 5–8, Tables 3–4`):** All OPGs cropped to tooth ROI (removing white border) and resized to `640 × 640` for YOLOv5 (`256 × 256` for ResNet50), then evaluated under 8 conditions:
  1. `Original image` (unprocessed `2943 × 1435` resized)
  2. `Cropped image` (ROI crop only)
  3. `Median filter` (`3 × 3` window, p. 6; `PSNR = 38.65 dB, MSE = 9.357`, Table 3)
  4. `Gaussian filter` (`PSNR = 37.762 dB, MSE = 11.353`)
  5. `CLAHE` (tile-based local histogram equalization, **`clip limit = 2`**, p. 6; `PSNR = 28.382 dB, MSE = 94.391`)
  6. `Histogram stretching` (full dynamic range `[0, L]`; `PSNR = 32.374 dB`)
  7. `Image sharpening` (unsharp masking with **`alpha = 1.5`**, Eq. 4, p. 6; `PSNR = 28.792 dB`)
  8. **`CLAHE + Image sharpening` (Hybrid):** `PSNR = 27.976 dB, MSE = 103.634`.

## training

- **YOLOv5 (`Table 2`, p. 6):** `100` epochs, learning rate `0.001`, image size `640 × 640` (batch size and optimizer for YOLOv5 `not reported`).
- **ResNet50 (`Table 6`, p. 9):** Adam optimizer, `100` epochs, batch size `32`, learning rate `0.001`, categorical cross-entropy loss.

## metrics/IoU

- **Detection metrics (`pp. 7–8, Table 4`):** Precision, Recall, F1-score, `mAP` (at standard `IoU ≥ 0.50` threshold, p. 7 Eq. 12), and mean bounding-box `IoU`.
- **Image quality metrics (`Table 3`, p. 8):** `MSE` and `PSNR`.

## timing/hardware

- **Hardware (`Table 2`, p. 6):** Google Colab Pro with **NVIDIA Tesla T4 GPU** and `32 GB RAM`.
- **Inference speed (`FPS` / ms per image):** `not reported`.

## results with locators

- **YOLOv5 Impacted Canine Detection Ablation (`Table 4`, p. 8 — direct test of `G5` / `RQ2`):**
  - `Original image`: Precision `0.603`, Recall `0.701`, F1 `0.648`, **`mAP = 0.701`**, `IoU = 0.523`
  - `Cropped image`: Precision `0.727`, Recall `0.763`, F1 `0.745`, **`mAP = 0.774`**, `IoU = 0.576`
  - `Gaussian filter`: Precision `0.750`, Recall `0.789`, F1 `0.768`, **`mAP = 0.766`**, `IoU = 0.592`
  - `Median filter (3×3)`: Precision `0.788`, Recall `0.855`, F1 `0.818`, **`mAP = 0.863`**, `IoU = 0.611`
  - `Histogram stretching`: Precision `0.859`, Recall `0.842`, F1 `0.851`, **`mAP = 0.857`**, `IoU = 0.626`
  - `Image sharpening (α=1.5)`: Precision `0.867`, Recall `0.895`, F1 `0.882`, **`mAP = 0.921`**, `IoU = 0.630`
  - **`CLAHE (clip=2)`:** Precision `0.911`, Recall `0.882`, F1 `0.896`, **`mAP = 0.924`**, `IoU = 0.673`
  - **`CLAHE + Image sharpening` (Best):** Precision **`0.934`**, Recall **`0.932`**, F1 **`0.931`**, **`mAP = 0.948`** (`+0.247` mAP gain over unprocessed OPGs), **`IoU = 0.741`**.
- **ResNet50 Horizontal Position Classification (`Table 7`, p. 9 & `Table 9`, p. 10):**
  - `Gaussian filter` degraded accuracy to **`0.482`** (F1 `0.462`) vs `Original` `0.648` (F1 `0.493`), showing that smoothing blurs critical tooth-boundary edges!
  - `CLAHE` achieved Accuracy `0.769` (F1 `0.756`), and `CLAHE + Image sharpening` achieved best Accuracy **`0.774`** (F1 `0.761`; 5-fold CV overall Accuracy `0.7735`, F1 `0.6612`, Table 9).

## limitations

- **Author-stated (`pp. 10, 13`):** (1) Single-institution dataset (`500` OPGs) lacking multi-center validation; (2) restricted to `YOLOv5` and `ResNet50` without newer architectures; (3) evaluates only horizontal canine overlap rather than all Pitt TDI factors (vertical height, root curvature, age); (4) severe class imbalance in horizontal scores (`Score 1 = 416` vs `Score 2 = 69`).

## critique

- Provides one of the clearest controlled ablations in the OPG YOLO literature for **Gap `G5` (`RQ2`)**: proves both a strong positive effect of **`CLAHE (clip=2)`** (`mAP` `0.701 -> 0.924`, and `0.948` with sharpening) AND a negative/harmful effect of **`Gaussian blur`** on cropped classification (`0.648 -> 0.482`), demonstrating that higher PSNR (`37.76 dB` for Gaussian vs `28.38 dB` for CLAHE, Table 3) does **not** correlate with better YOLO/CNN feature discrimination.
- Limitations in reporting: YOLOv5 variant scale (`v5s/m/l/x`), batch size, confidence/NMS thresholds, and whether the 660 left/right canine crops in 5-fold CV were grouped by patient are not stated.

## reuse

- **Primary citation for `G5` and `RQ2` (Preprocessing factor design at `STEP-019`):** justifies testing `CLAHE (clip_limit=2.0)` vs unenhanced baseline under controlled split rules, and warns against blind Gaussian smoothing.

## conflicts

- None; directly supports our operating system policy (`OS §4.3`) to treat preprocessing as a controlled experimental factor rather than assuming all filters help.
