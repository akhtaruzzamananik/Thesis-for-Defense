## Verified citation

Biçengil, K., Kurt, A., Naralan, M. E., & Okumuş, İ. (2026). Deep Learning-Based Dental Caries Diagnosis on Panoramic Radiographies: Performance of YOLOv8 Versus Human Observers. *Diagnostics*, 16(8), 1150. https://doi.org/10.3390/diagnostics16081150 (PDF p. 1).

## question/task/modality

- **Modality:** 2D pediatric panoramic radiographs (`Planmeca ProMax 2D S2`, `66 kVp, 8 mA, 16.6 s`, p. 2).
- **Task:** Single-stage object detection (and mask boundary analysis) of `3` primary-molar dental caries surface types (`approximal`, `occlusal`, `buccal`) using `YOLOv8x`, benchmarked against three human observers of graded clinical experience (`Intern Dentist [ID]`, `Novice Specialist Student [NSS]`, `Experienced Specialist Student [ESS]`, pp. 1–5).

## data/counts

- **Source cohort (`pp. 2–4`):** `1,533` initial pediatric OPGs (ages `5–12` years, acquired `2018–2024` at Recep Tayyip Erdoğan University, Rize, Türkiye; Ethics Decision `23.05.2024/111`). `7` caries-free radiographs were excluded from model training to reduce class imbalance, leaving **`1,526` panoramic radiographs** containing **`12,499` annotated caries labels** (p. 3–4).
- **Exclusion rules (`p. 3`):** Motion artifacts, syndromic conditions, and primary molars with extensive coronal destruction / residual roots.

## annotation

- **Platform (`p. 3`):** `CranioCatch` software (Eskişehir, Türkiye).
- **Training vs reference-standard design (`pp. 3–4`):**
  - *Training set (`1,526` OPGs):* Annotated independently by `3` non-expert/trainee observers (`ID` final-year dental student, `NSS` 1st-year pediatric dentistry resident, `ESS` 2nd-year resident). Crucially, their annotations were **retained as separate unmerged labels** during training to reflect inter-observer variability (pp. 3–4).
  - *Evaluation ground truth (`100`-OPG test subset, `n = 800` evaluations per surface / `2,400` pooled):* Consensus between a pediatric dentist (`>10` years experience, `A.K.`) and an oral & maxillofacial radiologist (`5` years experience, `M.E.N.`). Two-week intra-observer reliability of `A.K.` yielded **Cohen's kappa `κ = 0.83`** (p. 4).

## split

- **Patient-level split (`p. 3`):** Explicitly split **on a patient basis** (`80%` train = `1,222` OPGs, `10%` validation = `152` OPGs, `10%` test = `152` OPGs; `100` test radiographs used for the human-vs-AI comparison, pp. 3–4).

## model

- **Architecture (`p. 4`):** `YOLOv8x` implemented in PyTorch / TorchVision within `CranioCatch`. Exact parameter count / weight initialization: `not reported`.

## preprocessing

- **Operations (`p. 3`):** Anonymized JPEGs resized to width **`1,696` pixels** preserving aspect ratio. Explicitly **no contrast enhancement, CLAHE, histogram equalization, filtering, denoising, or sharpening** applied prior to annotation or training.
- **Augmentation (`p. 4`):** `HSV-Hue`, `Hue-Saturation`, `HSV-Value`, and `Mosaic` during training.

## training

- **Schedule (`p. 4`):** Configured for max `800` epochs with early stopping; optimal checkpoint reached at **epoch `33`** (Fig. 2, pp. 4–5). Optimizer, learning rate, and batch size: `not reported`.

## metrics/IoU

- **Detection threshold (`p. 5`):** Correct detection defined at **`IoU ≥ 0.50`**. Metrics: TP, FP, FN, Precision, Sensitivity (Recall), F1-score, and `mAP@0.5`.
- **Statistical testing (`pp. 5–8`):** Exact McNemar test (`Table 3`) and paired bootstrap difference tests (`Table 4`) with Benjamini–Hochberg (`BH`) multiple-testing adjustment across `n = 800` per lesion type (`2,400` pooled).

## timing/hardware

- **GPU / CPU hardware and inference speed (`FPS` / ms):** `not reported`.

## results with locators

- **Overall detection `mAP@0.5` (`p. 10, line 957`):** **`mAP@0.5 = 0.541`** (with overall approximal `F1 = 0.576`).
- **Instance-level detection counts & metrics (`Table 1`, p. 6 — AI vs `ID` / `NSS` / `ESS`):**
  - *Approximal caries:* AI `TP=408, FP=215, FN=385`, Precision `0.655`, Sensitivity `0.515`, **F1 `0.576`** (beats `ID = 0.399` and `NSS = 0.441`, below `ESS = 0.709`).
  - *Occlusal caries:* AI `TP=69, FP=72, FN=365`, Precision `0.489`, Sensitivity `0.159`, **F1 `0.240`** (below `ESS = 0.465`, `NSS = 0.280`, `ID = 0.257`).
  - *Buccal caries:* AI **`TP=0, FP=7, FN=22`**, Precision `0`, Sensitivity `0`, **F1 `0`** (complete failure; `ESS F1 = 0.348`).
  - *Overall Table 1:* AI `TP=477, FP=294, FN=772`, Precision `0.619`, Sensitivity `0.382`, **F1 `0.473`** (vs `ESS = 0.639`, `NSS = 0.379`, `ID = 0.329`).
- **Pooled diagnostic evaluation on `100`-image subset (`Table 2`, pp. 6–7, `n=800` per class / `2,400` pooled):**
  - *Approximal:* AI Sensitivity `0.860`, Precision `0.919`, F1 `0.889`, Accuracy `0.854` (McNemar vs `ESS`: `p = 1.000`; vs `ID`: `p < 0.001`).
  - *Occlusal:* AI Sensitivity `0.424`, Precision `0.820`, F1 `0.559`, Accuracy `0.856`.
  - *Buccal:* AI Sensitivity `0.022`, Precision `1.000`, F1 `0.043`, Accuracy `0.945`.
  - *Overall pooled (`n=2,400`):* AI Sensitivity `0.712`, Precision `0.905`, F1 `0.797`, Accuracy `0.885` (comparable to `ESS` F1 `0.809`, BH-adjusted `p = 0.407`; significantly outperforms `ID` F1 `0.681` and `NSS` F1 `0.767`).

## limitations

- **Author-stated (`p. 13`):** (1) Single-center, single-device (`Planmeca ProMax 2D S2`) dataset limiting generalizability; (2) severe underrepresentation and 2D anatomical superimposition of buccal caries (and underlying permanent tooth germs in mixed dentition) causing poor buccal/occlusal sensitivity; (3) exclusion of `7` caries-free images from training and exclusion of severe coronal destruction; (4) training on unmerged annotations from trainees of varying experience introduced label noise.

## critique

- Highly realistic, honest negative/moderate result (`mAP@0.5 = 0.541`, Table 1 overall `F1 = 0.473`, buccal `F1 = 0`) that directly refutes over-optimistic claims (`>95%` caries accuracy) by showing that 2D OPG superimposition makes **buccal and early occlusal caries on primary molars poorly visible** without bitewing radiographs.
- Feeding unmerged trainee annotations (`ID`, `NSS`, `ESS`) into training while evaluating against expert consensus (`A.K.` + `M.E.N.`) explains the depressed box-level sensitivity and highlights why expert-verified ground truth (`S07–S08`) is critical.

## reuse

- Primary evidence for **`G1`/`G5`/`G6`** and **`UD04` (Class ontology labelability):** proves that surface-specific `buccal caries` on 2D pediatric OPGs is not reliably labelable/detectable (`TP = 0` in Table 1), justifying why our class ontology (`STEP-014/015`) must require radiographic visibility on 2D OPG.
- Benchmark reference for patient-level split (`80/10/10`) and Cohen's kappa (`κ = 0.83`) QA reporting.

## conflicts

- Contrasts sharply with inflated caries metrics in non-OPG or leaked-split studies; provides essential counter-evidence against assuming YOLO automatically achieves high recall on all caries surfaces in 2D OPG.

## unresolved fields

- Hardware specifications, inference speed (`FPS`/ms), batch size, learning rate, and exact count of unique patients across the `1,526` radiographs are `not reported`.
