## 1. Problem & Clinical / Technical Motivation
- **Clinical motivation (pp. 1–3):** Manual charting of tooth categories (`Incisor`, `Canine`, `Premolar`, `Molar`) and dental anomalies/restorations (`Impacted` teeth and `Caps`/crowns) on 2D panoramic radiographs (`OPGs`) is time-consuming and prone to observer fatigue and misclassification of adjacent teeth.
- **Technical problem (pp. 1–4):** Standard CNN backbones (`CSPDarknet53` in `YOLOv4`) have limited global receptive fields when teeth overlap or exhibit variable contrast and noise, while manual hyperparameter selection can lead to suboptimal convergence.
- **Proposed solution (pp. 1, 3–8, Figs. 2, 4):** A **Transformer-based `YOLOv4` (`MHSA-Darknet`)** framework that:
  1. Applies multi-step image preprocessing (resizing, median/Gaussian filtering, rotation/scaling alignment, ROI cropping, and histogram scaling, `pp. 1, 3–4, Fig. 3`).
  2. Embeds a **Multi-Head Self-Attention (`MHSA`)** block at stage **`P7`** (`stride 128`, `8 × 8` feature map for a `1024 × 1024` input) of the 7-stage `CSPDarknet` backbone (`[1, 3, 15, 15, 7, 7, 7]` CSP blocks) coupled with `SPP + PANet` neck and `CIoU + CMBN + Mish` (`pp. 4–6, Fig. 4`).
  3. Uses a **25-bit Genetic Algorithm (`GA`)** over `1,000` generations (`population = 60`) to tune 5 hyperparameters: Learning Rate (`2` bits), Batch Size (`7` bits), Number of Anchors (`3` bits), Number of Filters (`7` bits), and Number of Convolutional Layers (`6` bits) (`pp. 6–8, Table 1`).

## 2. Dataset, Annotators & Splits
- **Primary internal dataset (`Sections IV-A, IV-C, IV-D`, pp. 3–4, 12):**
  - **Source:** **`475` anonymized panoramic radiographs** collected from **Cosmo Dental Clinic, Manipur, India** (`Acknowledgment`, p. 12; Internal Review Committee approval, `p. 1`).
  - **Annotations (`Section IV-C`, p. 4):** **`15,000` tooth and anomaly bounding boxes** labeled in **`Roboflow`** in `YOLOv4` format across **6 classes**:
    1. `Incisor`
    2. `Canine`
    3. `Premolar`
    4. `Molar`
    5. `Cap` (dental crown)
    6. `Impacted` (impacted tooth)
  - **Annotator credentials and inter-annotator agreement (`κ` / `ICC`):** `not reported`.
- **Pre-split augmentation & train/test split (`Section IV-D`, p. 4):**
  - Augmentation operations (rotation, flipping, cropping, and shearing) expanded the `15,000` labeled teeth to **`20,000` tooth instances**.
  - **Split (`p. 4`):** Performed *after* augmentation into **`90%` training (`18,000` teeth)** and **`10%` testing (`2,000` teeth; `Table 3` confusion matrix on `p. 10` contains `1,999` evaluated test instances: `350` Molar, `360` Premolar, `300` Canine, `325` Incisor, `325` Cap, `340` Impacted — sum `= 2,000` with row totals `350+360+300+325+325+340 = 2,000`)**. No separate validation split is described (`p. 4`).
- **External dataset (`Section V-D`, p. 10, Ref. `[38]`):**
  - Tested on the public Mendeley panoramic dental X-ray dataset (`Brahmi & Jdey, 2023 [38]`, `doi:10.17632/73n3kz2k4k.2`; number of evaluated images/instances `not reported`).

## 3. Methodology & Experimental Design
- **Preprocessing pipeline (`Section IV-B`, pp. 3–4, Fig. 3):**
  - Sequential application of: (1) resizing to a standard dimension (`p. 5` mentions `1024 × 1024` when describing `P3`=`128×128` at stride `8` and `P7`=`8×8` at stride `128`), (2) median filtering (`p. 4`; Abstract `p. 1` and Conclusion `p. 11` also state Gaussian smoothing and histogram scaling), (3) orientation/size alignment via rotation and scaling, (4) ROI cropping, and (5) median smoothing.
- **Genetic Algorithm (`GA`) hyperparameter evolution (`Section V-A`, pp. 7–8, Table 1):**

## 5. Limitations
### 5.1 Author-Stated Limitations (`Section V-F`, p. 11; `Table 4`, p. 11)
1. **Dependence on dataset quality and diversity (`p. 11`):** Performance is sensitive to noisy or low-quality panoramic radiographs (`Table 4`) and may not cover rare dental anomalies.
2. **Unvalidated on 3D modalities (`p. 11`):** Applicability to 3D `CBCT` or intraoral mesh scans remains untested, and the model lacks explainable AI (`XAI`) interpretability (`pp. 2, 12`).

### 5.2 Reviewer Methodological Critique (`OS §3.2`)
1. **Severe pre-split augmentation & tooth-level split leakage (`Section IV-D`, p. 4):** On `p. 4`, the authors explicitly state that the `15,000` annotated teeth from `475` OPGs were first augmented (rotation, flipping, cropping, shearing) to **`20,000` teeth**, and *"subsequently divided into two subsets: 90% for training and 10% for testing"*. Splitting *after* augmentation and at the *tooth* level rather than the *patient/OPG* level places augmented copies of the exact same tooth (and teeth from the same patient's OPG) into both the `18,000`-tooth training set and the `2,000`-tooth test set, directly explaining the near-100% metrics (`99.31%–99.50%`).
2. **Omission of `mAP` (`@0.50` / `@0.50:0.95`) and mismatch between `Table 2` and `Table 3` (`pp. 8–10`):**
   - Although `Equations (11)–(13)` (`pp. 8–9`) define `AP`, `IoU`, and `mAP`, **no `mAP` value or `IoU` matching threshold is reported anywhere in `Table 2` or the text (`not reported`)**.
   - `Table 3` (`p. 10`) is a `6 × 6` closed-set confusion matrix (`2,000` total instances) with **zero background false positives (`FP`) and zero missed-detection false negatives (`FN`)**. Furthermore, the exact cell counts in `Table 3` contradict `Table 2`: for example, in `Table 3`, `Molar` has `TP = 347`, row total `= 350` (`Recall = 347/350 = 0.9914`), and column total `= 348` (`Precision = 347/348 = 0.9971`), yet `Table 2` reports `Precision = 0.9943, Recall = 0.9943, Accuracy = 0.9914`; for `Cap`, `Table 3` has column total `328` (`325` TP + `3` FP -> `Precision = 325/328 = 0.9909`), yet `Table 2` prints `Precision = 0.9970` and `Accuracy = 0.9900`.
3. **Unverifiable external validation on `Brahmi & Jdey (2023) [38]` (`p. 10`) & absence of experimental baselines (`Table 4`, p. 11):** The public Mendeley dataset `[38]` (`doi:10.17632/73n3kz2k4k.2`) contains `107` OPGs annotated for binary tooth instance segmentation (`Mask R-CNN` in `[39]`), **not** the 6-class (`Incisor, Canine, Premolar, Molar, Cap, Impacted`) bounding-box schema evaluated in `P0021`, and no external annotation protocol or confusion matrix is given. Moreover, `Table 4` (`p. 11`) only copies literature numbers from `2017–2024` papers across incompatible modalities (`Dental CT`, `3D Tooth Cusps`, `Periapical`, `CBCT`) without running a single baseline model (such as vanilla `YOLOv4` without `MHSA` or without `GA`) on the Cosmo Dental Clinic dataset.
4. **Inconsistent GA binary encoding (`Table 1`, p. 8) & missing runtime metrics (`G6`):** Running `1,000` GA generations with a population of `60` (`60,000` full `YOLOv4` training runs) is computationally implausible; in `Table 1`, binary strings vary in bit length (`100000` [6 bits = `32`] vs `1111111` [7 bits = `127`] in the `Batch Size` and `No. of Filters` columns), and no GPU model, training duration, or inference `FPS`/`ms` is reported (`not reported`).

## 6. Relevance to Our Thesis (`STEP-007` Synthesis Links)
- **Methodological counter-example for `G2` / `G5` (Pre-split augmentation leakage & missing `mAP`):** Together with `P0002` (`Bhoopalan et al., 2026`), `P0006` (`Khurshid et al., 2026`), and `P0011` (`Scarano et al., 2026`), `P0021` demonstrates how pre-split augmentation and tooth-level splitting inflate reported accuracy above `99%`, and why object detection studies must report standard `IoU`-thresholded `mAP@0.50` and `mAP@0.50:0.95` including background false positives and missed detections.
- **Architectural reference for backbone `MHSA` placement (`G4`):** Places `MHSA` only at the deepest backbone stage (`P7`) of `CSPDarknet` (`Fig. 4`, `p. 4–5`), providing an older `YOLOv4 + MHSA` counterpart to `P0005` (`Khurshid et al., 2026`, `Modified YOLOv11n + MHSA`).
