## 1. Problem & Clinical / Technical Motivation
- **Clinical & Annotation Challenge (`pp. 1–2`):** Panoramic radiography (`OPG`) is the first-line screening modality for jawbone cysts, tumors, and tumor-mimics (e.g., lingual mandibular bone depression / Stafne's bone cavity, `LMBD`). Object detection approaches (e.g., `DetectNet` `[5]`, `YOLOv3` `[6]`) require expensive, ambiguous bounding-box or border-specific lesion annotations across all training OPGs, whereas vanilla whole-image classifiers suffer from spurious background/tooth focus.
- **Objective (`p. 2`):** Design a `ResNet50`-based `3`-class diagnostic classifier (`Normal`, `Cyst and Tumor`, `LMBD`) guided by a differentiable **Grad-CAM Soft-IoU attention loss** trained on only a small subset (`0%–100%`) of radiologist-delineated lesion masks, paired with OPG-tailored **trapezoid augmentation** to maximize label efficiency.

## 2. Dataset, Ground-Truth Curation & OPG-Specific Augmentation (`pp. 3–6`, `Tables 1–2`, `Figs. 1–2`)

### 2.1 Cohort, Pathology Composition & Scanners (`p. 3`, `Table 1`)
- **IRB & Collection (`p. 3`):** Yonsei University Dental Hospital (`IRB No. 2-2020-0084`, `Jan 2019–Feb 2021`); **`716` anonymized OPGs** (`n_patients`: `not reported`), acquired on two units: **Rayscan Alpha Plus** (`71 kVp, 12 mA, 14.1 s`) and **Pax-i Plus** (`71 kVp, 14 mA, 13.5 s`).

| Diagnostic Super-Class (`3`-class model) | Included Pathology (`Table 1`, p. 3) | Confirmation Standard | Total OPGs (`n`) | Training OPGs (`Table 2`, p. 3) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Cyst and Tumor** | Dentigerous cyst (`91`) + Odontogenic keratocyst (`92`) + Ameloblastoma (`90`) | Histopathology | **`273`** | `172` |
| **Tumor-like Lesion (`LMBD`)** | Lingual mandibular bone depression | Computed tomography (`CT`) | **`173`** | `87` |
| **Normal** | No jawbone pathology | Clinical / Radiographic | **`270`** | `170` |
| **Total** | **`5` conditions grouped into `3` output classes** | — | **`716`** | **`429`** (`59.9%`) |

- **Split & Attention-Label Budgets (`pp. 3, 6`, `Table 2`):** Reported split ratio **`6:2:2`** (`train : val : test`, `n_train = 429`; exact val/test per-class breakdown `not reported`). Two oral and maxillofacial radiologists (`OMFRs`) traced free-form lesion borders (`Fig. 1`, `p. 4`). Experiments varied the proportion of lesion training OPGs given attention masks across **`0%` (`0`), `5%` (`14 + 2 = 16`), `10%` (`23 + 6 = 29`), `20%` (`44 + 9 = 53`), `50%` (`86 + 40 = 126`), and `100%` (`172 + 87 = 259`)** (`Table 2`).

### 2.2 OPG Preprocessing & Trapezoid Augmentation (`pp. 3–6, 9–10`, `Fig. 2`)
- **Center Border Cropping (`pp. 4, 6`):** Raw OPGs (`1280 × 720` px) cropped to **`940 × 520` px** consistently during training and inference to restrict inputs to the jawbone region and remove peripheral device/institution text markers.
- **Selected Augmentations (`pp. 3, 6`, `Fig. 2`):** Intensity jitter (`brightness ±5%`, `contrast ±10%`), **horizontal flip (`p = 0.5`)**, and **trapezoid projective transformation** (`base width ±5%`) simulating real panoramic horizontal magnification / jaw-proportion variation (applied jointly to both OPGs and attention masks). Vertical flip, hue jitter, and unconstrained histogram warping were rejected as radiographically invalid (`pp. 9–10`).

## 3. Attention-Guided Architecture & Quantitative Results (`pp. 5–9`, `Tables 3–4`, `Figs. 3–6`)
- **Framework (`pp. 5–6`, `Fig. 3`):** ImageNet-pretrained **`ResNet50`** (`GAP` + `dropout 0.25` + `Kaiming-uniform FC`, `3` logits) with conditional **Grad-CAM** on the last conv layer:
  1. Min-max normalize Grad-CAM map $H$ to $\bar{H} \in [0, 1]^{m \times n}$, threshold via differentiable sigmoid mask $\tilde{H} = s(\omega(\bar{H} - \theta J_{m \times n}))$ (`e.g., ω = 100`; exact `θ` and `m × n` `not reported`).
  2. Optimize $L = \alpha_{cls} L_{cls} + \alpha_{att} L_{att}$, where $L_{cls}$ is 3-class cross-entropy (`all samples`), $L_{att} = 1 - \mathrm{IoU}(\tilde{H}, Y)$ (`only attention-labeled samples`), and `α_cls = 2`, `α_att = 1`.
  3. **Training (`p. 6`):** `SGD`, initial `LR = 10^-3`, weight decay `10^-5`, cosine annealing (`T_max = 50`), `batch size = 8`, `100` epochs (`early stopping patience = 40` on validation accuracy), `NVIDIA RTX A6000`; results averaged over `5` repeated runs (`p. 7`).

## 4. Limitations & Reviewer Methodological Critique (`OS §3.2`)

### 4.1 Author-Stated Limitations (`p. 10`)
1. **Residual reliance on partial location supervision:** While substantially cutting annotation labor versus full object detection, the framework still depends on radiologist border masks for at least a small fraction (`>= 5%`) of lesion samples rather than purely self-guided attention.
2. **Future directions (`p. 10`):** Notes the need for OPG-specific **self-attention guidance** and finer separation of individual cyst and tumor subtypes without requiring border masks.

### 4.2 Reviewer Methodological Critique (`OS §3.2` Audit)
1. **Zero Quantitative Localization Metrics Despite Localization Claims (`pp. 2, 6–8`):**
   - Although the paper claims *"enhanced class activation map (CAM)-based localization results"* (`p. 2`) and positions its framework against box detectors (`DetectNet`, `YOLOv3`), **no test-set localization metric (`Grad-CAM IoU`, `Dice`, `Pointing Game`, `mAP`, or `FROC`) is reported** — `Tables 3–4` evaluate **whole-OPG image classification (`Accuracy / Sensitivity / Specificity`)** exclusively, while localization is supported only by `3` selected visual examples in `Fig. 5` (`p. 8`, where the `50%` `Cyst and Tumor` row visibly activates the contralateral normal mandible too).
2. **Coarse `3`-Class Formulation (`Cyst + Tumor` Merged) & Incomplete `Table 4` Experimental Clarity (`pp. 3, 5, 9`):**
   - `Dentigerous cyst` (`91`), `Odontogenic keratocyst` (`92`), and `Ameloblastoma` (`90`) are collapsed into one `Cyst and Tumor` class (`Fig. 3`, `p. 5`; `Table 2`, `p. 3`). Thus, despite calling the task *"fine-grained classification"* (`pp. 1, 9`), the classifier only separates **cyst/tumor vs. `LMBD` vs. normal** and cannot distinguish benign cysts from locally aggressive ameloblastoma.
   - `Table 4` (`p. 9`) omits stating which attention-label ratio was fixed (matching `99.17 ± 0.54` implies `100%`), yet notice its un-augmented baseline (`94.50%`) beats `Table 3`'s `0%` baseline (`92.41%`), and adding **`Intensity + Trapezoid` (`95.74%`) scored lower than `Trapezoid` alone (`96.11%`)**, while **`Flip` alone (`97.50%`) matched `Intensity + Flip` and `Flip + Trapezoid` (`97.50%`)**, leaving only the combined trio higher.
3. **Production Defect in `Fig. 6`, Broken Cross-Ref, and Citation Swaps (`pp. 5, 7–9, 11`):**
   - **Missing Right Panel in `Fig. 6` (`p. 8`) & Broken Ref (`p. 7`):** Caption explicitly describes `"Left: proportion of attention labels 0%. Right: proportion of attention label 100%."`, yet **`Fig. 6` contains only a single left panel while the right half is completely blank**, and `p. 7` contains an unresolved `"results in ()."` cross-reference.
   - **Wrong Citation for `ResNet50` (`p. 5, 11`):** `p. 5` cites `Ref [25]` for `"pretrained ResNet50 model"`, whereas Bibliography `[25]` (`p. 11`) is **Simonyan & Zisserman (2014) (`VGGNet`)**! Also contains a text typo on `p. 9` (`"e omitted combinations"`).
4. **Missing Dataset, Inter-Rater, Split, Hyperparameter & Efficiency Reporting (`pp. 3–6`):**
   - Unique patient count (`n_patients`), whether the `6:2:2` split (`~60%` for `Normal`/`Cyst+Tumor` vs **`87 / 173 = 50.3%`** for `LMBD` in `Table 2`) was strictly **patient-level**, whether `"five repeated experiments"` (`p. 7`) varied random seeds vs. CV folds, **inter-rater reliability ($\kappa$/IoU)** between the two `OMFRs`, scanner-balanced stratification (`Rayscan Alpha Plus` vs `Pax-i Plus`), exact `θ` threshold (`Eq. 1`), Grad-CAM resolution `m × n`, and inference speed (`ms/OPG`) are all `not reported`. External validation is absent.

## 5. Relevance to Our Thesis (`STEP-007` Synthesis Links)
- **Empirical Evidence Against Blind `CLAHE` & For Geometric OPG `Trapezoid` Augmentation (`G2`):** Directly informs our preprocessing ablation: `P0038` demonstrates (`Table 4`, `p. 9–10`) that standard **`CLAHE + Intensity + Flip + Rotate (-1° to +1°)` (`95.69%`)** can underperform milder **`Intensity + Flip` (`97.50%`)** due to CLAHE over-brightening on panoramic radiographs, whereas **trapezoid projective warping (`base width ±5%`)** models realistic panoramic horizontal jaw-proportion magnification and improves robustness when paired with intensity jitter and horizontal flip (`99.17%`).
- **Weakly Supervised Label-Efficiency Insight & Pitfall Warning (`G3`):** Shows how supervising `Grad-CAM` masks via **scale-invariant Soft-IoU loss** on just `5%` (`16` masks) of training data suppresses teeth/jaw shortcut features (`Fig. 5`, `+4.16%` accuracy). Simultaneously reinforces why our thesis (`G4/G5`) must explicitly measure **bounding-box `mAP50 / mAP50-95` and `IoU`** on a patient-disjoint test split rather than relying on whole-image classification accuracy or unquantified heatmaps.
- **Literature Cross-Link:** `P0038` is primary study **`S3`** in `P0037` (`Demir et al., 2024`, `Table 4`), where it is highlighted as the sole jawbone-lesion study (`3.45%`).
