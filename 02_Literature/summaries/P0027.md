## 1. Problem & Clinical / Technical Motivation
- **Clinical & methodological motivation (pp. 1–4):** While `YOLO` (You Only Look Once) object detection and segmentation architectures (`YOLOv2` through `YOLOv11`) have been rapidly adopted across dental specialties, individual studies use heterogeneous imaging modalities, inconsistent annotation workflows, and incomplete evaluation metrics (`mAP50` without `mAP50-95` or external validation), obscuring clinical readiness.
- **Objective (pp. 1, 4–6):** Conduct a `PRISMA 2020`-compliant, `PROSPERO`-registered (`CRD420251135014`) systematic review and `QUADAS-2` risk-of-bias appraisal of peer-reviewed studies (`January 2020 – August 2025`) applying YOLO detection or segmentation models to human dental imaging.

## 2. Search Strategy, Screening & Study Characteristics (`Dataset / Corpus`)
- **Search databases & window (`Section 2.1`, p. 5):**
  - Databases: **`PubMed`**, **`Scopus`**, and **`Google Scholar`** (`January 2020 – August 2025`).
  - Query structure: `(Artificial intelligence OR deep learning OR CNN OR machine learning) AND (dentistry OR dental OR oral) AND (YOLO OR "You Only Look Once")` (with `SUBJAREA "DENT"` filter applied in Scopus, `p. 5`).
- **PRISMA 2020 study selection flow (`Section 3.1`, p. 7; `Fig. 1`, p. 4):**
  - **Initial records identified:** `1,159` (`Scopus = 970`, `Google Scholar = 98`, `PubMed = 91`)
  - **Removed before screening:** `65` duplicates + `273` automation-ineligible = `338` removed
  - **Screened at title/abstract:** `821` records (`510` excluded + `25` manual exclusions = `535` excluded)
  - **Sought for full-text retrieval:** `286` reports (`105` not retrieved -> `181` assessed for eligibility)
  - **Excluded at full text:** `105` failed PICOS criteria + `3` removed due to reviewer disagreement = `108` excluded
  - **Included in final narrative synthesis:** **`73` original peer-reviewed studies** (`Tables 2–3`, `pp. 9–19`; `3` independent screeners `HH, RFAR, DN` + `2` senior arbiters `SA, ASS`, `pp. 5–6`).
- **Distribution of the `73` included studies (`Table 2`, pp. 9–15; `Table 3`, pp. 17–19):**
  - **Imaging modalities (`Table 2`):**
    - **Panoramic radiographs (`OPGs`):** `~31 / 73` studies (`42.5%`, dataset sizes ranging from `200` OPGs in `Peker & Kurtoglu, 2025 [67]` to `8,000` OPGs in `Yurttas et al., 2024 [83]`)
    - **Intraoral / clinical / occlusal photographs:** `~15 / 73` studies (`40` raw images in `Ribeiro et al., 2025 [70]` up to `6,313` in `Faizan Ahmed et al., 2025 [40]`)
    - **Periapical radiographs (`PAs`):** `~13 / 73` studies (`240` to `8,000` PA images in `Chen et al., 2023 [38]`)
    - **Bitewing radiographs:** `~8 / 73` studies (`730` to `13,887` bitewings in `Pérez de Frutos et al., 2024 [41]`)
    - **CBCT / CT / Lateral Cephalometric / TMJ MRI:** `~6 / 73` studies (`100` CT scans to `1,000` cephalograms)
  - **YOLO architectures represented (`Table 2`):** `YOLOv2` (`2` studies), `YOLOv3` (`6`), `YOLOv4` (`6`), `YOLOv5` (`26`), `YOLOv7` (`11`), `YOLOv8` (`27`), `YOLOv9` (`6`), `YOLOv10` (`6`), and `YOLOv11` (`6` studies: `Ayhan et al. [32]`, `Faizan Ahmed et al. [40]`, `Nguyen et al. [62]`, `Ramírez-Pedraza et al. [66]`, `Salehizeinabadi et al. [72]`, `Şevik & Mutlu [74]`, `Wu et al. [79]`).
  - **Clinical domains synthesized (`Sections 3.4–3.17`, pp. 8, 16, 19–20):** Periodontics, Oral Surgery, Oral Pathology, Caries Detection, Tooth Detection/Numbering, Endodontics, Implants, Restorative Dentistry, Orthodontics, Preventive Dentistry, Pediatric Dentistry, Prosthodontics, General Disease Classification, and Radiograph Quality/TMJ.

## 3. Methodology & Quality Appraisal (`QUADAS-2`)
- **Reporting & synthesis framework (`Section 2`, pp. 4–6; `Section 4`, p. 21):** Narrative synthesis structured around `PRISMA 2020` and `PRISMA-DTA`, with conceptual reference to `CLAIM`, `CONSORT-AI`, `DECIDE-AI`, `STARD-AI`, and `TRIPOD-AI` (`pp. 4, 21, 26`). Meta-analysis was deemed infeasible due to high clinical, dataset, and metric heterogeneity (`pp. 6, 27`).
- **`QUADAS-2` Risk of Bias findings (`Section 3.19`, p. 21; `Figs. 2–3`, pp. 21–24):**
  - **Patient Selection & Index Test:** Predominantly **low risk of bias** across the `73` studies, except where case-control sampling or pre-split data leakage occurred (`p. 21, 26`).
  - **Reference Standard & Flow/Timing:** Frequent **"some concerns" or high risk of bias** (`Figs. 2–3`) due to unreported annotator credentials/counts, absence of multi-rater consensus or inter-observer reliability (`κ`/`ICC`), unreported software tools (`24 / 73` studies marked `"N/A"` in `Table 3`), and undocumented handling of excluded images (`p. 21, 26`).

## 5. Limitations
### 5.1 Author-Stated Limitations (`Section 4.1`, p. 27)
1. **Infeasibility of quantitative meta-analysis (`p. 27`):** High heterogeneity across imaging modalities, task definitions, and inconsistent reporting of `mAP50-95`, `Precision`, `Recall`, and `F1` restricted synthesis to a narrative review.
2. **Scarcity of external validation in primary literature (`pp. 1, 8, 26–27`):** Over `87%` of included studies lacked external validation, limiting clinical generalizability.
3. **Exclusion of non-YOLO detectors & non-accessible raw data (`p. 27`):** Did not systematically benchmark YOLO against non-YOLO architectures (`RT-DETR`, `Mask R-CNN`, `U-Net`), and could not independently audit raw datasets or code.

### 5.2 Reviewer Methodological Critique (`OS §3.2` Cross-Audit Against Our Full-Text Extractions)
1. **Uncritical praise of leaked pre-split augmentation metrics (`pp. 14, 20, 22`):** On `p. 22` (lines 1836–1838), the authors cite `Singh et al., 2025 [73]` (`P0021`, `F1 = 0.994`) as proof of *"the potential of earlier YOLO versions when trained on well-curated datasets with balanced case distributions"*. In reality, as `Table 2` (`p. 14`) itself records (`"15,000 teeth annotated; 20,000 teeth after augmentation; split 90/10 train/test"`) and our full-text audit of `P0021` proved, `Singh et al. [73]` augmented their dataset *prior* to a tooth-level split and omitted `mAP` entirely.
2. **Data extraction errors on `Bonfanti-Gris et al. [36]` (`P0022`) and `Şevik & Mutlu [74]` (`P0016`) (`pp. 11, 14, 18, 20`):**
   - For `Bonfanti-Gris et al. [36]` (`P0022`), `Table 2` (`p. 11`) mixes **object detection** metrics for `YOLOv5s` (`mAP50 = 0.631, mAP50-95 = 0.392`) and `YOLOv7m` (`0.740 / 0.481`) with **instance segmentation** metrics for `YOLOv8m` (`0.591 / 0.272`, omitting `YOLOv8m`'s detection `mAP50 = 0.708, mAP50-95 = 0.484` and `YOLOv8l`'s `0.725 / 0.495`), and `Section 3.16` (`p. 20`, line 1757) falsely states that `Bonfanti-Gris et al. [36]` used *"intraoral photographs"*.
   - For `Şevik & Mutlu, 2025 [74]` (`P0016`), `Table 2` (`p. 14`) lists `mAP50` and `mAP50-95` as `"N/A"`, and `Table 3` (`p. 18`) records `External validation = "No"` and `Annotation tool = "Label Studio"`, whereas our full-text extraction of `P0016` verified `mAP50 = 0.91` (internal) / `0.82` (external), `mAP50-95 = 0.69–0.71` (internal) / `0.62` (external), and `Roboflow` external validation (`150` OPGs).
3. **Widespread reference-number swaps and modality mismatches in prose (`pp. 10, 15, 16, 19–20`):**
   - `Section 3.8` (`p. 16`): Swaps `[35]` (`Beser et al.`, OPGs) and `[62]` (`Nguyen et al.`, intraoral photos), and claims `Abed et al. [23]` used `"CBCT datasets"` when `Table 2` (`p. 9`) says `"Clinical photographs"`.
   - `Section 3.9` (`p. 16`): Attributes `RCFLA-YOLO (YOLOv11)` to `Apurba et al. [29]` instead of `Ayhan et al. [32]` (`Table 2`, p. 10).
   - `Section 3.12` (`p. 19`): Swaps `[11]` (`Bağ et al.`, pediatric OPGs) and `[59]` (`Akyuz et al.`, lateral cephalograms).
   - `Section 3.14` (`p. 19`): Swaps `[51]` (`Kaya et al.`) and `[54]` (`Kurt et al.`).
   - `Section 3.18` (`p. 20`, lines 1783–1788): Swaps `[55]` (`Lee et al.`), `[64]` (`Ozsunkar et al.`), and `[78]` (`van Nistelrooij et al.`).
   - `Table 2` (`pp. 10, 15`): Lists `Ayhan et al. [33]` as `"Panoramic radiographs"` alongside `"1170 bitewing radiographs"`, and `Yavsan et al. [80]` as `"Bitewing radiographs"` alongside `"415 periapical images"`.
4. **Restrictive Scopus filter (`LIMIT-TO SUBJAREA "DENT"`, `p. 5`) & pre-August-2025 cutoff:** Restricting Scopus to dentistry-indexed journals excluded engineering/computer-vision venues (`IEEE`, `Elsevier MethodsX`, `Springer MTAP`), and the August 2025 cutoff missed the late-2025 and 2026 wave of `YOLOv11`, `YOLOv12`, and `YOLO26` OPG studies (`P0001`–`P0015`).

## 6. Relevance to Our Thesis (`STEP-007` Synthesis Links)
- **Primary 2026 systematic-review anchor for our Literature Review (`Chapter 2`) and Research Gaps (`G1`–`G6`):** Establishes from `73` studies (`2020–2025`) that: (1) **`mAP50-95` is omitted in `84.9%` (`62/73`) of dental YOLO studies** (`G5`), (2) **external validation is absent in `>87%` of studies** (`G2`), (3) **annotator calibration/kappa is poorly reported** (`QUADAS-2` Reference Standard bias, `G2`), and (4) **dataset quality and clinical context outweigh raw YOLO version upgrades** (`p. 1, 26`).
- **Demonstrates the value of our thesis's rigorous full-text verification (`STEP-007`):** By catching `P0027`'s extraction errors on `P0016`, `P0021`, and `P0022` and extending coverage to `2025–2026` `YOLOv11 / YOLOv12 / YOLO26 / RT-DETR` OPG studies (`P0001`–`P0015`), our thesis literature review achieves substantially higher methodological accuracy and currency than existing published reviews.
