## ReportGap

`G4: CBCT versus OPG` (`OS §3.3`)

## proposed statement

2D panoramic radiography (`OPG`) AI detectors either match 3D Cone-Beam CT (`CBCT`) / intraoral diagnostics without modality limitations, or can be ranked numerically across 3D `CBCT`, intraoral bitewings (`BW`), periapical (`PA`), and intraoral photographs.

## search coverage

Tested across `180` harvested files -> `122` unique screened records: `9` non-OPG studies were excluded upfront under `EXC-MODALITY` (`P0098–P0106`: `3` CBCT-only `P0103–P0105`, `2` periapical `P0098, P0102`, `2` bitewing `P0099–P0100`, `2` intraoral clinical photo `P0101, P0106`), plus non-AI modality measurement exclusions `P0112, P0113, P0120, P0121`, alongside all `42` extracted Core studies (`P0001–P0042`).

## supporting and contradicting P IDs

- **Supporting `PaperIDs` (prove hard `2D OPG` physical limits vs `3D CBCT` / intraoral `BW`–`PA` AND severe within-`OPG` cross-scanner/protocol domain shift):**
  - **Paired `OPG` vs `CBCT` comparison (`P0039`):** Direct within-patient evaluation (`49` Polish patients, `1,223` teeth, paired `OPG + CBCT <= 30d`, `Kazimierczak et al., 2024`) shows `Diagnocat` CNN achieved **`OPG Sens = 33.33% (F1 = 32.73%)` vs `CBCT Sens = 77.78% (F1 = 84.00%)`** against consensus CBCT reference (human readers on OPG: `Sens = 66.67%, PPV = 31.58%`).
  - **Systematic `CBCT` ground-truth dependency & projection limits:** `P0034` (`Bonfanti-Gris et al., 2025`, `p. 15`) documents that **`4/6` (`66.7%`) impacted-tooth OPG studies** in its meta-analysis required **`CBCT` as reference standard**. `P0005` (`p. 8–9`), `P0013` (`p. 13`: misses labiopalatal/3D resorption), `P0020` (`pp. 18–19`: 2D bone superimposition), `P0021` (`p. 11`), and `P0033` (`p. 11`: condyle cortex blur vs CBCT) explicitly document why 2D OPG cannot replace 3D CBCT for buccal/palatal position, true IAN contact, or 3D cortical defects.
  - **Paired/direct `OPG` vs Intraoral `BW`/`PA` limits:** `P0032` (`van Nistelrooij et al., 2026`, `200` OPGs vs `150 BW + 50 PA`) demonstrates OPG excels on full-arch permanent tooth numbering (`FDI Acc = 0.980 vs 0.956`, `perm mAP@50 = 0.990 vs 0.940`), **but** intraoral radiographs achieve much higher localization strictness (`overall mAP@50-95 = 0.869 vs 0.779`; primary `0.895 vs 0.625`), and **initial enamel caries had to be excluded on OPGs** (`p. 8`). `P0036` (`Pornprasertsuk-Damrongsri et al., 2025`, `500` OPGs) required **same-day posterior bitewings (`>=4/patient`)** as reference and cataloged **`8` OPG anatomical caries mimics** (`cervical burnout, pulp horns, palatoglossal air space`, etc., `Fig. 7`) driving `519–826` false positives. `P0012` (`p. 2, 10`: caries needs `30–40%` mineral loss; premolar overlap drops sens to `79.3%`) and `P0026` (`pp. 11–12`: lacks `PA`/vitality for `2/3`-dentin depth) confirm these resolution/overlap ceilings.
  - **Within-`2D OPG` cross-center, scanner & positioning domain shift:**
    - `P0023` (`UMFIH`): External multi-center `180` OPGs dropped `YOLOv8` **`mAP@0.5` from `0.600 -> 0.451` (`-14.9%`)** (`API 0.547->0.315`, `CAR 0.509->0.373`).
    - `P0015` (`UMFIH` re-benchmark): External `180` OPGs dropped `RT-DETR-l` **`mAP@0.5` `0.7697 -> 0.5476` (`-22.2%`)** and `YOLO11m` `0.7416 -> 0.5366` (`-20.5%`).
    - `P0016` (Pediatric YOLO): External `150` Roboflow OPGs dropped `YOLOv11x` **`mAP50` `0.91 -> 0.82` (`-9.0%`)** (`F1 0.93 -> 0.81`).
    - `P0029` (`ToothLens`): External `30` Tufts OPGs dropped `Mask R-CNN` **`IoU` `79.74% -> 69.48%` (`-10.26%`)** (`Recall 87.00% -> 71.90%`).
    - `P0030` (`Nishimura et al., 2026`): Cross-country shift (`Japan -> China` and reverse) dropped `mAP@0.5` by `-0.048` to `-0.052` and caused models trained without bite blocks to **hallucinate radiopaque plastic bite blocks as primary tooth `A`** (`Fig. 6`), while `P0028` cites `56%` vs `30.7%` radiographer/radiologist non-diagnostic disagreement (`Decoster [46]`).
- **Contradicting / Counter-evidence against invalid cross-modality ranking:**
  - `P0002` (`Bhoopalan et al., 2026`): Claims OPG disease detection while sourcing Kaggle ***"high resolution RGB intraoral images"*** (`p. 4`), proving dataset-modality provenance auditing (`STEP-010`) is mandatory.
  - `P0021 Table 4`: Ranks 2D OPG numbers numerically against `Dental CT`, `CBCT`, and `Periapical` literature results.
  - `P0027`: Mixes `15` intraoral-photo studies (`mAP50-95` up to `0.932`) with `31` OPG studies (`max 0.760`), and `p. 20` mislabels OPG dataset `P0022` as `"intraoral photographs"`.
  - `P0032 Tables 3–4`: Pools OPG + bitewing + periapical metrics together into single per-pathology F1 columns, obscuring OPG-only pathology accuracy.

## independence

Converges across independent European (`P0015, P0023, P0032, P0039`), Asian (`P0012, P0016, P0029, P0030, P0036`), and multi-national (`P0005, P0027, P0034`) cohorts. Note `P0015` and `P0023` evaluate the same `UMFIH` (`1,628` + `180`) cohorts across different model generations (`YOLOv8x` vs `YOLO11/RT-DETR`).

## feasibility

Fully compliant with `scope.md` non-goals (*"No CBCT modeling; strictly 2D OPG"*). Within `O1–O3`:
1. We **prohibit cross-modality numerical ranking** (`CBCT`, `BW`, `PA`, or clinical RGB photos vs `2D OPG`), treating `CBCT`/`BW` strictly as clinical reference context defining what `2D OPG` cannot reliably see (e.g., 3D labiopalatal impaction/IAN proximity, initial non-cavitated enamel caries, pulp vitality).
2. Cross-dataset / external-OPG testing (e.g., training on `DENTEX` / `UMFIH` and testing cross-cohort on `Rahman 2024` Dhaka or hold-out multi-source sets under `UD02`/`S04`) directly addresses the verified **`-9%` to `-22.2%` within-OPG domain shift** gap without violating CBCT scope bounds.

## narrower interpretation

Rather than pitting `2D OPG` numerically against `3D CBCT` (`P0039`), `G4` narrows to two rigorous, actionable scientific boundaries:
1. **Explicit modality-ceiling governance:** `2D OPG` is a valid low-dose full-arch screening modality (`P0032 FDI Acc = 0.980`, `P0028`), **not** a substitute for `CBCT` or `BW` on sub-threshold enamel caries (`P0032, P0036`) or 3D surgical depth (`P0005, P0013, P0039`).
2. **Untamed within-OPG domain shift (`-9.0%` to `-22.2%` `mAP50`, `P0015, P0016, P0023, P0029, P0030`):** Cross-scanner/population generalization (and device artifacts like bite blocks) remains a major unresolved challenge inside `2D OPG` itself (`87.7%` of `73` YOLO studies lack external validation per `P0027`).
