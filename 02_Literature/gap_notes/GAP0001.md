## ReportGap

`G1: task-specific studies` (`OS §3.3`)

## proposed statement

Existing YOLO-based and deep-learning panoramic dental radiograph (`OPG`) studies are restricted to narrow single-task or single-condition detectors and do not yet address multi-class dental pathology, restoration, and anatomical structure detection.

## search coverage

Tested across the full screened corpus (`SEARCH0001` + `SEARCH0002`, `2026-11-16`): `180` candidate files (`58` cross-batch duplicates removed) -> `122` unique screened records, comprising `42` Core studies extracted in full (`23` Group A direct OPG object detectors `P0001, P0003–P0009, P0011–P0025` + `19` Group B contextual studies `P0002, P0010, P0026–P0042`), `55` Reserve records (`14` HighPriority `P0043–P0056` + `41` Secondary `P0057–P0097`, of which `27` explicitly mention `YOLO` in title/task/model), and `25` Excluded records (`14 EXC-TASK`, `9 EXC-MODALITY`, `1 EXC-NO-FULLTEXT` `P0107`, `1 EXC-UNVERIFIED` retracted `P0122`). Search caveat: manual multi-source harvesting (`P0001–P0122`) without live API automation, so coverage is exhaustive within the `122`-study project register rather than all post-search global indices.

- `Tufts Dental Database` (`1,000` OPGs) is reused in `P0042`, `P0006`, `P0015`, `P0017`, `P0029` (`30` external), and `P0031` (`33`-class helper).
- `Do et al. (2024)` Mendeley (`PAI 3–5`) underlies both deduplicated `P0008` and pre-augmented `P0011`.

- Authorship overlap: `Khurshid` appears on `P0005`, `P0006`, and `P0017` (plus Reserve `P0046`); `Jacobs` appears on `P0001` and `P0023`; `Bonfanti-Gris` appears on `P0022` and `P0034`; `Mureșanu`/`Hedeșiu`/`Eftimie` share `P0015` and `P0023`. Even after accounting for shared cohorts, independent multi-class OPG studies remain numerous (`P0001, P0009, P0014, P0015/P0023, P0016, P0017, P0021, P0022, P0024, P0025`).

## feasibility

We **cannot** claim "no multi-class OPG YOLO detector exists" without violating `STEP-008` evidence standards. However, auditing and improving **per-class detection reliability across radiographically distinct tiers (high-contrast restorations/impactions vs. small/radiolucent caries & periapical lesions vs. rare classes) under strict patient/OPG-level splits** is highly feasible within `O1–O2` using verified public OPG datasets (`DENTEX`, `UMFIH`, `Rahman 2024` Dhaka, `MD-OPG`, `AU-OPG`). Target ontology (`UD04`) must exclude non-radiographic/unreliable surface labels (e.g., buccal caries `TP = 0` in `P0003`, clinical gingival-only exposure `RR clinical R = 0.506` in `P0006`, initial enamel caries on OPG in `P0032`).

## narrower interpretation

1. **Broad claim refuted:** Multi-class (`4–13` classes) YOLO and transformer OPG detectors clearly exist across multiple independent cohorts (`P0001, P0009, P0014–P0017, P0021–P0025`).
2. **Surviving narrower gap:** Existing multi-class OPG detectors exhibit severe **radiographic-contrast and scale disparity**—achieving high `AP` on radiopaque/large targets (`Impacted teeth, Implants, Crowns`: `~0.83–0.98`) while collapsing on **small radiolucent lesions (`Periapical lesions 0.07–0.32`, `Caries 0.25–0.40`), diffuse bone loss (`0.15–0.17`), and rare classes (`Supernumerary 0.00–0.58`)** (`P0009, P0014, P0022, P0023, P0030`)—or mask these failures via ontology merging (`P0016`), subset-only training (`P0001`), omitted per-class tables (`P0024`), and pre-split leakage (`P0006, P0021, P0025`).
