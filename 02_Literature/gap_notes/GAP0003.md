## ReportGap

`G3: segmentation versus detection needs` (`OS §3.3`)

## proposed statement

Pixel-level or instance segmentation is either universally superior to bounding-box object detection for panoramic radiographs (`OPGs`), or conversely bounding boxes suffice uniformly for every dental target structure.

## search coverage

Tested across `180` harvested files (`58` duplicates) -> `122` unique screened records (`42` Core extracted in full: specifically `20+` Core papers contrasting or combining boxes, oriented boxes, keypoints, semantic masks, and instance masks — `P0001, P0006, P0007, P0010, P0011, P0013, P0015, P0018, P0022, P0023, P0026–P0029, P0031–P0037, P0040, P0042` — plus Reserve `P0045, P0052–P0054`).

## supporting and contradicting P IDs

- **Evidence showing bounding-box / oriented-box detection outperforms or replaces pixel segmentation for discrete multi-instance screening:**
  - `P0018` (`Küçük et al., 2025`) vs `P0035` (`Durmuş et al., 2025`): Direct natural benchmark on the **exact same `407` OPG cohort & `325/21/61` split** (`Impacted Tooth`). `P0018`'s bounding-box detector ensemble (`YOLOv8L + RT-DETR + Bayesian WBF`) reached **`mAP@0.5 = 98.3%, F1 = 96.0%`**, beating `P0035`'s best binary pixel semantic segmentation (`U-Net + EfficientNetB7`: `IoU = 85.29%, F1 = 92.02%`), where binary masks merged touching adjacent impacted teeth into single connected components (`P0035 Fig. 4 Ex. 3`).
  - `P0007` (`Ozcelik et al., 2026`, `1,378` pediatric OPGs, `44` FDI): Single-stage box/instance `YOLOv11` (`mAP50 = 0.8715, F1 = 0.8592`) substantially outperformed semantic `DeepLabV3` (`mAP50 = 0.6167, F1 = 0.7319`, adjacent-tooth merging) and two-stage `Mask R-CNN` (`mAP50 = 0.7870, R = 0.7584`).
  - `P0022` (`Bonfanti-Gris et al., 2024`, `600` OPGs, `12` classes): Bounding-box `YOLOv7m` (`mAP0.5 = 0.740, mAP0.5:0.95 = 0.481`) substantially outperformed polygon instance segmentation `YOLOv8m-seg` (`mAP0.5 = 0.591, mAP0.5:0.95 = 0.272`), where masks failed particularly on large jaws (`Maxilla 0.070–0.125`) and tiny structures (`Screw 0.011–0.077`).
  - `P0011` (`Scarano et al., 2026`): `YOLOv12-turbo-m-seg` (`mAP50 = 0.9773, mDice = 0.9354`) outperformed `Mask R-CNN` (`0.7620`) and `U-Net` (`mDice = 0.8803`), while `P0001` (`YOLO26m`) and `P0015` (`RT-DETR-l / YOLO11m` pathology boxes + `YOLO11-seg` teeth at `IoU >= 0.25`) demonstrate that bounding boxes provide fast Stage-1 localization for discrete lesions/restorations (`O1`).
- **Evidence showing axis-aligned bounding boxes (`AABB`) alone are insufficient for diffuse, angular, or linear-measurement targets:**
  - `P0013` (`Özden et al., 2026`, `624` OPGs, `OIEARR`): `YOLOv12x-OD` (`AABB`) suffered `4.2×` larger root-length error (`3.13%` vs `0.74%`) and `-0.28` `F1` (`0.60` vs `0.88`) relative to `YOLOv12x-pose` (`12` anatomical keypoints) on inclined maxillary incisors.
  - `P0023` (`Mureșanu et al., 2024`, `UMFIH`): Explicit author finding that `AABB`s fail on diffuse periodontal **`Alveolar Bone Loss (BON)`** (`AP@0.5 = 0.165` internal / `0.154` external, `R = 0.107`) because boxes cannot encode anatomical CEJ–crest context (`pp. 8–9`). Similarly, `P0031` lost **`91%` (`202/222`)** of diffuse `Bone loss` polygons when forcing 1-to-1 tooth pairing.
  - `P0006` (`Khurshid et al., 2026`) & `P0026` (`Alabd-Aljabar et al., 2026`): On oblique/tilted teeth and roots, **`YOLOv11-OBB`** (`mAP@0.5 = 0.833` in `P0006`, `+4.1%` over shrunk `AABB` at identical `14.2–14.4 ms` latency; `mAP@0.5 = 0.954` in `P0026`) removes background intrusion of `AABB`s without needing pixel masks.
  - `P0029` (`Nayak et al., 2026`, `Table 13`): `BBox-only` ROI masking collapsed downstream enhancement segmentation (`IoU = 0.1489` vs `0.8173`), while `P0032` (`van Nistelrooij et al., 2026`) showed tight tooth `BBox` crops truncate apical lesions (`Sens = 0.538` vs `0.854`), and `P0011` showed Stage-2 seg-guided ROI cropping still clipped `9.7%` (`569/5,857`) of lesions.
  - **Segmentation metric pitfalls:** `P0033` (`Yilmaz et al., 2026`) proves `99.08%` background pixels inflate semantic accuracy to `0.996–0.997` and validation `AP50:95` reverses `Dice` ranking across `RT-DETR/YOLOv9/YOLOv11-Seg`; `P0042` (`Panetta et al., 2022`) provides `1,000` Tufts binary tooth masks (`max IoU = 86.67%, Dice = 92.49%`), while `P0040` (`Zhou et al., 2024`) uses `YOLOv9-e-seg` (`1280×1280, mAP50 = 0.975`) for `33`-class teeth.

## independence

Strong cross-cohort corroboration across independent teams and public benchmarks: `P0018`/`P0035` share `407` OPGs (different authors); `P0007` (Ordu), `P0022` (Madrid), `P0013` (İnönü), `P0023`/`P0015` (Cluj), `P0006` (Pakistan/Thailand/Tufts), `P0026` (UAE), `P0029` (Mangalore), `P0032` (Europe/Egypt), `P0033` (Ankara) all use independent clinical cohorts.

## feasibility

Directly aligned with `scope.md` non-goals (*"No segmentation model replacement"*). Within `O1–O3`, our thesis rightly uses **bounding-box object detection** (`YOLO`-family) for discrete multi-class pathology/restoration localization and triage, rather than chasing pixel-level segmentation across whole OPGs. Simultaneously, `P0013`, `P0023`, `P0006`, and `P0032` govern our **`UD04` target-ontology boundary**: exclude continuous morphometric root-length grading (`OIEARR` requires keypoints) and avoid diffuse area-wide `Bone loss` boxes unless bounded cleanly at an anatomical unit, or restrict pathology targets to compact focal lesions/restorations/impactions well-suited to bounding boxes.

## narrower interpretation

Neither paradigm holds universal superiority. For discrete pathology, restoration, and impacted-tooth screening (`O1–O2`), **bounding-box object detection separates crowded instances better (`P0018 > P0035`, `P0007`, `P0022`), avoids `99%`-background semantic distortion (`P0033`), and stays computationally faster than cascaded mask decoders (`P0011`, `P0029`)**. Conversely, pixel masks, oriented boxes (`OBB`), or keypoints are specifically required for sub-mm linear root-length measurement (`P0013`), diffuse bone-loss contours (`P0023`), or tight background suppression on oblique roots (`P0006, P0026`).
