## ReportGap

`G5: image-quality influence` (`OS §3.3`)

## proposed statement

Contrast enhancement (`CLAHE`, histogram equalization, sharpening, or super-resolution) universally improves `2D OPG` object-detection performance, and published OPG benchmarks consistently control image preprocessing, resolution, label fidelity, and dataset splits.

## search coverage

Tested across all `122` unique screened records and `42` extracted Core studies (`P0001–P0042`), specifically auditing preprocessing/enhancement ablations (`P0001, P0002, P0004, P0008, P0010–P0015, P0017–P0019, P0021–P0025, P0028–P0032, P0036, P0038, P0041, P0042`) and split/metric integrity across all `42` full texts.

## supporting and contradicting P IDs

- **Supporting `PaperIDs` (where enhancement/tiling/resolution HELPED or was positively cited):**
  - `P0004` (`Roopitha/C.H et al., 2026`, `Table 4`, p. 8 — **only controlled single-factor YOLO `CLAHE` ablation found in Core**): On `500` cropped OPGs (`400/100` split, `Impacted Maxillary Canines`), `YOLOv5` progressed **`Original (0.701 mAP@0.5)` -> `Cropped (0.774)` -> `CLAHE(clip=2) (0.924)` -> `CLAHE + Sharpening (0.948)`** (`+0.247` total), whereas `Gaussian blur` hurt `Stage-2 ResNet50` (`0.648 -> 0.482`) despite achieving higher `PSNR` (`37.76 dB` vs `28.38 dB`).
  - `P0024` (`Rahman et al., 2024`): Applied `CLAHE` (omitting `clipLimit`/`tileGridSize`) on `232` handheld-photographed Dhaka OPGs (`YOLOv7 mAP50 = 93.54%`), **without** a `Raw vs CLAHE` ablation.
  - `P0013` (`Özden et al., 2026`, `CLAHE clip=(1,4), grid=(8,8)` as aug) and `P0012` (`Wang & Li, 2026`, `HE + bilateral filter`): both **unablated** against raw OPGs.
  - `P0042` (`Panetta et al., 2022`): `CLAHE` won visual enhancement scores (`AME = 24.32, LogAME = 0.3702`), **explicitly warning** (`Table IV` context) that visual enhancement metrics were not evaluated on downstream AI accuracy.
  - `P0028` (`Lutescu et al., 2026`, citing `Rohrer et al. [30]`): Partitioning `1,781` OPGs into **`20` rectangular tiles** boosted `U-Net` restoration `F1` from `0.70` (full OPG) -> `0.95` (`+294%` root-canal fillings).
  - `P0008` (`Oztekin et al., 2026`, `Tables 4 & 8`): **Without any CLAHE**, native `640×640` lesion-preserving tiling raised `mAP@0.50` across all `6` YOLO generations by **`+0.039` to `+0.121`** (best `YOLO12m 0.494 -> 0.615`), while demonstrating background negative tiles are mandatory (`0.3729 [37.5% FP] -> 0.5125` on dense grid).
  - `P0011` (`Table 11`: increasing resolution `640 -> 1600 px` gained `+8.27%` `mAP@50-95`) & `P0015` (`Exp 1–4`: Full `1280×1280` beat `1024×1024`, whereas `75%` central cropping deleted **`5.7%`** of GT boxes and clipped `20.8%`).
- **Contradicting `PaperIDs` (where `CLAHE`/enhancement HURT, FAILED to beat raw OPGs, or was UNNECESSARY, PLUS severe dataset-leakage / quality-control flaws):**
  - **`CLAHE`/Enhancement DEGRADED or FAILED to improve detection/classification:**
    - `P0011` (`Scarano et al., 2026`, `Table 4`, p. 17): Bundled **`Sharpening + CLAHE + Gaussian blur` (`Enh.=Yes`) HURT `YOLOv12-m`**, dropping `mAP@50` from **`0.9194 -> 0.9023` (`-1.71%`)** and **`mAP@50-95` from `0.7794 -> 0.7403` (`-3.91%`)**.
    - `P0029` (`Nayak et al., 2026`, `Tables 17, 26, 30`): Under `8×` augmentation (`Table 30`, p. 36), **Raw `Mask R-CNN` (`IoU = 0.8173, Dice = 0.8993`) outperformed ALL enhanced variants**: fixed `CLAHE clip=3.0` (`0.8104`), `Entropy CLAHE` (`0.8134`), `Entropy CLAHE + ROI-FFT` (`0.8131`), `Full CLAHE+FFT+Canny` (`0.8151`, adding `7.9×` latency penalty!), and `Global FFT` (`0.8005`, `Bg SSIM = 0.522`). Original Deployable Stage-2 enhancement performed significantly **worse** than raw Stage-1 (`0.7974 vs 0.8061, Wilcoxon p < 0.001`); `CLAHE clip > 5.0` amplified background noise.
    - `P0038` (`Gwak et al., 2024`, `716` OPGs): `CLAHE + Intensity + Flip + Rotate` scored **`95.69%`** — lower than `Intensity + Flip` (**`97.50%`**) and `Intensity + Flip + Trapezoid` (**`99.17%`**).
    - `P0041` (`Kaya et al., 2022`): Explicitly rejected `CLAHE` on `4,518` pediatric OPGs, citing `Ha et al. (2021) [19]` showing raw unenhanced OPGs outperformed `CLAHE`.
    - `P0018` (`Küçük et al., 2025`, `Table 2`, p. 11): `GFP-GAN` facial-prior super-resolution had `0.0%` effect on `YOLOv8X` and **degraded `RT-DETR-L` (`-0.5%`) and `RT-DETR-X` (`-1.9%`)**.
    - `P0001` (`YOLO26m mAP@0.5 = 0.87–0.98`) and `P0008` (`YOLO12m` without `CLAHE`) explicitly avoided `CLAHE`/enhancement and achieved SOTA detection performance (`P0002`'s `+1.3%` ViT `CLAHE+ROI` gain is confounded by pre-split augmentation and RGB intraoral data).
  - **Pre-split Augmentation Leakage, Cohort Contamination & Label/Metric Quality Defects:**
    - **Smoking-gun `+30.4%` inflation (`P0011` vs `P0008`):** On the exact same `Do et al. (2024)` dataset and `YOLOv12-m`, `P0011` used all `17,004` pre-augmented copies (`mAP@50 = 0.9194, mAP@50-95 = 0.7794`), whereas `P0008` deduplicated back to `3,924` originals (`mAP@50 = 0.494–0.615, mAP@50-95 = 0.230–0.277`).
    - **Confirmed pre-split augmentation / train-test leakage:** `P0002` (Gingivitis/Hypodontia pre-split aug), `P0006` (`2,405 -> 4,768` before `80:10:10` split, confirmed `p. 10`), `P0021` (`15k -> 20k` teeth before `90/10` tooth split -> `F1 = 0.9941`), `P0025` (`687 -> 1,025` before 4-fold CV), `P0036` (evaluates pipeline on all `500` OPGs contributing training crops; clean `mIoU = 0.484`), `P0040` (`14,000` crop random Stage-2 split across `5,000` OPGs), and suspicious `100%` `Inverted` scores on Mendeley in `P0005`.
    - **Acquisition, label & metric quality flaws:** `P0024` (handheld smartphone re-photography `1176×603` of printed/screen X-rays), `P0031` (`1280px JPEG 85` MLLM downscaling), `P0032` (tight tooth crops truncating PA lesions), `P0016` (merges caries + fillings into one label + implausible `κ = 1.00`), `P0003` (trainee label noise depressed recall), `P0014` (`Precision` vs `COCO AP` conflation + `Table II/III` mismatch), `P0020` (`F1 > max(P,R)`), `P0022` (`P = 1.0` when `R = 0`), `P0025` (`P/R/F1` from 3 different confidence thresholds).

## independence

Independent controlled negative/null results on `CLAHE`/enhancement (`P0011` Rome on `Do 2024`; `P0029` Mangalore on private + `Tufts`; `P0038` Yonsei Korea; `P0041` Istanbul citing `Ha 2021`) outnumber the single positive controlled YOLO ablation (`P0004` Manipal on cropped `IMC`), while split-leakage/metric defects span `>10` independent groups worldwide.

## feasibility

Extremely high (`O2`). Our thesis (`S04 / STEP-010+` & `S07 / Phase 2`) should **NOT treat `CLAHE` as a blind prerequisite**. Instead, `G5` defines three directly testable, high-rigor methodological rules for `O2`:
1. Make `CLAHE` (with explicit `clipLimit` & `tileGridSize`, `Raw` vs `CLAHE`) and input resolution / ROI strategy **empirical, falsifiable ablations** evaluated on accuracy AND end-to-end latency.
2. Enforce **mandatory dataset deduplication + strict patient/OPG-level splitting before ANY augmentation** (`P0008` standard), blocking `17,004`-style leakage.
3. Audit target classes and metric computation with deterministic scripts (`mAP@0.50`, `mAP@0.50:0.95`, fixed confidence threshold, never `F1 > max(P,R)` or `P=1` on `TP=0`).

## narrower interpretation

1. **Broad enhancement superiority refuted:** `CLAHE` and contrast/SR pipelines frequently degrade (`P0011, P0018, P0038`) or fail to beat raw augmented OPGs (`P0029, P0001, P0008, P0041`) while adding runtime cost (`7.9×` in `P0029`).
2. **Surviving real `G5` gap:** Resolution preservation / native tiling (`P0008, P0011, P0015, P0028`) and **rigorous data-integrity hygiene (deduplication, leak-free splitting, acquisition normalization, and deterministic metric evaluation)** are far more consequential than unablated contrast filters — leaving **controlled Raw vs. parameterized `CLAHE` + resolution ablation under verified leak-free splits** as the exact empirically testable gap.
