## ReportGap

`G2: classification lacks spatial localization` (`OS §3.3`)

## proposed statement

Panoramic radiograph (`OPG`) AI studies rely on whole-image or patch classification without spatial bounding-box localization, leaving lesion and structure localization unaddressed in existing literature.

## search coverage

Tested across `180` harvested files (`58` duplicates removed) -> `122` unique screened records (`42` Core full-text: `23` Group A direct OPG box/landmark detectors `P0001, P0003–P0009, P0011–P0025` + `19` Group B contextual studies `P0002, P0010, P0026–P0042`; `55` Reserve [`14` HighPriority, `41` Secondary]; `25` Excluded). Reclassification of `P0002` and `P0010` from Group A to Group B specifically audited title-vs-task localization claims.

## supporting and contradicting P IDs

- **Contradicting `PaperIDs` (prove bounding-box spatial localization IS widely practiced in OPG literature):**
  - All `23` Group A studies (`P0001, P0003–P0009, P0011–P0025`) plus Group B Stage-1/single-stage box detectors (`P0026` `YOLOv11x-OBB`, `P0030` `YOLOv8m/v10m`, `P0032` `RTMDet+Swin-B`, `P0036` `YOLOv5s`, `P0041` `YOLOv4`) output explicit spatial bounding boxes on 2D OPGs. For instance, `P0001` evaluates localization precision/recall at strict `IoU >= 0.80`; `P0008` evaluates `6` YOLO generations on `3,924` deduplicated OPGs with `mAP@0.50:0.95` and `FROC`; `P0015` pairs `32`-FDI localization with `12`-class pathology boxes via `IoU >= 0.25`; `P0027` catalogs `73` dental YOLO papers.
- **Supporting `PaperIDs` (demonstrate where classification/localization confusion and weak localization evaluation remain widespread):**
  - **Whole-image or patch classifiers framed as "detection" without box localization metrics:**
    - `P0002` (`Bhoopalan et al., 2026`): Title claims *"dental disease detection in OPG radiographs"*, yet models `ViT-Base` and `Swin` for `4`-class mutually exclusive image-level classification (`0` bounding-box `IoU` or `mAP` reported; mis-matched against Kaggle intraoral RGB dataset).
    - `P0010` (`Rahimi et al., 2026`): Title claims *"Detection of Pediatric Dental Caries"*, yet trains a `64×64` sliding-window `ResNet-18` patch classifier inside ground-truth `Smile Zone` crops — suffering precision collapse (`Precision = 0.318`, `F1 = 0.464`, `>2` false-positive patches per true caries patch) — alongside non-detector `U-Net` masks.
    - `P0038` (`Gwak et al., 2024`): Proposes weakly supervised `Grad-CAM` Soft-IoU loss on `ResNet50` classification, yet reports **zero quantitative localization metrics (`IoU`, `Dice`, or `mAP`)** on the test set (`3`-class image classification only).
    - `P0017` (`Khurshid et al., 2025`, `Table 3`, p. 14): Ranks a non-localizing `ViT` whole-image classifier (`Acc = 94.15%, F1 = 93.52%`) directly against `Faster R-CNN` (`mAP@0.50 = 82.0%`) and `YOLOv11` (`70.7%`) without noting the task asymmetry in headline ranking.
    - `P0034` (`Bonfanti-Gris et al., 2025`): Pools an image classifier (`Aljabri [32]`) into an impacted-tooth detection meta-analysis.
    - `P0031` (`Peng et al., 2026`): Proves frontier zero-shot MLLMs fail at whole-OPG spatial tooth–finding localization (`Task 2 Pair F1 = 0.340–0.485`; `Caries <= 0.075`, `Periapical lesion <= 0.023`, `Bone loss = 0.000`), and naive geometric FDI assignment failed on `85.2%` of lesion boxes.
  - **Omission of strict localization quality (`mAP@0.5:0.95`) and background false positives:**
    - `P0027` (`Hartman et al., 2026`, `Table 2`): Systematic review of `73` YOLO studies finds **`84.9%` (`62/73`) omit `mAP@0.5:0.95`** (only `5` OPG studies report it) and `38.4%` (`28/73`) even omit `mAP@0.5`.
    - `P0019` (`Peker & Kurtoglu, 2025`, `Table 1`, p. 9): Proves `mAP@0.50` hides catastrophic box-alignment failure (`Tooth 71` shows `mAP@0.50 = 0.995` while `mAP@0.50:0.95 = 0.0995`).
    - `P0021` (`Singh et al., 2025`, `Tables 2–3`, p. 10): Claims object detection while reporting only a closed-set `6×6` confusion matrix (`F1 = 0.9941`, `0` background `FP/FN`) and omitting `mAP@0.5`, `mAP@0.5:0.95`, and `IoU` matching rules.
    - `P0003` (`Biçengil et al., 2026`): Pooled surface classification (`F1 = 0.797`) overstates instance-level box localization (`F1 = 0.473`).
    - `P0032` (`van Nistelrooij et al., 2026`): Decoupled tight tooth-box cropping truncates periapical lesions extending beyond the root apex (`Sens = 0.538` vs dentists `0.854`), while `P0026` evaluates Stage-2 classification on GT test boxes only, hiding the `9.4%` Stage-1 miss rate (`P0029` similarly shows Oracle GT-ROI `IoU = 0.9507` collapses to `0.7974` under deployable Stage-1 localization, `r = 0.9925`).
    - `P0036` (`Pornprasertsuk-Damrongsri et al., 2025`): Excludes all `519` false positives from weighted `κ` (`0.943`) and substitutes tooth-level binary classification `IoU/DSC` (`0.75 / 0.85`) for pixel localization (`clean mIoU = 0.484`).

## independence

Independent audit evidence converges across separate geographic groups: Turkey (`P0003, P0019`), Taiwan/China (`P0009, P0012, P0031`), Iran (`P0010, P0014, P0025`), India (`P0021`), Indonesia systematic review (`P0027`), Spain systematic review (`P0034`), Europe (`P0032`), Korea (`P0038`), Thailand (`P0036`), and UAE (`P0026`). Only `P0017`/`P0005`/`P0006` share a lead author (`Khurshid`), and `P0022`/`P0034` share `Bonfanti-Gris`.

## feasibility

High (`O1–O2`). Our thesis can rigorously enforce true spatial object-detection benchmarking (`IoU`-matched `Precision, Recall, F1`, `mAP@0.50`, **`mAP@0.50:0.95`**, explicit confidence/NMS operating thresholds, background `FP/FN` confusion accounting, and qualitative bbox overlay verification) rather than whole-image/patch classification or closed-set confusion matrices, plus compare unified single-stage detection against multi-stage crop truncation risks.

## narrower interpretation

The broad claim that OPG AI lacks spatial box localization is **refuted** by all `23` Group A detectors plus `5` Group B box detectors (`P0026, P0030, P0032, P0036, P0041`). The **narrowed and verified gap** is that:
1. Literature frequently **conflates whole-image, patch, Grad-CAM, or tooth-crop classification with object detection** (`P0002, P0010, P0017, P0021, P0034, P0036, P0038`), and
2. True localization strictness (**`mAP@0.50:0.95`**, omitted in `84.9%` of YOLO dental papers per `P0027`, plus full background `FP/FN` & end-to-end propagation accounting) remains severely under-reported, masking poor box tightening (`P0019 Tooth 71`) and ROI truncation (`P0032, P0026, P0029`).
