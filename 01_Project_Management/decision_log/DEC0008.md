## question

What exact deterministic algorithm, random seeds, rounding/group-preservation rules, per-class evaluation support minimums, and final-test custody rules govern `SPLIT0001–SPLIT0006` (`G07`)?

## report facts and locators

- Deleted report facts preserved in `OS §1.3` (`RF10`) and `§4.4`: Figure 1 proposed a **`70/15/15` (`train / validation / test`)** split ratio without specifying the stratification algorithm, seed, or group-rounding rule (`UD08`).

## planning options and evidence (`STEP-020` Split Execution)

1. **Option 1 (Simple image-level random 70/15/15 split):**
   - *Strictly Rejected (`OS §4.4`, `STEP-020` Common Mistakes):* Breaks connected duplicate/re-shoot groups across partitions and risks leaving ultra-rare classes (`DS0001 Apical surgery N=84`, `DS0003 Fractured Teeth N=14`) with `0` validation or test instances.
2. **Option 2 (Adopted — Sechidis Iterative Multi-Label Group-Stratified `70/15/15` Allocation + Sealed `external_test` on `DS0001`):**
