## options considered (audit trail)

1. **Option 1 (`Version 5`, superseded earlier):** `2` datasets (`CAND0001` + `CAND0003`, `~1.5 GiB`) only.
2. **Option 2:** `3` datasets (`CAND0001` + `CAND0002` + `CAND0003`, `~11.8 GiB`).
3. **Option 3 (Adopted `Version 6` — 6-Dataset Corpus Ceiling):** Authorize `STEP-012` off-Git intake of all **6 live downloadable open-licensed `2D OPG` datasets (`CAND0001, CAND0002, CAND0003, CAND0004, CAND0005, CAND0007`; `~7,788–7,790` total OPGs)** while enforcing **track isolation** (`OS §6.2 #4`, `§7.2`: never blindly merge incompatible partial-annotation, OBB, single-condition `PAI`, or pediatric cohorts into one pooled label matrix without explicit `UD04/UD05` alignment and `G05/G06` QA).

## tradeoffs & non-merge constraints

- Expanding from `2` to `6` cohorts provides multi-country coverage (`Romania, Türkiye, Bangladesh, Vietnam, UAE, Iran`) and dedicated sub-study scales (`PAI 3–5` tiling on `CAND0004`, `OBB` vs `AABB` comparison on `CAND0005`, pediatric mixed-dentition caries test on `CAND0007`), at the cost of `~15–20 GiB` total download bandwidth/storage, Mendeley archive extraction (`CAND0003/0004`), `.rar` handling (`CAND0007`), strict deduplication (`CAND0003/0004`), and per-dataset track separation (`UD04–UD08`).
- **ShareAlike (`CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0`) isolation:** Any weights/derivatives trained specifically on `CAND0002 DENTEX` keep non-commercial attribution & ShareAlike tracking separate from pure `CC BY 4.0` tracks (`CAND0001`, `CAND0007`) until `in-archive LICENSE` is confirmed (`STEP-012/013`).
