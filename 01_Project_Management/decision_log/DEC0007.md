## UD reference

`UD07` — *"Patient/study/source grouping availability, duplicates, supplied split reliability"* (`01_Project_Management/decisions.csv` row `DEC0007`, required for **`G07 / STEP-020`**).

## question

Are upstream supplied dataset splits reliable, how are exact, re-encoded, and near-duplicate radiographs grouped across `DS0001–DS0006`, and what bounded independence claim is approved where upstream public repositories omitted patient-level identifiers?

## report facts and locators

- Deleted report facts preserved in `OS §1.5 #7` and `§4.4`: The report did not verify patient grouping metadata or duplicate leakage in public OPG datasets (`UD07`).

## planning options and evidence (`STEP-013` & `STEP-020` Forensic Findings)

1. **Supplied Benchmark Split Audit (`OS §4.4`):**
   - **`DS0001` (`UMFIH`):** Official Zenodo `train`/`valid` folders contain **`42` byte-identical `train <-> valid` copies (`27.5%` validation leakage)** plus `18` re-encoded identical pairs ($\text{corr} \ge 0.99$) and `141` intra-train duplicate groups. **Official internal split is contaminated and unusable.** However, `external_validation` (`180` OPGs -> `178` unique canonical + `2` internal duplicates) has **`0` overlap** with internal splits and is clean as a sealed `external_test` holdout.
   - **`DS0002` (`DENTEX`):** Official `validation_data.zip` (`50` PNGs) lacks public ground-truth labels; the `705` labeled training PNGs contain **`0` exact or near-duplicates** ($\max \text{corr} < 0.90$).
   - **`DS0003` (`Rahman Dhaka`):** Official `Augmented Dataset` (`604` files) and `Classification` (`517` files) were quarantined at `STEP-012`; inside `Original Dataset` (`230` labeled OPGs), `DS0003_IMG_001153 <-> DS0003_IMG_001321` (`dHash H=1, corr=0.8625`) are two handheld photos of the same film (`229` unique groups).
   - **`DS0004` (`Do 2024 Hanoi`):** After quarantining `13,071` `Augmentation JPG Images`, `Original JPG Images` (`3,924` files) still contained **`59` extra exact SHA-256 duplicates + `2` re-encoded identical copies ($\text{corr} \ge 0.99$) + `1` near-duplicate pair ($\text{corr} = 0.9533$)** (`3,863` canonical OPGs across `3,862` unique groups).
   - **`DS0005` (`AU-OPG`):** Official HuggingFace `train`/`val` CSVs contain **`6` re-encoded identical pairs ($\text{corr} \ge 0.99$) leaking directly across `train <-> val` (`4.4%` validation contamination)** plus `2` near-duplicate pairs ($0.95 \le \text{corr} < 0.99$) (`895` canonical OPGs across `893` unique groups).
   - **`DS0006` (`MD-OPG`):** `170` pediatric patients (`1` OPG/patient stated in `P0010 p. 9`). `STEP-020` border-cropped interior pixel correlation check (after removing the `66.17%` black letterbox border) confirmed **`0` pairs $\ge 0.95$** ($\max \text{corr} = 0.9149$), proving `170` independent patient groups.

### Options Considered
1. **Option 1 (Reuse supplied `DS0001` and `DS0005` official train/val/test splits):** *Strictly Rejected (`OS §4.4`):* `27.5%` validation leakage in `DS0001` and `4.4%` in `DS0005` invalidate scientific evaluation.
2. **Option 2 (Keep redundant exact/re-encoded duplicate copies inside training and evaluation denominators):** *Rejected:* Double-weights duplicated radiographs and inflates test metrics.
3. **Option 3 (Adopted — Connected-Component Union-Find Grouping + Canonical Deduplication + Honest Radiograph-Cluster Independence Claim):**
   - Discard contaminated official internal splits (`DS0001`, `DS0005`) while keeping `DS0001 external_validation` sealed as `external_test`.
   - Group all exact SHA-256, re-encoded ($\text{corr} \ge 0.99$), and near-duplicate / re-shoot ($0.95 \le \text{corr} < 0.99$ + `DS0003_IMG_001153/1321`) pairs via connected components (`7,451` unique groups across `7,738` labeled OPGs).
   - Designate `1` `active_canonical` image per exact/re-encoded cluster (`7,457` canonical OPGs total) and tag the `281` redundant byte/re-encoded copies (`214` in `DS0001`, `61` in `DS0004`, `6` in `DS0005`) as `deduplicated_duplicate_copy` (inheriting their group's partition but excluded from training/evaluation denominators).
   - Explicitly state in `SPLIT0001–0006/report.md` and `G07` that because upstream repositories (`DS0001–DS0004`) omitted patient IDs, independence is certified at the **connected duplicate/study-cluster level** (and multi-center external level on `DS0001 external_test` and cross-dataset `HC01–HC05`), not guaranteed against unlinked separate-year longitudinal visits.

## recommendation and rejected alternatives

Adopt **Option 3**.
