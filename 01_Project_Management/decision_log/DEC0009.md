## UD reference

`UD09` — *"Preprocessing algorithms/parameters, annotation reference image, augmentation limits"* (`01_Project_Management/decisions.csv` row `DEC0009`, designed at **`STEP-019`** and verified on materialized releases at **`G08 / STEP-021`**).

## question

Which preprocessing operations, parameter configurations, coordinate-preserving resize/autocrop rules, privacy sanitizations, and training-only augmentation constraints are approved for `STEP-019`–`021` (`G08`) and `RQ2` controlled experiments?

## report facts and locators

- Deleted report facts preserved in `OS §1.3` (`RF08`) and `§4.3`: Figure 1 listed *contrast enhancement, noise reduction, resizing, normalization, and filtering* as candidate operations without parameters, ordering, or proof of benefit (`UD09`).

## planning options and evidence (`STEP-013` Quality Audit & `GAP0005` Synthesis)

1. **Option 1 (Apply all 5 diagram operations — including arbitrary Gaussian/median blur and fixed CLAHE — as mandatory defaults on every image):**
   - *Strictly Rejected (`OS §4.3`, `STEP-019` Common Mistakes):* Low-pass denoising blurs subtle periapical bone rarefaction (`PAI 3`) and interproximal caries edges, and applying `CLAHE` universally prevents testing `RQ2` (`GAP0005`: `P0004` positive vs `P0011, P0029, P0038, P0041` null/negative findings).
2. **Option 2 (Naive square resize to `640 × 640` without aspect-ratio preservation or `DS0006` black-border autocrop):**
   - *Rejected:* Panoramic radiographs have aspect ratios of `1.73–2.20` (`DS0001–DS0005`), so anisotropic squashing distorts root/crown morphology; and `DS0006` (`MD-OPG`) has `66.17%` median solid-black letterbox framing, which shrinks pediatric teeth to tiny pixel areas if not border-cropped first.
3. **Option 3 (Adopted — Minimal Aspect-Preserving Baseline `PP0001` + Controlled `RQ2` Factors `PP0002–PP0004` + Invertible `TransformLedger`):**
   - **`PP0001` (Baseline — `640 × 640` Raw Letterbox):** Strip EXIF/XMP, standardize `RGBA/L -> 3-ch RGB`, autocrop `DS0006` outer solid-black margins (`black_threshold=8, margin_px=8`, expanded to guarantee `0` clipped boxes), aspect-preserving letterbox resize (`pad_value=114`), scale to `[0, 1]`, zero contrast or blur filters.
   - **`PP0002` (`RQ2` Factor 1 — `CLAHE clip=2.0, grid=8×8 @ 640×640`):** Deterministic Luma `CLAHE` with identical geometry to `PP0001`.
   - **`PP0003` (`RQ2` Factor 2 — `CLAHE clip=3.0, grid=16×16 @ 640×640`):** Finer-grid local `CLAHE` ablation.
   - **`PP0004` (`RQ2` Factor 3 — `1280 × 1280` High-Res Raw Letterbox):** Spatial-scale ablation for small radiolucent lesions (`DS0001`, `DS0004` `PAI 3–5`, `DS0006`).
   - **Geometry & Augmentation Contract:** All transforms emit an invertible `TransformLedger` (`max round-trip error < 0.02 px`, `0` dropped boxes); stochastic augmentation (`rotation ±5°`, `scale [0.9, 1.1]`, `translation ±5%`, `brightness/contrast ±10%`; vertical/horizontal flips prohibited) is restricted strictly to `train` after `STEP-020 / G07`.

## recommendation and rejected alternatives

Adopt **Option 3** (`PP0001–PP0004` in `05_Preprocessing/specs/pipeline.md` and `src/opg/preprocessing.py`).
