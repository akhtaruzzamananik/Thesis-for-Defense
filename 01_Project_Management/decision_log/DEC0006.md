## question

Because all `6` approved cohorts (`DS0001–DS0006`) are secondary public datasets with existing specialist annotations (`44,251` raw annotations across `7,789` active OPGs), what reviewer roles, stratified pilot and batch QA sample sizes, agreement/integrity metrics, prospective acceptance thresholds, and adjudication rules govern `STEP-016`–`STEP-018` (`G06`)?

## planning options and evidence

1. **Option 1 (Accept all secondary public annotations blindly without QA or pilot audit):**
   - *Strictly Rejected (`OS §6.4`, `STEP-018` Common Mistakes):* Violates `G05`/`G06` and misses the `4` zero-area boxes, `DS0001` `labels (1).txt` corruption, `DS0003` missing `.txt`, and `OBB`/polygon conversion edge cases.
2. **Option 2 (Re-draw all 44,251 bounding boxes manually by computer-science students):**
   - *Strictly Rejected (`OS §6.1`, `§6.4`):* Unqualified amateur relabeling would corrupt specialist dental ground truth.
3. **Option 3 (Adopted — Prospective Two-Tier Secondary-Label Verification, Stratified Visual Overlay Audit & Conservative Adjudication):**
   - **Roles:**
     - *Primary QA Auditor (`Reviewer 1`):* **Mahajub Al Shabah** (`Annotation QA Lead`).
     - *Independent Second Auditor (`Reviewer 2`):* **Akhtaruzzaman Anik** (`Data Steward`).
     - *Clinical / Methodological Adjudicator:* **Supervisor (`Ms Faiza Feroz`) + Dental Review Consultation** (preserving original specialist dental labels unless structurally corrupt/degenerate).
   - **Prospective Pilot Plan (`STEP-016`):**
     - Stratified sample of **`60` active OPGs (`10` per dataset across `DS0001–DS0006`, seed `20261119`)** covering high-density, rare-class, and low-sharpness/edge-clipped cases.
     - Independently audited by Reviewer 1 and Reviewer 2 using `match_boxes_greedy` and visual box-overlay sheets.
     - **Prospective Thresholds:** `100.0%` schema/round-trip validity (`< 0.01 px` error), **$\ge 90.0\%$ inter-reviewer audit disposition agreement** ($\kappa \ge 0.80$), **mean matched box $\text{IoU} \ge 0.85$** (`1.00` for deterministic export), and **$< 5.0\%$ gross source-label defect rate**.
   - **Production Batch Plan (`STEP-017`–`018` / `G06`):**
     - `6` controlled batches (`ANNB0001–ANNB0006` covering all `7,789` active OPGs): **100% automated structural/geometry/duplicate check** across all `44,251` annotations + **stratified `240`-OPG visual overlay audit (`40` per dataset)** under the same prospective thresholds before freezing `ANN0001–ANN0006`.
