### Options Evaluated
1. **Option 1 (Single-dataset baseline only):** Use `DS0001` (`UMFIH`) alone. *Rejected:* Misses local Bangladeshi clinical shift (`DS0003`), `DENTEX` benchmark comparison (`DS0002`), large-scale `PAI 3–5` tiling (`DS0004`), `OBB->AABB` conversion (`DS0005`), and pediatric caries (`DS0006`) requested by team & supervisor (`DEC0003 v6`).
2. **Option 2 (Naive mega-pool of all 6 datasets into one training set):** *Strictly Rejected (`OS §6.2 #4`, `§7.2`):* Scientifically invalid due to conflicting partial-annotation rules (`DS0003` labels healthy teeth while `DS0001/0002/0004/0005/0006` leave healthy teeth as background; `DS0004` labels only `PAI 3–5`).
3. **Option 3 (Adopted — 6-Dataset Non-Merged Multi-Track Architecture):** Approve all `6` inspected datasets (`DS0001–DS0006`) on their dedicated tracks (`Tracks A–F` above) with harmonized cross-cohort evaluation bridges (`HC01–HC05`) only where class definitions and background rules are explicitly compatible (`UD04–UD05`).

## recommendation and rejected alternatives

Adopt **Option 3** (`Tracks A–F` non-merged portfolio across `DS0001–DS0006`), enforcing all `STEP-013` quarantine, duplicate-grouping (`UD07`), and privacy-stripping (`UD09/UD23`) rules. Reject Option 1 (narrow coverage) and Option 2 (silent partial-label contamination).
